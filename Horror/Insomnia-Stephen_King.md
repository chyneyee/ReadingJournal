# Insomnia - Stephen King

## Summary
The story is set in the fictional town of Derry, Maine. When Ralph is doing his daily walk, he witnesses a car accident involving his neighbour, Ed, who leaves the airport service gate in his car. Ralph usually sees Ed as a kind and warm man, but at the accident scene, Ed looks like a different person and is filled with rage. Ralph tries to calm Ed down after he accuses the other driver of having dead babies in the truck that carries fertiliser. Ed goes back to normal after Ralph's intervention. After that day, Ralph forgets about the incident because he's too busy focusing on taking care of his sick wife.

<br>

### Part I - Little Bald Doctors
Ralph begins to suffer insomnia for the first time in his life a month after his wife's death. It starts with waking up earlier than usual. Ralph spends his time on various activities during the day to exhaust himself and also tries different remedies suggested by his friends to improve his sleep. He hesitates to call his family doctor because he believes the doctor didn't diagnose his wife, Carolyn, early enough for her brain tumour.

When Ralph is in the convenience store, he sees Helen Deepneau, Ed's wife, with a bloodied face and bruises walking on the street, attracting the attention of the public. Helen is in bad condition and confused. Ralph brings her inside the convenience store with the help of Sue and Bill. Helen doesn't want Ralph to call the police. Ralph hesitates a moment but makes the call in the end. Helen tells them that she got beaten up by her husband, Ed, after Ed knew that she had signed a petition asking Susan Day to come to Derry to speak on behalf of WomanCare. Ralph goes to Helen's house to confront Ed. When Ralph mentions the petition, Ed turns into an angry person and babbles about dying foetuses, the Crimson King, and some biblical stories. The police take Ed away and charge him with domestic assault. Helen stays in a women's shelter while Ed lives in a cottage near his working lab. Helen is grateful for Ralph's help and writes a letter to him, telling him that she has decided to divorce her husband. 

Meanwhile, Ralph's insomnia never gets better, and he has tried various remedies, from honeycomb to camomile tea, but nothing works during that half year. Ralph decides to try sleeping pills as the last resort before he sees Dr Litchfield. At the pharmacy, he meets Joe Wyzer, the pharmacist, who has extensive knowledge about insomnia, and discusses it with him. Joe later reveals that he has been suffering insomnia since he was thirteen. He recommends an acupuncturist and hypnotist to Ralph. After Ralph leaves the pharmacy, he realises that he begins to see colourful auras surrounding people, and balloon strings appear from every person's head. He also sees diminutive white-coated beings, and he calls them "little bald doctors" based on their appearance.  Meanwhile, Ed has been released on bail and appears on TV after getting involved with the anti-abortion protest. Ralph tells the police about the airport incident; Ed threatens Ralph to mind his own business or else the forces and entities in Derry will go after him.

<br>

### Part II - The Secret City
Lois tells Ralph that she is also experiencing insomnia, and the pattern is similar to Ralph's. She is extremely upset with Dr Litchfield, who reported her health condition to her son. Her son and daughter-in-law come all the way to Derry to convince Lois to move to the retirement home. Lois reveals to Ralph that she sees something strange appearing on people. Ralph tells her that she is actually seeing auras like him. He asks Lois whether she sees any bald men in Derry, but she says she doesn't see any of those.

Ralph realises that Doc #1 and Doc #2 were clean, invested with auras, carried scissors, and seemed sane. Doc #3 was dirty, had no auras, carried a scalpel, and seemed like a crazy man. Ralph and Lois go to the hospital to look for Doc #1 and Doc #2 because they want to know who they are and who is Doc #3. Ralph wonders whether the first two bald men are similar to the Greek mythology, Three Sisters, but he believes Doc #3 is a rogue. When Ralph and Lois find the two little bald doctors, surprisingly, Doc#2 says that they've been waiting for them. 

Doc #1 introduces himself as Clotho and Doc #2 as Lachesis. Clotho reveals that Doc #3 is Atropos, and Atropos is an agent of the Random. Lachesis and Clotho are agents of Death and also agents of Purpose. Clotho and Lachesis are responsible for freeing people from life when it is "their time" to pass away. A third bald doctor, Atropos, does want to interfere with how the operation works and has taken some lives prematurely, which causes serious imbalance between the Random and the Purpose. Lachesis and Clotho are unable to intervene directly, and only short-timers like Ralph and Lois can oppose the will of Atropos. They give Ralph and Lois insomnia to help them perceive, gain and even access other levels of reality so they can defeat Atropos. Ed is neither Random nor of the Purpose and Atropos has cut his cord. Crimson King, the ultimate ruler of Random, sends Atropos to manipulate Ed as part of a plan to upset the entire order of the universe. Lois and Ralph have to stop Susan Day from giving a speech because Ed plans a murder-suicide mission to assassinate Susan and also kill everyone who attends the speech.

### Ending
<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

- Ed's allies lead his people to the women's shelter. The women's shelter is on fire, and Gretchen Tillbury is killed. Ralph and Lois save the residents. 
- Lois and Ralph seek for Atropos. Ralph overcomes the malicious being and uses an unusual method to force Atropos to make a promise that he will not interfere with Ralph and Lois. Once Atropos made the promise, he torments Ralph with a vision of a car accident in the near future that will take the life of Helen's young daughter Natalie. Her death will be retaliation for Atropos not being able to interfere with Ralph.
- Ralph calls Lachesis and Clotho to tell them the real reason for asking him and Lois to stop the mass killing. Lachesis reveals that they need to stop the killing to protect a boy that Ralph saw at the women's shelter. The boy must not die before his scheduled time. If the child does, the Tower of all existence will fall. Ralph promises them that he will give a try to stop the killing by trading his life with Natalie, Helen's daughter.
- The Crimson King's main target is Patrick Danville, a young boy prophesied to one day play an instrumental role in preserving the Dark Tower and aiding in the defeat of the Crimson King.
- Ed takes off in his plane, and Ralph fights him on board. The Crimson King manifests to prevent him from interrupting Ed's mission, but Ralph succeeds in causing the plane to crash some distance away from the centre, surviving by shifting himself to a higher plane of reality before impact. 
- Returning to reality, Ralph and Lois fall in love and get married, gradually forgetting their adventures with the little bald doctors.
- In an epilogue taking place some years later, Ralph again starts experiencing insomnia. He once again sees auras and eventually remembers the promise he made to exchange his life for Natalie's. Ralph arrives in time to see the car from his vision appear and steers towards Natalie. Ralph pushes her to safety, losing his life in the process. He dies peacefully with Lois at his side as Clotho and Lachesis watch over him.

## Personal Thoughts
Surprisingly, I quite enjoyed reading the story, although I'm not fully certain that I understand it due to the complexity as it progresses. There is something charming about the story, and I couldn't figure out whether it comes from the plot or the characters. Although this story has a connection to the Dark Tower series and I'm not a fan of it, I still find it intriguing, and it doesn't require the reader to read the series before reading this book. The connection is only mentioned when the story almost reaches the ending.
