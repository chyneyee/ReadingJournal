# Run with the Wind - Shion Miura, Yui Kajita (Translator)

## Summary
Haiji Kiyose comes out from the bathhouse and encounters an incident of a convenience store worker chasing after a shoplifter on the street. Haiji is impressed with the speed and style of the runner and chases after the shoplifter with a bike. When the runner finally stops, Haiji asks the shoplifter whether he likes running. The young man that Haiji pursued after is Kakeru Kurahara. He stole a bag of sweet pastry because he's too hungry to do anything else. Haiji introduces himself to Kakeru as a fourth-year student at Kansai University. Kakeru says that he'll be starting at the same university in April. Haiji wants to know why Kakeru steals, and Kakeru briefly explains that he used up all the rental money given by his parents on mahjong games and became homeless. Haiji offers to introduce Kakeru to the landlord of the apartment he's living in since there's one room available in Chikusei-so. The given offer is too good to be true, but Kakeru has no choice but follows Haiji home.

The landlord of Chikusei-so is Gen'ichiro Tazaki. He lives in a house besides Chikusei-so with a dog called Nira. Chikusei-so consists of the following residents: 

<b>First Floor</b>
<ul>
<li>Room 101 - Haiji. Fourth-year in the Faculty of Letters.</li>
<li>Room 102 - Yukihiko Iwakura. Fourth-year in the Law Faculty.</li>
<li>Room 103 - Kakeru. First year in sociology.</li>
<li>Room 104 - Akihiro Hirata or Nico-senpai. Third-year in science and engineering in that spring. Heavy smoker.</li> 
</ul>

<b>Second Floor</b>
<ul>
<li>Room 201 - The twins, Taro Jo and Jiro Jo. Taro is known as Jota and Jiro as Joji. First-year students.</li>
<li>Room 202 - Yohei Sakaguchi. Fourth year in sociology. Everyone called him King, as in Quiz King, because he loves to watch quiz shows.</li>
<li>Room 203 - Musa Kamala. Exchange student. Second-year in science and engineering.</li>
<li>Room 204 - Akane Kashiwazaki, 2nd year in the Faculty of Letters. Nickname is Prince.</li>
<li>Room 205 - Takashi Sugiyama, 3rd year in business. Nickname is Shindo.</li>
</ul>

Later, the story reveals that Kakeru is a promising runner and received numerous offers from various universities due to his talent. Unfortunately, he got kicked off the track team for engaging in violence. He feels better for not being a representative because he is free from the institutional expectations and ambitions.

The Chikusei-so residents gather together for Kakeru's welcoming party. Haiji uses the opportunity to announce that he plans to have a team of ten people to participate in the biggest annual relay marathon, Hakone Ekiden. Without the residents' knowledge, once they moved into Aotake, they were entered into the track team of the university and automatically registered in the Inter-University Athletic Union of Kanto. Their head coach is their landlord, Gen'ichiro Tazaki. Some of the residents agree to participate due to instant excitement, but some are being forced to do so. Kakeru foresees that the plan is doomed to fail because most of the Chikusei-so residents are not athletic, and Hakone Ekiden is one of the most challenging marathons. The Hakone Ekiden consists of ten sections, and the run splits between 10 people. Each leg is more than 20 km. The preliminary race is a 20 km race, and the entire team's time is obtained by adding up each member's record. The condition for the preliminary is to hold an official record of running 5000 m in under 17 minutes or 10000 m in under 35 minutes.

Although the majority of the Chikusei-so residents are not runners like Kakeru, Kakeru is impressed that all of them have strong determination to work towards the ambitious goal. They never give up after experiencing tremendous body pain from their daily practice set by Haiji's customised schedule. Shindo and Musa wear their PR shirts when they practise running to gain supporters to sponsor the team's running clothes.

As the big day is coming nearer, Kakeru is getting to know more about what kind of runner he wants to be. The entire team is also getting more confident and starts to believe that competing in Hakone is not a mirage. They were a group of people with different interests and backgrounds. Running connects them and brings them rush and joy. It's even made every one of them know themselves more than before.

<br>

## Perrsonal Thoughts
I love every bit of the story. It captures the scenes and every feeling very well. The story is well-written and brilliantly translated, which easily brings the reader to dive into the minds of every resident of the Chikusei-so to understand their struggles, emotions, and the strong bond among them.
