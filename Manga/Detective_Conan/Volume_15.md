# Detective Conan Volume 15 - The Frozen Teacher

## Summary

### Case 039 - Ski Lodge Case
#### Gadget Introduced - [Earring Cellphone](https://www.detectiveconanworld.com/wiki/Earring_Cellphone).

<br>

#### File 141 - Gone!? / Missing?! 
Everyone assumes Sugiyama froze to death outside, but Conan deduces that he was murdered. There are rope marks on his neck, and his stiff body indicates that he probably died more than nine hours ago. His hand is written with the words "KO". Kohei suddenly screams, runs away and says that he has nothing to do with it.  Ran wishes Shinichi were there, and Akiko remembers Shinichi as a smart kid. Atsushi says that he plans to do a feature story about Shinichi but hears a rumour about his disappearance after getting involved in the wrong case. Ran wants to tell him that Shinichi is alive, but Conan stops her. Atsushi tells them to keep an eye on Kohei because his instinct tells him that Kohei probably is the next target. 

Agasa tells Conan that three years ago, a girl student from Haido Elementary School was found hanging in the volleyball locker room. The cause of suicide was probably the stress of the entrance exam, and many people blamed the homeroom teacher. The student's name was Minako Mochizuki. Coincidentally, during the call, Ran sees Conan and wonders what he's doing. She takes the phone and hears Agasa calling Shinichi. Ran asks whether Shinichi is with him, and Agasa says that he just went into the bathroom. Suddenly, a scream comes from upstairs. Conan runs upstairs, asking Ran to wait there, and Atsushi appears nearby. Kohei is found lying upside down from the bed with the rope marks on his neck. The window in the room is closed. Conan tries to find the rope used to strangle Kohei but to no avail. He believes one of the three has the rope.

<br>

#### File 142 - The Disappearing Rope / The Missing Murder Weapon
The staircase is the only way down to the first floor. Conan was on the first floor and didn't see anyone flee. He went through the rooms and everyone's belongings but couldn't find the rope. Akiko suggests Atsushi do a body search on Ryuichi, and Ran searches the women. No one has the rope.

At the outside of the lodge, Conan finds a long piece of kite string and a broken rubber band on the end. Conan somehow figures out the tricks of the attack and knows who the attacker is. When he's thinking about how to reveal the killer, Ran finds him, and he drops the earring-shaped cellphone. He remembers that he can transmit his voice from the bow tie to the cell phone. He has no choice and makes a call to Ran when she's picking up the earring-shaped cellphone. Conan uses Shinichi's voice and explains everything through the phone. He asks Ran to tell the truth based on his explanation. Ran wears the earring and repeats what Shinichi says to her.

<br>

#### File 143 - Truth From Tears / The Truth, Through Tears

<details>
  <summary>Spoiler</summary>
  
  With Shinichi talking to Ran through the earring cellphone, Ran explains how the kite string with a rubber band at its end was used to tie Sugiyama in front of the door. The snow, wind, and cold will deteriorate the rubber band, and when it snaps, the body will fall into the doorbell and make it ring. 

  Ran hesitates but has to reveal that the killer is Akiko. She explains that Akiko braided her hair to act as a rope and strangled Kohei with it. Ran tells them that Akiko is wearing a wig, and Conan found a single strand of human hair from the wig on Kohei's finger. Then, Sonoko says that she saw Akiko was lying on the floor when she was attacked. Ran explains that the one who was lying on the floor was actually Sugiyama. Akiko dressed Sugiyama to look like her and hid the body under the bed. She pretended to lie on the floor before Ran came in. Akiko even called Kohei on his cell phone, telling him that she knew who the killer was and that she went to his room to kill him. 

Akiko admits that she's wearing a wig to hide her haircut, which is too short, but the wig proves nothing. Ran insists Akiko is the killer because there is something else on the wig. Kohei's neck was deeply scratched up where he had clawed at the rope. Ran believes some of the blood appears on the wig. Akiko in the end admits that she kills to avenge the death of Minako. She reveals that exactly three years ago, Minako went to her and told her that there were two teachers who were influencing the outcome of the entrance exams. Minako named Sugiyama as one of the teachers, but she kept another teacher secret because she admired him a lot. The next day, Minako hanged herself, and it was treated as suicide. Akiko believes that Sugiyama and another teacher killed her to shut her up. Akiko organised the trip to gather the teachers she knew Minako was a fan of. She used her lipstick to spell out Minako's name on all of the victims' hands to bait the other guilty teacher. Kohei's panic reaction revealed his involvement. Akiko couldn't forgive them for betraying a student who looked up to them and even killing her.

Ran leaves the room and sobs heavily. She sees Conan standing outside the hall, ready to "tell" her that Shinichi is gone; before he can do so, she falls to her knees and hugs him tearfully. After two hours, the blizzard ended and the police arrived. Conan feels he has successfully solved the case but fails in keeping Ran's tears from spilling out.
  
</details>
