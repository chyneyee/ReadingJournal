### Case 045 - Famous Potter Murder Case
#### File 161 - The Unmoving Evidence / Immovable Proof
Masuko Tsuchiya, Kikuemon's daughter-in-law, was found hanging herself in the storage room. They were partying until late last night. Yoshihiko Arita, Kikuemon's apprentice, carried Masuko to her room the night before when she fell asleep. Kaoru Ooya, another Kikuemon's apprentice, went to wake her up that morning, but she wasn't in her bed.

There is an open gash on the victim's calf. The blood is splattered everywhere. Kogoro assumes that she probably fell twice after failing to kill herself the first time. The cut on her leg was caused by a shard of the vase. She probably tried to commit suicide for the second time, but Ran claims that she only heard a vase breaking once. Kikuemon thinks Masuko committed suicide because of the regret of breaking the Fusuimaru vase the day before. Conan hinted that Masuko was carried to the room by someone because her shoes were not in the room. He also finds that Masuko's feet are clean. If she went to the room barefoot, her feet should be dirty. 

In the living room, Conan reflects on the marble he found near the new vase that was broken on the previous day. He accidentally drops the marble, leading him to notice a lipstick mark on a seat. He runs back to the storage room and discovers irregular blood spatter on the floor and more on the shelf. He climbs to the top of the shelf and finds a potter with holes in it. Conan narrows the suspects down to two, and he now knows who the murderer is.

<br>

#### File 162 - Killed by a Sound!? / Killing with Sound?
Conan puts Kogoro to sleep to reveal the truth and the killer. Conan hints at the various blood patterns on the floor, leading Megure to check the different shapes of the blood drops. Megure explains that the higher the source of the blood, the larger the circle and the longer the splatter lines. The blood pattern indicates that Masuko was on top of the shelves before her death. Conan deduces that the culprit carried Masuko to the room in the middle of the night when she was asleep. He laid her on top of the shelf and tied the rope around her neck while the other end of the rope was tied to the ceiling beam. The killer places a pile of vases directly under the knot on the beam so that there would be a loud crash when she fell. The setup is to make it as if Masuko stepped on the vases and knocked them over to hang herself. The loud crash will immediately alert everyone to discover the body in order to protect the suspect's alibi. When Masuko lost her balance and fell, the nail on the top of the shelf cut her leg. The murderer didn't notice the nail, and the swaying body had scattered the blood around the room but not near the shelf. To cover the earlier blood traces, the murderer asked the others to move the body to the side of the room.

Masuko, who runs the business, frequently went to the storage room, and the vase would topple in a matter of time. When Masuko was drunk the night before, it was the best opportunity for Ryuichi to make it appear as if she committed suicide due to her regret for breaking the new vase. Ryuichi reveals that it doesn't matter because the broken vase was a replica made by him, and the real vase has been stored away somewhere. He doesn't want his master to get upset if he knows that Ryuichi's fake works are being paid more than his genuine workpiece.

### Case 046 - Scuba Diving Case
#### File 163 - Three Puzzled Tricks / Three Schemes
ogoro, Ran, and Conan are spending their time at the Izu beach. Kogoro tries to flirt with a woman, and it turns out that the woman is his ex-wife, Eri Kisaki. Apparently, it was Ran's plan of arranging them to meet at the beach in hopes of reuniting them, but it backfires. While they're having a drink at the hotel, Kogoro and Eri are bickering against each other. Kogoro later makes up an excuse and leaves. After a while, Kogoro reappears in front of them and is talking to two girls. When Ran approaches her father, he introduces Kiwako Toda and Harumi Matsuzaki to Ran. Suddenly, Kunio Kawazu appears and warns Kogoro not to mess with the girls because they're with him. He even asks Masahiko Matsuzaki to speak up since Kogoro is trying to flirt with Masahiko's fiancee and also his younger sister.  Masahiko and Harumi recognise Kogoro as the famous detective and Kiwako invite them to join them for lunch at the seaside restaurant.

Kiwako, Harumi, Kunio, and Masahiko are in the university's scuba-diving club. Harumi says that it's difficult to get her brother, Masahiko, to join the club since he can't swim. Kunio adds that a person still can dive even if he can't swim. Later, it reveals that Kiwako and Masahiko are childhood friends and are getting married soon. Ran tries to tease her parents, who were also childhood sweethearts, but it leads them to bicker again and sell their services to the couple. Kunio asks Masahiko where his other childhood friend is, and Masahiko replies that Hiroshi Ito went snorkelling after the dive. Kiwako asks Harumi to get a fresh towel from their room as she prepares to take another dip in the ocean. Harumi warns Kiwako of the danger since she just had a drink, but Kiwako replies that she only drank a few and she'll be fine.

Later, Hiroshi joins the group while Kiwako is snorkelling. Harumi waves to Kiwako at the sea, but it appears that Kiwako seems in a drowning state. Masahiko rushes to the sea to try to rescue her, but he can't swim. Hiroshi stops Masahiko from going further and rescues Kiwako. While Hiroshi is carrying Kiwako, Ran finds out that there's a sea snake nearby. Masahiko carries Kiwako back to the shore, but when he calls her name, there is no response from her.

<br>

#### File 164 - The Lost Weapon / The Weapon that got Away 
Conan rushes to the shore and tells the group that Kiwako might have been bitten by a sea snake. Hiroshi found the bite mark at Kiwako's left hand and tied her hand with a handkerchief. Conan runs to Kogoro and Eri and asks them to call for an ambulance after giving them the update. He takes an iced tea from the table for Hiroshi to wash the wound. The tannins in the tea will help counteract the venom. The ambulance arrives and takes Kiwako to the hospital, with Masahiko following along.

In the hotel's office, Ran refers to the illustrated guidebook and recognises that the sea snake she saw earlier was an Erabu sea snake. The hotel staff tells the visitors that there was a similar case in the past where a fisherman carelessly reached the snake that stuck in his net. The snake bit the fisherman, and he died after that. The Erabu is not an aggressive snake and doesn't go after the humans. It never happened to any guests in the hotel for the last 20 years. Hiroshi assumes that maybe Kiwako stuck her hand in a snake nest, but Conan believes that it was a murder. Initially, Conan suspects Hiroshi because he was in the ocean just before Kiwako almost drowned. Then, he remembers that Hiroshi was trying hard to treat her wound. Ran asks the hotel staff whether there's a sea snake with wings, but there's no such type. Ran remembers that she saw the sea snake had small wings behind its head.

They immediately contact the hospital, and Masahiko tells Hiroshi to get Kiwako's insurance card. Hiroshi asks about Kiwako's condition, and Masahiko says that he's unsure but thinks that the condition got worse because the doctors are scurrying in and out of the room. Harumi can't get into her room for the insurance card because she left her key card inside. Kunio calls the front desk for help, and Conan takes the opportunity to ask him about their diving session on that day. Kunio is unsure what others were doing at that time but reveals more about the relationships among Kiwako, Masahiko, and Hiroshi. Three of them were childhood friends, and Hiroshi is the same age as Masahiko. Kiwako and Harumi are a year younger than them.

In the room, they can't find Kiwako's insurance card until Hiroshi asks them to look at the bottom of the suitcase for something wrapped in a towel and tells them that Kiwako's grandmother taught her how to keep something safe in that way. Kogoro arrives at the hotel with a rental car, and Hiroshi asks him to drive them to the hospital. Since the car can only accommodate a certain number of passengers, Eri offers to drive Ran to the hospital before heading home. Kogoro asks Eri to have a word after that.

In the car, Eri drives speedily because she believes that someone tried to murder Kiwako. Conan, who follows along, also believes it was an attempted murder. Eri is not sure why Kogoro wants to talk to her after that and wonders whether it is about divorce or custody rights. She claims that Kogoro often tries to test her limit and see how she'll respond. The remark offers Conan an insight about the case, and he knows who the culprit is.

### Case 047 - Hospitalised Robber Case
#### File 166 - What Should We Do? / What to Do?
Kogoro breaks his foot, and the Detective Boys visit him in the hospital. Kogoro asks them to leave for their football game at the park. When the patient next to him, Daizaburo, says that he's lucky to have visitors, his son, Yoshio, arrives to visit him. Suddenly, the patient next to his father wakes up and asks Yoshio what he is looking at. Daizaburo asks Yoshio whether the police had caught the man who robbed the bank (his workplace) the week before. Yoshio replies that the robber probably is still on the run, and the father introduces Kogoro to him in case Yoshio needs help. Yoshio decides to leave, and the other patient glares at him from the back.

When Yoshio is outside the room, the police arrive and ask him whether he knows the man in the bed next to his father. He replies that he doesn't know anything and leaves hurriedly. At the staircase, Yoshio sweats profusely and says to himself that he can't do such a thing. Then, a man suddenly appears and questions Yoshio whether he's trying to pull out from the plan. Yoshio is worried about the presence of police and Kogoro. The man claims that he already made arrangements for the police, and Kogoro will be going to the lobby to watch TV. Yoshio is hesitant to carry out the plan in front of his father, and the man asks whether Yoshio has put his father into sleep. Yoshio asks whether his daughter is safe, and the man calls his cellphone. The man's accomplice, a woman, is accompanying Yoshio's daughter, Yumiko. Yumiko says that she's having fun and asks her father to come and get her once he finishes his hospital visit. The man ends the call, passes a box of snacks to Yoshio and asks him to do it for the sake of his daughter.

When Yoshio goes into the toilet, Megure is there. He is talking to the police officer about a man who was part of a robber gang and got into an accident while fleeing from a bank robbery. The doctor is going to examine the man at 3:30 and instructs the police officers to bring the man to the police station once he's declared fit. Yoshio thinks this is the only chance to tell everything to the police, but a police officer warns him that he's being watched. Megure and the rest leave the toilet, and Yoshio is dumbfounded.

Yoshio has no choice, and he plans to do what is being told before 3:30 for the safety of his daughter. Conan, who sits nearby, stops him from thinking of doing it. He asks Yoshio whether he is being instructed to kill the burn victim with the box of cream puffs. Conan figures it out because Yoshio is still in the hospital, although he told his father that he had something to do. Yoshio is holding a box of cream puffs which his father can't have because he's diabetic. Conan also observes that a few shady men with mobile phones are watching Yoshio, although mobile phones are not allowed in the hospital.

Yoshio tells Conan that the robber deceived him into coming to the hospital. The robber later kidnapped his daughter, and she's on the roof of the department store near the hospital. The man instructs him to kill the burn victim and puts the blame on Kogoro. Conan says that the robbers plan to kill Yoshio after that and put the blame on him, like an inside job for the robbery. There are three minutes left before 3:30, and Conan comes out with a different plan. He pretends that he loves cream puffs and suddenly takes the box from Yoshio. Conan opens the box and finds a gun inside. He takes the gun out from the box, and the robbers panic. Conan figures out that there are two robbers at the scene. The shady police officer pulls Conan's shirt collar, and Conan kicks the ball up to the high wall. The ball bounces back and hits the shady police officer. Conan kicks the ball to the other robber, and both are on the ground. Conan realises that there's another robber who wears a face mask. The man rushes to leave the hospital and calls his phone, telling the woman to take the kid hostage away. However, before he's able to finish his words, he's being hit with a tranquiliser dart. Conan uses his bowtie, mimics the robber's voice, and asks the woman to look down at the hospital entrance. Conan kicks the ball up to the rooftop with his power-enhancing kick shoes, and the ball hits the woman's face. At the same time,  the other Detective Boys are at the rooftop, looking for Yumiko and calling her name. They find her next to the woman who has already passed out, and Yoshio reunites with his daughter.

### Case 048 - Mysterious Clocks Case
#### File 167 - The Cuckoo Clock... / The Clock
Misao Nakamura, a mystery writer, sends a letter to Kogoro to invite him to investigate some strange occurrences at Misao's house. The house is an old western-style building which Misao recently inherited from the grandfather. Before Misao's grandfather passed away, he told Misao that the house given to him is special. 

When they arrive at the house, Kogoro is disappointed to see that the sender of a letter is a man, as he was expecting that the name belonged to a woman. Conan figures out that something is not right with the pillar at the top of the bannister. On one side of the pillar, there is a tiger at the top of the bannister. The other pillar has nothing at the top but only some odd holes.

Misao's grandfather is a well-known clockmaker named Monjiro Izubuchi. There is a collection of clocks, varied from wind-up clocks to digital clocks, but the digital clocks have scratches. The grandfather likes animals, and all the furniture has an animal on it. Misao comes with a tray of drinks but accidentally spills them. Conan realises that all the teacups have animals on the handles except one and the teacup has been smoothed over with a file. Conan remembers that earlier, he saw all the clocks have animals on them except a digital clock.

Misao and his brother bring Kogoro to the study room where all the strange events occurred. He says that the cuckoo clock makes noise at certain times and a strange thing pops up. When Kogoro is there, the cuckoo clock doesn't show any strange occurrences when the time is at 10:50. Kogoro is no longer interested and plans to leave. The brothers try to stop him and mention that there are other strange things that have happened in the house. Meanwhile, Conan sees the timer on the video recorder is set. As the clock strikes 11, all the clocks in the house go off, including the video recorder. 

<br>

#### File 168 - The Demon Appears!? / A Demon?
After a while, all the clocks stop ringing. Suddenly, they hear a strange sound. Misao claims that the sound was from cats living in the attic. Kogoro assumes that the grandfather set up some jokes. Conan says that only the digital clocks went off but not the cuckoo clocks or the others. Kogoro wonders why the VCR was set up since the TV is not connected to the antenna. Conan is thinking about why there is a need to use an alarm clock when the video timer is already set to record. Conan points out the odd scratch after the zero on the VCR timer. The others find that there's a similar scratch on other digital clocks. Kogoro thinks that the grandfather leaves a message behind. He asks everyone to look for the dogs in the house since the code '110' is an emergency number and police relate to the police dogs. 

Conan climbs the stairs and finds the attic is locked. Conan looks through a hole, and an eye looks back to him. Misao's brother brings Conan down and claims that the cat is inside the attic. In the study room, Conan finds a file with articles about the goblin crime ring. The leader was called "Demon", which reminds him that he saw a bag of golf clubs in the house with the name "Demon" on it. Conan finds out that the mirror is attached to the desk. He also notices that there is a weird hole in the wall behind him, and the cuckoo clock is above it. Suddenly, a mini figure of three goblins pops up from the cuckoo clock at 1:10. The letters on the goblins' forehead are "L", "N", and "R".

Conan wonders whether the code for the cuckoo clock is different from the code for the digital clocks. Suddenly, he figures out that "LNR" means the LNR triplets. He also cracks the code behind all the mysteries. Ran says that they haven't found out anything and it's getting late. Conan hints that maybe the answer will come up after the sunset.

### Case 049 - Actor's Apartment Case 
#### File 170 - The Actors Gathered / The Actors 
A famous samurai movie star, Kozaburo Hijikata, hires Kogoro for some consultations. Kogoro thinks that the star wants to talk to him to learn about detective work for an upcoming TV series. Kozaburo suddenly approaches them in a low-profile manner in the restaurant, and he invites the group to his house.

When the group is going to Kozaburo's apartment, they bump into his neighbour, Hajime Okita, who is also an actor. Hajime reminds him of having a conversation later about something important. Kogoro whispers to Ran that the rumour is about Kozaburo's wife cheating on him with Hajime. Isami Nagakura, Kozaburo's actress wife, welcomes the group and appears loving with her husband.

Behind the guests, Kozaburo praises Isami for acting nice in front of people. They're going to get a divorce soon, and Isami will get all his money from it. Isami is not sure whether she will be with Hajime after the divorce but is surprised with Kozaburo inviting Kogoro to the house since they will be having a discussion that night with Hajime. Isami says that Kozaburo is acting strange recently with the room redecoration and cleaning. She leaves him with the guests and waits for him in the office. Kozaburo replies that she won't have to wait too long for it. 

The group waits for half an hour before Kozaburo appears with a tray of tea. He says that he's not familiar with preparing the drinks, and Isami went to visit her friend. Kozaburo tells them that he bought a model handgun and asks Kogoro to show him how to handle it. His first scene is a shootout with a suspect along the river. They go to the veranda, and he shows them that the shootout scene will take place at the river across from the apartment. Suddenly, there's a phone ringing next door at Hajime's apartment, and they see Isami falling off onto the veranda there. Kozaburo wants to jump over to save his wife, and Ran tries to stop him. 

Kogoro goes down to the front desk to get the key to Hajime's unit while Ran goes to the police station next to the building. When the police arrive, Kozaburo says that he has been pounding the door for some time, but there's no one answering from inside. Suddenly, Hajime opens the door and wonders what the commotion is about. Kozaburo dashes into the apartment and finds Isami still lying on the floor. Kogoro checks on Isami and tells them that she's no longer breathing. She was strangled to death with something like a fishing line less than 30 minutes ago. Hajime is shocked and unaware that Isami is on his veranda. Conan thinks that the key thing is to find the murder weapon first, but when he sees Kozaburo's crocodile tears, Conan begins to suspect him.
