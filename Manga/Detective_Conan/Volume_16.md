# Detective Conan Volume 16 - The Black Star

## Summary

### Case 042 - Bond of Fire Case
#### File 151 - White Murder / Murderer in White
Kogoro and Yasue (the chairman's second daughter and the victim's wife) are convinced that Hideomi (the chairman's son) is the killer. Miyuki (the chairman's secretary and Hideomi's fiancee) doesn't believe that Hideomi is the murderer, and Heiji is unsure whether the bandaged man is Hideomi. At that stage, Hideomi is missing, and Kogoro assumes that he already fled away after the killing. Then, a police officer informs Megure that they had reviewed the tape from the security camera. The tape didn't show anyone leaving the mansion after the crime occurred. When they realise the suspect is still in the mansion and Ran is with the chairman, Conan and Heiji rush to the chairman's bedroom. Nobuko, the eldest daughter of the chairman, opens the door and claims that she went to the room after she heard Yasue's scream. Megure asks her where she was before the scream, and she replies that she was in her room drinking before that. She asks whether it is true that Hideomi has killed Mitsuaki.  Nobuko later reveals that Hideomi and Mitsuaki were friends when they were in the same grade school. However, after the fire incident, they never got along. The fire incident also caused Hideomi to burn his face when he rescued Miyuki. 

Conan asks the reasons why Heiji invited him to the mansion for the case. Heiji tells Conan that on the night before, the chairman heard the sound of someone running down the hallway and also the sound of two things hitting each other. Heiji believes it will be easier to catch the predator with additional help. Both Conan and Heiji believe it's a premeditated murder, and the strange sound was probably someone practising the murder method beforehand. Heiji wonders why the murderer left behind the cap and the bandages, which leads Conan to believe that someone is trying to frame Hideomi.

The police have searched the entire mansion, but they couldn't find him. On the second evening after the incident, Nobuko and Miyuki have an argument near the pond. Nobuko slaps Miyuki, causing Miyuki to fall, and her fountain pen drops into the pond. The butler tries to stop Miyuki from going into the pond because of the depth. Megure suggests draining the pond so that Miyuki can retrieve her fountain pen, which is a memento from her father. Once the pond is dry, they find Hideomi is lying there.

<br>

#### File 152 - Truth of the Telephone/ The Phone Call
Hideomi's death is estimated to be between a day and a half to two days. The forensic officer reports to Megure that drowning was not the cause of death, and it appears that he took poison before getting into the pond. The butler believes that Hideomi killed Mitsuaki because he is always against the marriage between him and Yasue. Meanwhile, a police officer found a paper in Hideomi's pants pocket. The paper is a suicide note for Miyuki. 

Heiji and Conan question the security guards and the kitchen staff. They found marks under Nobuko's balcony and also ransacked Nobuko's, Hideomi's and Mitsuaki's rooms. Based on their deductions and what they found in the room, Heiji asks Ran to buy something for him and asks Miyuki to have everyone gather in her room in an hour's time. Conan put Kogoro to sleep but later realises that they don't have any solid evidence. Suddenly, both Heiji and Conan thought of something, and at the same time, Conan found something inside an electric kettle. 

When Megure and others enter the room, Kogoro tells them that he knows who killed Mitsuaki and drowned Hideomi. Megure believes that Hideomi was the killer, and the handwriting analysis confirmed that the writing on the suicide note was Hideomi's. The phone suddenly rings, and Conan asks Megure to pick up the phone, telling him that the truth of what happened that night will be revealed from it.

#### File 153 - Bonds of Fire / Bonds of Flame

Megure picks up the phone, and Heiji replays that night's incident. Heiji claims that someone has turned off the lights and is coming to him with a knife. Megure wants to rush to the room below to help Heiji, but Conan stops him and tells Megure that the perpetrator got everyone to leave the chairman's bedroom on that night the crime occurred. The perpetrator uses the opportunity to execute his terrifying act in the chairman's bedroom after that. Suddenly, a bandaged man who appears to be Heiji climbs up to the room from the room below. Conan reveals that Mitsuaki was the bandaged man in that night's incident. He purposely showed his bandaged face to everyone to frame Hideomi. Mitsuaki took some of his blood with a syringe and sprayed it onto his face before putting on the bandage. He probably used a swim cap to ensure none of his hair was found on Hideomi's hat that he wore. Mitsuaki was planning to kill the chairman after his accomplice had drugged the chairman to sleep. However, he was killed by the accomplice after that. 

Heiji reveals that Miyuki was the killer who killed Mitsuaki. Mitsuaki climbed up to the chairman's room and never expected Miyuki to be there. Miyuki saw him at the balcony and pushed him off. Miyuki's target was Mitsuaki, but she was willing to be his accomplice so she could kill him. Heiji explains how Mitsuaki used the rope with a hook and swung it over to the chairman's room, which he had been testing the night before the crime. Miyuki also tested how fast to run from Mitsuaki's room to the storage room where the spare key was kept so that she could keep the rest at bay after the murder.

Hideomi wrote his suicide note the day before the crime and committed suicide by drinking the poison. When Miyuki found Hideomi in his room dead, she began to plan to kill Mitsuaki. The corpse was buried in dirt, which caused the decomposition to be slower. Heiji assumes that Miyuki called Mitsuaki and they buried Hideomi near the pond. Mitsuaki later dug the corpse and sunk it into the pond just before the other crime took place. He did it while pretending to look for Hideomi. The security guard told Heiji that Mitsuaki was wandering near the pond before the crime, and Heiji also found a sheet covered with dirt in Mitsuaki's room. 

Ran doesn't believe Miyuki would frame Hideomi for the murder. Conan deduces that Miyuki's watch strap was broken when she pushed Mitsuaki off the balcony. Mitsuaki grabbed Miyuki's hand when he was falling, and she used the tip of the fountain pen to stab his hand. Heiji found that the nib of the pen was bent when he wanted to write a note. Heiji believes that Hideomi and Mitsuaki were the ones who caused the fire that happened twenty years ago. Conan deduces that Hideomi never had his facial burns fixed because of his shame and regret about the fire. Megure concludes that Miyuki was avenging her parents who died in that fire. Suddenly, Miyuki threatens them while turning on the lighter and holding the electric kettle with gasoline. Miyuki reveals that she couldn't forgive Mitsuaki, who lives freely without guilt, and Hideomi, who kept it a secret from her. She fell in love with Hideomi, who rescued her from the fire. He kept encouraging her when she was in the orphanage. Later, she proposed to him, and he accepted it. Miyuki decides to commit suicide to join Hideomi but later finds out that the electric kettle is empty because Heiji and Conan emptied it beforehand. 

After Miyuki is led away by the police, Heiji questions whether it's better to let her die. Conan strongly disagrees because if a detective does nothing to stop a suspect from committing suicide, the detective is equivalent to a murderer himself. Heiji teases Conan about being perfect, but Conan says that he's not a perfect person. Conan thinks of Narumi Asai from the Moonlight Sonata Murder Case, who threw him out of the fire and committed suicide by staying in the burning house.

Before Miyuki leaves the mansion with the police, the chairman reveals that he gave the fountain pen to her mother when they were in elementary school before she moved and left their school. He hopes Miyuki will live fully and believes that's what her mother would want.

Later, on a different day, Conan reveals to Heiji that Miyuki's mother was the chairman's first love.  The chairman lied about Miyuki's alibi to protect her. Conan thinks that the chairman suddenly wanted to look for his first love because he saw a resemblance to her in his secretary, Miyuki.

### Case 043 - School Mystery Case
#### File 154 - The School's Mystery / Mystery at School
A rumour is circling in Conan's school that something mysterious happened at night. It started a week ago when a student went to the art room and all eight sculptures were glaring at her in frozen eyes. Then, a group of students claim that they saw the anatomical model running speedily down the hallway.  When Conan and Ayumi are cleaning up the classroom after school, she tells him that she saw someone in a face mask wandering around in the dark classroom a few nights ago. Ayumi already told the vice-principal about that incident, and he promised her that he will look into it. However, the vice-principal was absent on that day and the day before. She asked the teachers about the vice-principal, but the teachers didn't know why he was absent. Ayumi went to the vice-principal's house, but nobody answered.

The Detective Boys come at night to investigate the string of mysteries that occurred recently in the school. Ayumi is shocked when she sees the anatomical model and runs out from the infirmary. She bumps into a box of toys, and they find a figure that has their name on it. Conan feels their names on the figure are not a coincidence. Suddenly, Mitsuhiko sees the anatomical model is moving. Conan believes what Mitsuhiko sees is real because Ayumi's handkerchief which she dropped earlier is stuck underneath the base of the anatomical model. Conan believes someone has moved the model, but he doesn't know what the perpetrator's intention is.

#### File 155 - Someone's There!?

Conan sees someone on the other side of the windows, looking at them, but when the Detective Boys run to the room, they can't find anyone there. Conan is convinced someone was there because there's a cloudy spot on the window, which indicates someone pressed their cheek up against the glass window. Conan wants to call the security guard, but the rest suggest that they go to the teacher's room. Conan thinks the perpetrator works here since the teachers' room is unlocked. When Conan calls the security guard's room, nobody answers the phone because the guard is drunk. Conan calls Kogoro for help, but Kogoro is also in a drunken state.

The perpetrator uses smoke and paint to scare them. Conan decides that they pretend that they are scared of ghosts and leave the place. After hiding in the janitor's room for an hour, they come out and see a few shadow figures appear in their classroom. There are at least ten people, and the suspicious masked figure is there too. Conan wants to catch the culprit and runs to the classroom. When he turns on the light, he sees different figures with notes labelled with 'Parents', and the dolls are put on the students' chairs. Conan reveals the person who is hiding behind the podium is Miss Kobayashi, their teacher.

Genta and Mitsuhiko tell Miss Kobayashi that they prefer her to treat the kids nicely instead of doing the practice. Miss Kobayashi reveals to them that the reason she is strict with the students is because of what happened in her last school. She loved the kids so much and never managed to get angry in her previous school. On the first parents' day, a kid teased her and got into a fight with another kid. As parents' day approaches, she feels nervous and worries that the same thing will happen again. Miss Kobayashi knows that she is being called names due to her strictness and starts to question whether she is suitable to be a teacher. Ayumi disagrees and feels Miss Kobayashi is a great teacher because she's the only teacher who waters the flowers and changes the water in the fish tank after the class. The kids assume that Miss Kobayashi was the one that tried to scare them earlier with smoke and paint, but she tells them that she never saw them earlier.

After hearing what Miss Kobayashi said, Conan realises that someone else is in the school and rushes to the teachers' room. They find out that the vice-principal is there who tried to scare them away earlier. He is looking for his wig. Two days ago, the wig had blown off by the wind after Mr Ohata opened the window. The kids stay up that night to help the vice-principal to look for the wig, but they can't find it. The vice-principal says that he can't come back to school without the wig.

### Case 044 - Kaitou Kid and the Black Star 
#### File 156 - Chance Meeting / Chance Encounter
Sonoko's father, Shiro Suzuki, has loaned their family treasure, the "Black Star" pearl, to the Beika Museum for exhibition. She wants Ran to ask her father, Kogoro, to protect the treasure from the notorious phantom thief, Kaito, who is popular among the young girls. Sonoko prefers Kogoro instead of police to catch the thief so she can have a look at Kaito's face. According to Sonoko, Kaito 1412 is a master burglar who travels around the world heisting artwork and jewels. There's a theory that his name was the top secret international criminal number assigned to him by Interpol or other agencies. The number leaked out, and the public started using it. Ran tells Kogoro about the "Black Star" pearl and passes him a copy of Kaito 1412's cryptic warning message. The message was given to Mr Suzuki, which mentioned April Fool's Day.

On the 31st of March, Beika Museum tightens its security with more police surrounding the building. Shintaro Chaki, the Superintendent of Tokyo Police (2nd Division), is giving commands to the police to guard by the river. Around 11 o'clock at night, Conan calls Agasa to help him to do research on Kaito 1412 before he heads to the top of the Haido City Hotel. While waiting for Kaito's appearance at the rooftop of the hotel, Agasa calls him and tells him about Kaito's background. Kaito made his first appearance 18 years ago in Paris. After that, he disappeared for ten years and was assumed to have died. He reappeared now after 8 years, and his recent activity is mainly in Japan. Other names used to describe him are "The Heisei Lupin" and "The Magician Under the Moonlight". A young novelist took an interest in the thief's childlike behaviour of toying with the police around the world, took the number 1412 and redesigned it to look like K-I-D. It becomes "Kaito Kid", the moniker that most people use to describe the thief.

<br>

#### File 157 - Annihilation / Disappearance
Conan meets Kaito face-to-face and informs the police by lighting up the fireworks on the rooftop of the hotel. Conan plans to shoot him with a tranquiliser dart when he tries to flee, but before Conan is able to do so, Kaito imitates the superintendent's and the captain's voice and informs all the police of his whereabouts via walkie-talkie. Captain Ginzo Nakamori and other police officers rush to the hotel's rooftop while the helicopters are circling the building. Captain Nakamori orders Kaito to give up on the pearl and to surrender. However, Kaito says that he has no intention of taking the pearl on that day. The warning message has indicated that it's an April Fool. Then, he uses a glider to leave and release a flash bomb. Before Kaito disappears, he tells Conan that a thief is a creative artist who devises brilliant ways to steal his prize but a detective is just a mere critic who follows in his footsteps and looks for faults. He leaves another cryptic message that he would claim the Black Star on 19th April when the Q. Selizabeth set sail from Yokohama Bay.

On 19th April, there was a party for the 60th anniversary of the Suzuki Financial Group, the lavish ocean liner the Q. Selizabeth. Sonoko's mother, Tomoko Suzuki, has a personal challenge for the thief. She gives every guest a small box with a black pearl in it. There's only one of them that is real, and only Tomoko knows who has it. Sonoko calls her elder sister after she couldn't find her on the ship. Her elder sister is surprised that the ship has departed. She tells Sonoko that the superintendent called earlier, telling them that they were on a two-hour delay, and asks for confirmation from her father. Sonoko is shocked to hear that her father is at home because her father gave a speech earlier when the party started. Conan rushes to the toilet after one of the guests tells him where the chairman is. When he reaches there, he only finds the clothes that Kaito wore before to disguise himself as the chairman.

<br>

#### File 158 - Presence / Party Crasher
Tomoko reveals a hint to Kogoro about the "Black Star" pearl. She has entrusted the pearl to the most fitting person, but she never mentioned who the person is. The superintendent makes an announcement about Kaito being on the ship and suggests the guests pair up with someone with secret passwords. This is to prevent the thief from switching disguises from one person to another. Suddenly, Kaito appears on the top of the ship with a loud laugh. He claims that he already has the Black Star pearl on his hand. Tomoko retrieves a gun from her handbag and shoots Kaito. The guards caught him on the tablecloth, and he pretended to be shot by the toy gun. It appears that he is a magician hired by Tomoko for entertainment on that night. His name is Kazumi Manada, and he claims that he's an honest magician and won't be defeated by a thief. When he's performing magic in front of the guests, Conan recalls Kaito's final words before he disappeared with the glider. He knows Kaito is impersonating one of the guests, but he doesn't know who it is. Conan gets a guest list from Kogoro and tries to think of who will be the one that Tomoko entrusted with the "Black Star". The magician asks Ran to pick a card, and the card is a cryptic message from Kaito Kid. Everyone is panicked, and the superintendent asks all the guests to check their passwords.

<br>

#### File 159 - Finale / Final Act
The ship will reach Tokyo in less than ten minutes. Captain Nakamori commands the police to secure the entrance to the room. Meanwhile, Sonoko realises that Ran's pearl is missing. She sees a black pearl on the floor and asks one of the guests to pick it up. Before he is able to do so, the black pearl explodes, and more black balls appear in the room with explosions. Some of the guests throw their black pearls on the floor, as they are worried that the pearls contain explosives. The crowd is panicking, and everyone wants to leave the room, demanding the police open the door. Tomoko's black pearl is missing, and she screams because she has been wearing the "Black Star" all this time.

Conan pulls Ran away and tells her that he knows where Kaito is. They run to the engine room, and Conan explains the magician's trick about the cards, which contains the cryptic message left by Kaito before he addresses Ran as Kaito Kid. Kaito disguised himself as Ran and stole the Black Star when he pretended to help Tomoko during the chaos earlier. The dullness of the pearl that Tomoko was wearing and the fact that she wore gloves when handling it already showed that she was wearing the "Black Star". Kaito didn't steal the pearl in the exhibition because the pearl was shiny, which means it was a fake one. In the second warning message, Kaito specified the "real one" was to provoke Tomoko to bring out the real pearl.

Kaito asks Conan to call the police if he doesn't believe that he is Ran. Conan kicks the ball and hits the phone. He knew that Kaito is trying the same trick to distract him. Conan explains that on the rooftop of the hotel that night, Kaito didn't fly away with the glider, but actually, he mingled with the cops after distracting them with the explosions. Conan wants to confront him one on one by putting him in a prison. Kaito decides to give up the "Black Star" and toss the pearl to Conan. He tells Conan that he borrowed Ran's clothes and left her sleeping naked in a lifeboat. He suggests it's better that Conan go and save Ran before she catches a cold. Kaito disappears after throwing a flash bomb. 

Conan rushes to the boat with Ran's clothes. He sees the police have already found her and wants to bring her to the deck. Conan wants to stop them, but then he realises that Ran is still wearing the same clothes as before. Kaito leaves a message as a part-time dry cleaner and praises Ran for looking nice in the red dress that she brought in for dry cleaning the other day. Later, it reveals that the police found a hang glider, but Kaito has vanished from the ship.

Conan appears in the newspaper as the hero who protected the "Black Star". When Ran, Sonoko, and Conan are discussing what Kaito looks like, they see another guy who looks like Shinichi passing by and talking to a girl. It's probably the same person Ran mistook as Shinichi when the last time she saw him in Shibuya. Ran is happy and looks at Conan, and he looks confused.

### Case 045 - Famous Potter Murder Case
#### File 160 - The Machination of the Potter / The Potter's Plot
Kikuemon, a potter, invites Kogoro, Ran, and Conan because he's a big fan of Kogoro. Kikuemon has two apprentices. The first one is Yoshihiko Arita, and the second one is Ryuichi Seto. Yoshihiko's work is improving rapidly, whereas Ryuichi seems to be experiencing slow productivity. Kikuemon wants Kogoro to choose one of his workpieces as a gift for him.

Kikuemon asks his daughter-in-law, Masuko, to go to the storage room to take his new workpiece, which will be showing at the exhibition. Afterwards, Kikuemon decides to show them the kiln. At the kiln, they are talking about Masuko, whom Kikuemon entrusted to choose his successor. The three apprentices, including Kaoru Ooya, who is working at the kiln, are discussing Masuko's strictness, and their work is usually difficult to pass her standard. While Kikuemon is giving advice to the apprentices, they suddenly hear Masuko's scream. They rush to the storage room, and Masuko tells them that the new vase had dropped to the ground when she was climbing up the ladder. The apprentices are joking that Masuko always breaks Kaoru's work. Next to the ladder, Conan finds a marble.

At dinner, Masuko gets drunk, and Yoshihiko later puts her to bed. The next morning, she is no longer in her bed and doesn't answer her cell phone. Suddenly, everyone in the house hears something break in the storage room. Conan runs to the storage room, followed by the rest. They find Masuko hanged in the room.
