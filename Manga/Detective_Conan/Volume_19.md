# Detective Conan Volume 19 - And Then There Were Two

## Summary

### Case 052 - Murdered Professor Case 
#### File 181 - Why... / Why?
The suspects grow restless and plan to leave. Suddenly, Agasa shouts at them, demanding them to stay. Agasa is taken aback. He turns around and sees Conan is using the Voice-Changing Bowtie. Conan asks Agasa to just go along with it.

Conan demonstrates the killing method using a chess piece and a cassette tape. He wears the gloves and pulls some tapes from the cassette. He puts the cassette in the machine, pulls the tape across the room and goes out the door. He slips the tape through the key, leaves it outside of the door, and takes the rest of the tape back inside the room. Then, he uses three chess pieces of pawns, arranges them in a triangle, and ties the end of the tape over the pawn closest to the answering machine. He places the notebook on top of the pawns and turns it upside down. The pawns' rounded tops are facing on the floor, while the thick notebook will balance on top of them perfectly. Then, he locks the door from outside, places the key on the floor, and makes the phone call. When the answering machine switches on, the tape starts winding, causing the key to move under the door's gap. When the key reaches the middle of the room, the pawn is knocked down, and the key falls right under the notebook. Sango deduces that if someone keeps calling the answering machine, the tape will keep winding until there's no more evidence. This leads him to believe that Akira Shirakura is the culprit, who had left over ten voicemails on Professor Hirota's answering machine.

Akira denies he is a murderer and asks why he would come back if he is the culprit. Conan, who disguises himself as Agasa, claims that Akira needed to retrieve the tape that has his fingerprints on it. Conan deduces that he had an argument with the professor and bludgeoned him to death. Akira made it look like an accident, but he didn't wear the gloves because the murder was not a premeditated one. Akira was planning to be the first person who arrived on the crime scene. Once he and Mrs Hirota discover the murder, he will take the opportunity to steal the tape. Unfortunately, Conan's group discovered the body first. Akira purposely left his name on the messages because a tape with many blank messages is suspicious. Lastly, Agasa suggest to look for the professor's missing floppy disks at Akira's house. In the end, Akira admits that the collection of floppy disks are in his car parked outside.

Akira explains that he wanted the photo of him dressed as a drag queen with heavy makeup, as his pre-surgery face would be hardly recognisable. However, the professor sent him a different photo with Akira's face before the plastic surgery. He asked the professor for the drag photo, but the professor said that he forgot where the photo was. The professor said that he wants to send Akira's old photo to the magazine. Akira loses his temperament. The next thing he knew was the professor was already on the floor in front of him. Sango asks the officer to take Akira to the police station. As the police officers are taking Akira away, Agasa suggests that the professor sent him his pre-surgery photo to encourage him to be himself. Akira acknowledges the sentiment, wishing the professor had told him so in person.

Sango congratulates Agasa on his brilliant deduction but refuses to hand over the requested floppy disk as it is classified as investigative evidence.  Before they leave, Ai, who is in teary eyes, questions why Conan didn't save her sister Akemi. Conan is still confused about who Ai's sister is, and Ai tells him that her sister operated under the alias "Masami Hirota" during her missions for the Black Organisation. The girl breaks down in front of Conan, who can only stand there silently while thinking about both his bitter memories of a case that he tried to forget and the first time that Ai showed her true feelings.

One week later, the police return the floppy disk to the Agasa residence. Ai inserts the disk onto Agasa's desktop, explaining that all Black Organisation files are password encrypted, thus allowing for the contents of the disk to remain untouched by police investigation. She adds that not only does the disk contain data on APTX4869 but also contains critical information about the identities of several members of the Black Organisation as well as sponsors of the pharmaceutical research.

Meanwhile, Gin and Vodka drive through the streets of town while discussing the whereabouts of a woman who disappeared from the lab room. Gin is confident that the woman will turn up in due course and remains unconcerned about their floppy disks in the hands of the police, hinting that something else will take care of the disk.

Back at the Agasa residence, Ai, Conan, and Agasa stare in shock at the rapidly corrupting computer screen. Ai infers that it must be the Night Baron Computer Virus that activates whenever someone tries to open the disk on a computer not belonging to the organisation. Since the data in the floppy disk is completely wiped out, Ai remarks that she will have to stick around for quite some time with him.
