# The Convenience Store by the Sea (The Convenience Store by the Sea #1) - Sonoko Machida, Bruno Navasky (Translator)

## Summary
<blockquote>
Tenderness was a convenience store chain that operates only in Kyushu. Their motto was 'Caring for People, Caring for You'.
</blockquote>

The story involves the lives of various local residents who are regular customers of a convenience store called Tenderness. Tenderness is a unique convenience store which accepts suggestions from the public, and they offer special care for the senior residents who live in the apartment block above the store. Mitsuhiko Shiba, the manager of the convenience store, is a very attractive man who looks like a pop idol and frequently causes chaos due to his charm and care for the customers. Besides Mitsuhiko, there are other workers and a senior staff member, Mitsuri Nakao, who enjoys drawing manga and creates a manga story of Mitsuhiko's life. The convenience store not only offers delicious food but also the unlimited care of the workers for their customers. It's not as simple as knowing the customers' food preferences but also understanding their personal problems and giving encouragement and support whenever they need.


***

## Personal Thoughts
* I thought I might not like it because it's too light to read, but surprisingly, I find it peaceful and calm. I feel this book is a good read for anyone who wants to escape from a fast-paced life.
* A light reading with beautiful and heartwarming stories.
* All the stories are very meaningful and focus on telling the readers to follow your dreams and choose your path. I like the friendship story of Azusa and Nayuta the most, and also it's a very touching one.
