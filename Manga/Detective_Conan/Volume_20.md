# Detective Conan Volume 20 - Conan's Sense of Snow

## Summary

### Case 055 - Stadium Threatening Case 
#### File 191 - An Eye in the Distance / The Eye From Far Away
The football match continues while the police have only thirty minutes left to catch the criminals. One of the officers informs Megure that they lost track of the accomplice's car and left with the cash. Megure now hopes to get the man who is still in the stadium. Conan suggests reviewing the videotape from the broadcast. The suspect might have been caught on tape when he was looking for someone using a phone and binoculars.

After reviewing the videotape, the police can't find such a person who used binoculars and a phone at the same time. Kaneko arrives with over 100 million yen, way shorter than the requested amount. As Megure decides to put some newspapers into the bag to deceive the culprit, Wataru suggests that the culprit is not after the money but had always intended to shoot someone when the match ends. After Kaneko confirms that the caller has remained the same person in all three calls, Conan believes that the caller's accomplice must have given his gun to the caller after he shot the ball. The conclusion is based on the fact that the accomplice did not own the weapon when he picked up the money.

Megure instructs his men to review the videotape from the time the ball was shot until halftime. In that span of 36 minutes, the culprits must have been changing the ownership of the weapon. The match is almost over, but the police still can't find the culprit. Conan wishes the time could stop, and Ai tells him to calm down, saying that a man cannot stop the flow of time, and if you persist in going against nature, you will suffer for it. As Conan wonders what Ai means, he is distracted by the Detective Boys, who appear to be focused on the match. However, the kids claim that they're looking for the culprit but are annoyed that the camera keeps losing sight of the caller's accomplice. Ayumi points out that the camera shifts away from the culprit every time there's a sight of him. Conan figures out the culprits. Megure instructs to place the money at gate 18 and deploys his officers to cover the stadium exits when the match ends.

Meanwhile, Conan approaches the operator of the broadcast camera, Hiruta, who is recording the remaining few seconds of the match. He stands behind the operator and states that he is the man who threatens Nichiuri Broadcasting. Conan states that Hiruta made the call via his headset that connected to his phone and used his broadcast camera to maintain visual over the stadium activity. Additionally, Hiruta shifted the camera off his accomplice every time the accomplice appeared. The undercover officers pointed out by the caller were all positioned on the front-facing stands, and the only camera who could have seen them was the one and only camera stationed on the back-facing stand, camera 13.

Conan asks Hiruta whether he's willing to turn himself in, forcing Hiruta to remove his gun from his jacket. Conan attempts to tranquilise the man with his wristwatch, but the needle deflects off the gun. Conan remarks that he possesses a Beretta, to which Hiruta says his partner's TT pistol has since been thrown away. He says he obtained both guns for a bank robbery he had planned a year ago. However, the robbery was ruined after Nichiuri TV had spontaneously hosted a television programme at the bank. Hiruta's girlfriend, who was counting on the money, eventually fell into depression and committed suicide. Thus, he joined the broadcasting company to plot his revenge.

Conan asks Hiruta if he intends to shoot him, which will attract attention. Hiruta is confident that he can escape during the chaos. He plans to tell the story to the press that Nichiuri TV is a stingy corporation who caused the death of an innocent child. Suddenly a football lands on the pavement. Conan smiles. He quickly activates his shoes and launches the ball into Hiruta's face, incapacitating the man. 

The referee then blows the whistle, ending the match in a 2-1 victory with Big Osaka. Soon, the police would arrive and arrest Hiruta. Thirty minutes later, the authorities also manage to arrest Hiruta's accomplice, who was waiting at home, putting an end to the wild events at the Emperor's Cup.

The Detective Boys complain about their inability to both watch the game and contribute to the operation. However, Conan consoles his friends, informing them that the inspector praised them for their assistance for the successful capture. Ai congratulates Conan for his brilliant deductive skills, commenting on him as a truly interesting subject. Conan retorts that he wishes not to be patronised by an eighty-four-year-old. Ai corrects him and reveals that she is eighteen in a flirty manner, leaving Conan in confusion.

### Case 056 - Magic Lovers Case
#### File 192 - Run Away!
While Kogoro is driving down a road surrounded by trees, Sonoko demonstrates a simple magic trick, and Ran is fascinated with it. Conan joins the trip, and Kogoro drives them to a gathering of magic fans that Sonoko met online. Sonoko tells them that she met someone special online at the magic enthusiasts chatroom, and his name is Katsuki Doito. She describes him as someone who is witty and well-mannered in his speech. She imagines him as a handsome man, and he will be surprised by her magic trick. Conan, however, immediately criticises the simplicity of the trick, and Sonoko is not impressed. Ran asks Sonoko to ignore him but remains sceptical about whether she should attend such a gathering. Sonoko assures her, revealing that she has been posing as a middle-aged man on the chatroom with Ran as his girlfriend. Ran is surprised, asking how she could hide her name. Sonoko replies that she uses an alias and is called "Magician's Apprentice", while Katsuki's is "Red Herring". The group crosses a suspended bridge, and Sonoko is excited to go to the lodge early to surprise everyone. Kogoro is concerned about a strange smell while Conan sneezes in his mask.

When they arrive at the lodge, Sonoko is greeted by a man who immediately recognises her as the "Magician's Apprentice ". To Sonoko's embarrassment, all other members in the group were aware of her attempts at portraying a middle-aged man because of her feminine tone. She is then introduced to Kikue Tanaka ("Imposter Child"), Toshiya Hamano ("Disappearing Bunny"), Naoko Kuroda ("Illusion"), and part-time housekeeper, Kiyohiro Sugama. The person who greeted Sonoko at the door earlier introduces himself as Yoshinori Ara ("The Silent Ventriloquist"), the lodge owner. Sonoko asks whether Katsuki ("Red Herring") has already arrived. Yoshinori replies that Katsuki is upstairs. She soon hears the sound of descending footsteps and is instantly disappointed when she finds out that Katsuki Doito is a rotund college-age man. 

Ran later introduces herself and explains that Sonoko invited her to come along to spend the night, and Kogoro tells Yoshinobu that he is Ran's father to drop them off. Conan wants to stay overnight at the lodge with Ran, but Kogoro disallows it because Conan has a cold. As Kogoro and Conan are leaving, the group wonders about the whereabouts of the moderator ("Escape King") and "Shadow Monk". Yoshinobu decides to call him on the phone but receives no response. Without their knowledge, "Escape King" is seen dead with his head on the desk, and the computer screen in front of him displays the words "The first victim" and signed by "Shadow Monk". 

In the car, Conan asks Kogoro whether it's alright to let Ran stay overnight at the lodge. Kogoro is confident about it because Ran is stronger than the rest, and without her, he can join the party at the pub. Shortly after, the two notice a disturbing news story over the radio detailing Tsutomu Nishiyama's death. The report describes that Tsutomu was preparing for the same magic fan club gathering that Ran and Sonoko are attending. Both of them are in shock, and they quickly decide to turn back to the lodge.

Back at the lodge, the group discusses their favourite Japanese magicians during dinner. Everyone gives the name of their favourite magician. When it comes to Sonoko, she confidently answers "Kaito Kid", and other members give a few remarks about Kaito Kid's profession as a thief and his nationality.

Meanwhile, Kogoro drives speedily back to the lodge and notices a bellowing smoke rising in the distance. To their horror, they discover the suspended bridge is on fire, and the bridge is the only way to the lodge. Conan instructs Kogoro to call the police and sprints across the burning wood with Ran's safety in his mind as the bridge begins to collapse. 

At the lodge, Ran senses someone calls her name and decides to check the front door. To her surprise, she finds Conan unconscious on the ground, and his forehead is burning up.

#### File 193 - Ain a Snowy Meadow / In The Middle Of The Snow...
The group lays Conan on a bed and discusses the reason he collapsed in front of the lodge. Ran remembers that Conan mumbled something like asking her to get out of the lodge, but she couldn't understand what he meant. Katsuki brings some medicine and reveals that he's a medical student. Yoshinobu suggests contacting Tsutomu again.

Meanwhile, Kogoro calls Megure to send some officers to the lodge. The inspector tells him that it's difficult to go up the mountain late at night and mentions that they tried to call the lodge, but it couldn't go through. Megure tells Kogoro that they are on their way and asks him to stay put.

Back at the lodge, the phone lines are not working, and the group decides to choose someone to be an interim leader. Toshiya suggests competing for the title via magic demonstration. He begins by placing a blindfold over Sonoko's eyes and asks for a pen and paper. He instructs Kikue to write the names of each person on individual slips of paper and hand them to Sonoko. Then, he instructs Sonoko to write O X and ∆ on the back of three cards of her choice. Toshiya explains that the O marks the leader, the X marks the entertainer for their party, and the ∆ marks the individual responsible for preparing the bath. After Sonoko lays her cards out onto the table, Toshiya claims he can predict which task was assigned to which person, and he correctly calls Naoko the new leader. He also predicts correctly that Kikue will be in charge of the bath. Kikue leaves to prepare the fire before Toshiya, then calls out Katsuki as the entertainer. This time, the prediction goes wrong. When Yoshinobu turns the card over, it reveals Toshiya's name. While Naoko is still wondering how to lead, Yoshinobu says to her that it's just for temporary until the real leader arrives. Then, he goes to the wine cellar to choose some wines for the group and asks Kiyohiro to prepare some appetisers.

Sonoko checks on Ran and Conan in the bedroom. She asks Ran to join the party, but Ran is hesitant as she is still worried about Conan's condition. Sonoko insists she join to see Toshiya's magic performance since Conan's fever has gone. 

Some time later, the table is set for their party. Kikue returns after preparing the bath, and Yoshinobu returns with the selected wine. Toshiya is still upstairs practising his magic tricks. The group discusses when "Escape King" (Tsutomu Nishiyama) and "Shadow Monk" will arrive since both of them don't get along. Conan then suddenly appears in the room, telling them that Tsutomu will not come because he was murdered in his apartment. He relays the news he had heard over the radio, believing that "Shadow Monk" is planning to murder more members of their group. Kikue suddenly remembers that Toshiya is also having conflicts with "Shadow Monk" and "Escape King". The group quickly goes to Toshiya's room to inform him of the alarming news but finds him missing. Kikue notices the balcony door is open and Conan rushes to investigate. Ran then notices Toshiya lying in the snow for some distance from the lodge. Conan looks on in disbelief. Everyone rushes out of the lodge and heads for Toshiya's location. Katsuki examines Toshiya and confirms that he is dead. He then instructs everyone to keep their distance from the body, stating that the site has become a crime scene. Yoshinobu appears confused, and Conan explains that the body is over 10 metres from the lodge, but there are no footprints before theirs.

#### File 194 - Suspicious Movement/ Suspect Behaviors
Katsuki notes that Toshiya was strangled before being transported to the middle of the field. He deduces that the killer is nearby the lodge, and Kiyohiro reminds them that the group is still missing a member, "Shadow Monk". Kikue intends to leave the lodge immediately, and Conan tells her that the bridge is destroyed. Besides that, the phone lines are cut, and their cell phones are without service, leaving the group isolated from the outside world.

Megure informs Kogoro that the police have tracked the person using the identity of "Shadow Monk", but the person has no connections to any magicians, and he hasn't touched a computer for a couple of years. He states that someone else must have stolen his identity. Additionally, the inspector mentions the police have discovered that a suspicious person in the group gathered at the lodge had been using a false ID online.

At the lodge, the group quietly sits at the dining table. Sonoko begins to feel terribly guilty about choosing Toshiya to provide entertainment. Katsuki consoles her, saying that she was blindfolded and Toshiya was the one who controlled the decisions when he chose her as his magician's assistant. Yoshinobu then wonders why Toshiya and Tsutomu were killed, mentioning the argument that took place in the chatroom few months ago. The group explains that "Shadow Monk" had accused the members of causing the supposedly accidental death of legendary escape artist Fuden Harui last month. Six months ago, the chatroom hosted a discussion about wanting to revive an old TV programme that involved a dangerous magic trick. When the accident happened, "Shadow Monk" said it was because of the discussion about bringing the show back. Yoshinobu was thinking of what he said and asked Tsutomu to check whether Harui was in the chatroom. Tsutomu didn't think Harui was there, but no one knows for sure. He then reveals that Tsutomu had invited the members of the club who were present in the chatroom that day to confirm whether Harui was present. Naoko says that "Shadow Monk" only joined the club recently and wonders how he knew about the chat six months ago. Kikue suddenly recalls that "Shadow Monk" used to say that he can fly up in the sky and make himself disappear, leading Katsuki is convinced that "Shadow Monk" is the killer.

As the room gets colder, Conan, Ran, Katsuki, and Kikue head upstairs to find warmer clothing. While putting on a larger sweater, Ran tells Conan of the events when he was sleeping. Suddenly, Kikue notices a strange sound coming from the window followed by a crossbow bolt darting across the room. The bolt strikes the wall, and Kikue rushes to open the window to check who the culprit was. Both Conan and Katsuki shout at her to stop, but Sonoko's scream then echoes throughout the lodge, alerting everyone to the bathroom.

The group discovers Sonoko in a crouched position with another crossbow bolt stuck in the bathroom mirror. Kikue reaches her breaking point and sprints outside to find the culprit. She ignores the group's warnings, forcing them to pursue her in a thick snow. The group finds out that there are footprints all over the place, and both Kikue and Sonoko stumble and fall in the deep snow. Yoshinobu manages to find the culprit's dropped crossbow, and everyone feels relieved that the culprit is not one of them. At the same time, Conan notices Katsuki plunge his fist into the snow in frustration.

#### File 195 - Investigation / Search
Kikue is still feeling distress, and Ran suggests going back to the lodge for safety. Conan notices that there's a tree with a pile of snow falling off the branches to the ground. Looking closely, he finds a small hole carved into the trunk. Conan feels there's something not right about Katsuki and decides to ask around for their alibi at the time of the crime.

Conan poses the question of their whereabouts during the crime once everyone settles themselves in the main room. Katsuki says he was setting the table with Naoko. Naoko confirms that statement and claims that she later went into the kitchen to help Kiyohiro prepare the appetisers. Katsuki went to his room on the upper floor to get some crackers for the party. Sonoko says that she went into the room to check up on Ran. Kikue was lighting firewood for the bathwater while Yoshinobu was in the wine cellar, and both took their time, around 8 minutes. When Conan asks Yoshinobu why he spent such a long time to get some wine, the man says he had forgotten that he replaced the lock and had to return to his room to grab the new key. Kikue feels odd with the statement, leading Yoshinobu to offer to show them the cellar.

Yoshinobu explains that someone broke into the lodge recently, and he has to increase the security by adding more locks on the cellar. Conan also asks to see where Kikue heated the bathwater. Yoshinobu shows him a neatly piled stock of firewood covered by a slanted metal roof. He notices a window to the second floor placed directly above the roof and suggests Kikue could reach the second floor from the exterior of the building. Kikue agrees but notes that she entered the house several times to monitor the temperature of the bath, meaning other people also could do the same thing. Conan walks off in his deep thoughts and soon finds a staple on the ground in front of the bathroom window. He climbs atop the windowsill and finds two staple holes clearly on the building's exterior. Before he can investigate further, he is caught by Katsuki and returned to Ran and Sonoko.

Before they return indoors, Conan asks Sonoko to explain the magic trick used by Toshiya to assign the tasks. Conan is still trying to figure out how Toshiya's body was placed in the snow without any footprints around. Yoshinobu then offers the group wine. Conan grabs a bottle and flips it upside down. He sees the inverted image of a sail ship on the label of the wine and confidently smirks, having cracked the case. Conan then asks Yoshinobu if he can borrow a stapler, a pair of scissors, and a long string with an excuse to make progress on a school art project. The man agrees and takes him upstairs, where the requested objects are stored. However, before Yoshinobu offers him a box of coloured pencils, Conan disappears from the room. Yoshinobu returns to the dining table and reports his disappearance, adding that the spare crossbow bolt he had kept is also missing. Suddenly, they hear Conan's scream from a nearby room, prompting everyone to rush to him.

The group find Conan in one of the bedrooms standing besides a bed with his back facing the door. A crossbow bolt then flies through the window and into the wall. Conan looks out the window and claims he can see someone outside. He rushes out of the house as the group is forced to chase after him. Ran finally catches him and scolds him for his dangerous act. Conan appears satisfied with the experiment. He turns to Sonoko and happily claims that the plan works. Sonoko is confused, and as Conan goes closer to her, Conan uses the opportunity to tranquillise her with his wristwatch. Using Sonoko's voice while she's asleep, Conan asks Ran to pick up the crossbow behind her and take it to Toshiya's room. The group are in confusion, and Conan declares that he (Sonoko) and Ran will reenact a violent magic show performed by the killer.

By firing the bolt high and away, the strings are pulled through their rings and fly off with the bolt into the night. Kiyohiro says that there are still two bolts stuck in the trees. Conan explains that the culprit burned the bridge and isolated them from the outside world to retrieve the bolts before the police could arrive. Conan  then reveals that Kikue Tanaka is the culprit.

Kikue points out that she was nearly killed by the crossbow bolt, indicating that it's impossible that she's the killer. Conan explains how Kikue put a staple into the wall above the window and tied a string to it. Then, she tied a weight to the string and looped it around the railing on the balcony above. The string slipped under the window and attached it to the middle of the bed under the clothing. When she picked up the coat, she cut the string, causing the weight to smash the window. At the same time, she took a crossbow hidden under her jacket and shot it behind her. While Ran was looking at the arrow, she pulled the string, raised the weight, and opened the curtain to show Ran the smashed window.

Kikue says that there was another bolt that hit the bathroom mirror, and Conan explains that an almost similar trick was used to perform that act. Following the incident with the second bolt, Kikue stormed outside to find the culprit, using the time to collect the bolts that were still attached to the trees.

Kikue states that it's impossible that she could perform multiple tasks at the same time while she was heating the bath. She also points out that Sonoko was the one who assigned her to prepare the bath. Conan reveals that those papers were already marked. Kikue would write down the names according to their predetermined plan onto the respective sheets of paper. She then swapped out the pen for an inkless pen and handed the tool to a blindfolded Sonoko, who believed she was legitimately writing the symbols onto the sheets. Therefore, Kikue was directly responsible for the task assignments made via the magic trick. Kikue changed the initial plan when Toshiya was assigned to the role of entertainer. She used the metal awning above the firewood, waited for Toshiya to come into the room, and strangled him. Conan deduces the recent thief to the cellar was Kikue so that she can research the surroundings to prepare for her murder. Kikue asks for evidence and Conan points out that the bolt with a loop of string on the end is in one of her boots. Conan uses Sonoko's identity claims to have seen Kikue in the act of hiding the remaining bolt in her boot while she was collecting them from the trees.

As the group discusses the identity of "Shadow Monk", Conan states that "Shadow Monk" is another account operated by Kikue, who probably owned two modems and two computers. Kikue pulls out the hidden arrow from her boot and reveals that Kikue Tanaka is her real name and she is the granddaughter of Fuden Harui. "The Imposter Child" was originally the account that Harui used while participating in the chatrooms.

She tells them that she finally understood the reason her grandfather forced his aged body to perform the escape tricks after she went through his belongings. She knew her grandfather did it in his own will, and she didn't blame the others since they drove up the group's popularity. However, she states that she couldn't forgive Toshiya and Tsutomu for their heartless remarks about her grandfather's death. Kikue was in rage and heartbroken after learning that her grandfather had intended to reveal his identity to the chatroom if the trick was successful. Naoko then says that if those two were aware of Harui's presence, they wouldn't have made such comments. Kikue acknowledges the sentiment but reveals that Katsuki had been aware all along, as Harui had received an email from Katsuki shortly before his stunt, wishing him good luck. Conan then adds that it is likely because Katsuki was a magic enthusiast. 

Conan walks through the second floor of the lodge, approaching Toshiya's room. He reveals that "Katsuki Doito (ドイトウ カツキ)" is an anagram of none other than "Kaito Kid (カイトウキッド)." He swings the door open to see the Phantom Thief standing atop the open balcony. Conan asks about Ran's whereabouts, to which Kaito says she is sleeping in the room next door, free of harm. 

Kaito explains that he came to investigate when he saw "Imposter Child" kept appearing online after Harui's death. "Imposter Child" was Harui's stage name when he debuted. He confesses that after seeing Kikue's face, he became sure she was Harui's granddaughter, but he didn't realise she was the killer until it was too late. Nonetheless, Kaito commends Conan for his incredible deduction and expresses his hopes to meet him again one day. Kaito sets off a cloud of smoke and disappears in his hang glider.

Shortly after, Megure and Kogoro arrive at the lodge with the police helicopter. Kikue makes her confession, and the case is closed.

Sonoko, however, was extremely upset about failing once again to meet her crush, Kaito Kid. She drowns her romantic sorrows away through karaoke, dragging Ran and Conan along with her. However, three days later, her enthusiasm for looking for romance is back and excited to tell Ran that she met another guy online at a Bonsai group.

### Case 057 - Sealed Bathroom Case
#### File 197 - Sayonara / Goodbye...
Mina Aoshima returns home with bath cleaner, packing tape, and beer as requested by Masayo Aoshima. Mina asks Masayo whether she's taking away their collection of pop star videos. Masayo confirms it and also plans to take along her books and CDs, mentioning that she's moving out as a favour to her. Mina offers to help with the packing, and Masayo apologises for asking her to run errands and helping her to pack at such a last minute. Mina then expresses her appreciation for the concert tickets given to her and Kenji. Masayo suggests having a drink to celebrate, adding that she was too busy and never properly congratulated her younger sister after the announcement of the engagement. Without Mina's knowledge, Masayo spikes her beer.

Meanwhile, at a restaurant, Kogoro is excited to meet his favourite idol, Yoko Okino, at a concert they will attend later. He is extremely thrilled but shocked after finding out that there's a hole in his pocket and his ticket is missing. Conan suggests Kogoro check the bathroom since that was the first place he went when he arrived at the restaurant. Kogoro appears disappointed after he couldn't find the ticket in the bathroom and suggests Ran find something else to do so he can have either Ran's or Conan's ticket. While Kogoro is persuading Ran for the ticket, they notice Masayo is in a heated conversation on a payphone. She got upset when Mina's fiance, Kenji, couldn't drive them to the Yoko Okino concert due to a cold. As Masayo angrily hangs up the phone, Kogoro approaches her with a proposition, asking if he can buy Kenji's spare ticket from her at a low price. Masayo agrees to give him the ticket for free, but on the condition they drive to her house to pick up Mina.

Masayo apologises for the trouble, but Kogoro is happy to do so for the free ticket. During the car ride, Masayo feels odd that Mina wasn't at their meeting place even though she was so excited about the concert. Masayo tried to call her, but no one answered. She then expresses her concern about Mina's wellbeing and that she bought the concert tickets to cheer her up. Kogoro is curious about Masayo's bandana due to familiarity, and Masayo states that the bandana is a limited edition included with Yoko's new album. Kogoro wishes that he knew sooner, and Masayo is surprised that they didn't buy the album, assuming they are Yoko's fans as well. Kogoro says that he gets all the CDs directly from Yoko, which exclude all the promotional goods. Masayo feels amazed and wonders whether Kogoro knows Yoko in person. Kogoro confirms the acquaintance, stating that he was involved in an investigation and introduces himself as Kogoro Mori, a private investigator. Masayo's face changes and drops the keychain. Kogoro wonders what's wrong, and she replies that she was surprised to meet a celebrity. Masayo looks worried after that while Conan takes note of her strange reaction.

Once they arrive at the house, Kogoro wonders whether Mina lives alone, and Masayo explains that she used to live there until the previous week. She is moving out because Mina's fiancé will be moving in next month. Inside the house, Masayo tells her guests to relax in her living room while she looks for her sister. The trio soon finds the room is covered in posters of various idols such as Yoko Okino, Asuka Anzai, TWO-MIX, and Terumi Hoshino. The sisters must be pop idols' fans, as Conan finds magazines and CDs in two with their names on them. While they're assessing the progress in moving out, the three suddenly hear loud banging noises coming from the bathroom. They rush to find Masayo, who keeps knocking on the bathroom door and frantically calling for Mina. The word "Goodbye (サヨナラ)" is written on the bathroom with duct tape. Kogoro asks whether the door is locked from the inside. Masayo says that there's no lock on the door, and it appears that Mina has taped the door shut from the inside. Kogoro suggests everyone push the door together. Once the door is open, they discover a bloodied Mina slouched on the bathtub with a razor in her right hand. Kogoro lifts her left hand out of the water to see several slash marks on her wrist. Kogoro confirms Mina's death and asks Ran to call for the police. Conan notices that the window and door are both sealed with tape and two bathroom cleaners on the floor. Initially, it appears suicide to Conan, but he changes his mind after he closely monitors the bathtub. Meanwhile, Masayo is in distress and questions how the murderer escaped the bathroom despite the room being sealed with tape.

#### File 198 - Visible Evidence / Tangible Proof
Inspector Megure arrives at the crime scene, stating that the cause of death is blood loss from the severed ulnar artery, which resulted from the knife cuts on the left wrist. He also notices that the window and door are sealed up with tape. Kogoro theorises that Mina attempted to mix a chlorine-based cleaner with an acidic cleaner to release toxic chlorine gas. He believes that she sealed the room to confine the gas. Megure disagrees because both cleaners are chlorine-based, and she hardly needs to seal the room if she wants to create chlorine gas. Kogoro assumes that the victim might not know how powerful chlorine gas is or how to make it. Megure is not convinced, but Kogoro adds that Masayo mentioned that she was concerned with Mina's wellbeing prior to the suicide. He then concludes that Mina resorted to cutting her wrist to commit suicide after she realised she had mistakenly purchased two chlorine-based cleaners. Kogoro earlier finds the purchase receipt, and Megure orders Wataru to check the purchase from the convenience store.

Conan points out the zigzag pattern of the cut on Mina's wrist, which Kogoro and Megure realise is extremely odd since almost all individuals will cut their wrist parallel to the fatal wound if they use such a method to commit suicide. Kogoro explains that maybe something else was sitting on the edge of the bathtub when the blood splattered, and before he can finish his sentence, Conan replies that another possibility is another person was next to the victim and cut her wrist. However, just as Kogoro declares that it's a murder, Masayo points to the duct tape. She suggests that perhaps the duct tape roll was placed on the edge of the tub and caught the splattered blood. As Kogoro congratulates her for the excellent observation, a forensics analyst informs Megure that only Mina's fingerprints appear on the tape, suggesting that Mina had sealed the room herself. 

Megure concludes that it's suicide after checking the "Goodbye" message on the door. Suddenly, Kogoro remembers something and tries to remind Masayo. Masayo is taken aback, but to her relief, Kogoro requests a spare ticket that she promised earlier and heads for the living room.

Before Conan leaves the room, Masayo accidentally drops her keychain when she tries to retrieve her handkerchief. Ran arrives to collect Conan, and they bid their goodbyes. 

As Conan walks through the hallway, he is confident about Masayo's guilt but remains ignorant about how she could seal the door with duct tape from the outside. Wataru then returns from the convenience store where Mina is thought to have bought her supplies. Wataru informs Megure that the clerk reported nothing unusual about Mina when she purchased the items. Wataru wants to add extra remarks but keeps it to himself in the end. Conan questions Wataru. As Wataru is telling him about something that the store manager said, Conan notices a blood stain on the interior side of the entrance door. Wataru asks why he would check on the house after receiving no response via phone call. Kenji explains that they were planning to have dinner together, and Mina always switches on her answering machine. After hearing Kenji's words, Conan smiles, having all of the necessary evidence to declare Masayo the culprit.

#### File 200 - Invitation to a Castle / Invitation to an Isolated Castle
Professor Agasa forgot to pack the camping tent, and he has to drive through the wilderness to find some accommodations for him and the Detective Boys. Ayumi hopes that they can find a place like a lakeside castle; Conan feels it will be a dream to find one, but Ai remarks that Ayumi's dream may come true when they see a large Western-style castle.

While the group is fascinated by the castle's beauty, Genta climbs over the front gate and trespasses on the property. Katsuo Tabata, the estate's gardener scolds Genta for illegally entering. Agasa explains that they are passing by and saw the magnificent castle and are hoping that they can have a look around inside. Katsuo insists that they leave the property, and suddenly, Mitsuru Mamiya, the husband of the eldest Mamiya daughter, appears and asks who they are. After hearing Agasa is a scientist, Mitsuru invites the group to stay a night in the castle. Katsuo asks whether it's fine to invite guests without informing Mother Mamiya first. Mitsuru tells him to say to the mother that the guests are his friends, depending on her condition. Agasa curiously asks what condition that Mitsuru was implying, and Katsuo replies about the forgetfulness that comes with old age.

The Detective Boys are happily exploring the spacious garden, where Ayumi is thrilled with a large statue of a horse. Ai explains the statue is a chess piece, and it's a knight. Conan adds that the lawn has been mowed to appear like a chessboard. Agasa asks whether there's someone in the family who likes chess. Katsuo says he maintains the chessboard by following the wish of the previous owner, Master Sadaaki. Master Sadaaki was carrying the will of his father-in-law, who died 15 years ago. Agasa then asks who was the man who invited them to stay overnight earlier. Katsuo replies that Mitsuru is Lady Mamiya's second husband. The first husband passed away six years ago. Mitsuru's wife, the only daughter of Mamiya, died in the great fire four years ago. Katsuo adds that the fire occurred the night the daughter returned to the castle to celebrate her mother's birthday. She arrived at midnight, and the fire happened before dawn. The incident also killed dozens of friends, workers, and family members. The only survivors were the matriarch, Mitsuru, Katsuo and Takahito, the only son of Sadaaki. Katsuo notes that both Mitsuru and Takahito had spent most of their lives abroad, only visiting the castle for the first time on the night of the fire. Though ever since the incident, the two have continued to live at the castle.

As Katsuo leads the group into the main hall, he shows them large portraits of the patriarch, Sadaaki. Besides the large portrait, both sides of the wall have the portraits of Master Sadaaki and Lady Mamiya, the daughter, who regularly expressed her aversion for her father's work as a historian. The group is then joined by the matriarch, Masuyo Mamiya. Hearing that Agasa is a scientist, she voices her hope that he will solve the mystery left within the castle by her husband. Katsuo clarifies that the patriarch stated shortly before his death that he will give his greatest treasure to whoever solves the castle's mystery. Conan then asks if there's a room where he can see all the chess pieces in the garden.

Caught in a dark passage, Conan activates his Wristwatch Flashlight and descends a winding set of stairs. His foot is then caught on a lump in the steps. When he points his light down to investigate, he finds the old remains of a deceased human. Before he can process his discovery, an unknown figure approaches him from behind and brings a brick down onto his head.
