### Case 049 - Actor's Apartment Case 
#### File 171 - It Must be Similar / The Same?
Hajime claims that he is shocked by what happened and has never heard anyone pounding on his door. When Megure questions about his whereabouts during the crime that occurred, Hajime replies that he was listening to music in that room after Kozaburo asked him to stay at home that evening. The three of them would be having a meeting that night to talk about the rumour between Hajime and Isami.

A forensic officer later finds a fishing line on the floor, but Hajime denies owning it. Megure plans to bring him to the police station for further questioning. Conan notices a chafing mark on Isami's arm, and he tries to imply that the body was moved to a different place. Ran suddenly remembers that when they saw Isami fall earlier, her legs were in the room, but now the whole body is on the balcony. The forensic officer finds several scrapes on Isami's body, which indicates that the body was roughly handled on its way to the balcony.

Kogoro accuses Hajime of being the killer and handles the body hurriedly when Kozaburo comes to pound on his door. Conan points out that if Kozaburo comes here for the first time, how could he dash to the balcony straight away earlier? Kozaburo claims that the entire apartment from the fifth floor up has the same basic layout, and he is confident about it because he owns the entire sixth floor, above the current floor.

Another police detective, Wataru Takagi, calls Hajime's unit and asks for assistance when there's a swarm of reporters in front of the apartment building. In the lift, Megure asks Kogoro not to reveal anything about the case and tries to distract the reporter when the police take Hajime to the station. Without their knowledge, Conan is in the lift with them and points out the loopholes of the case. He reminds Kogoro that Hajime opened the door himself when they went to his apartment unit with the spare key. If Hajime is the killer, he should run away after he opens the door and also hides the corpse in a better spot. 

Conan goes to Kozaburo's apartment to look for more evidence. Then, he finds a small part of the broken gun on the floor and searches the sofa. He is convinced that Kozaburo set up the trick to deceive them.

### Case 050 - Shinichi's First Love Case 
#### File 173 - First Love... / First Love
Ran is cleaning Shinichi's house  with the help of Sonoko. Sonoko feels disappointed with this arrangement of spending the summer vacation and mentions finding romance and first love at such a time. Ran wonders what Shinichi's first love was, and Sonoko thinks maybe Ran is his type since they are childhood friends. Sonoko guesses that Ran's first love is Shinichi, leaving Ran strongly denying it while blushing.

Suddenly, the doorbell rings, and Sonoko guesses that it's Shinichi who wants to give a surprise to Ran. When they open the door, they see a girl in front of the house and Sonoko recognises her. She was the president of their junior high in the first year, and her name is Asami Uchida. Asami is in the college mystery club, and she has invited Shinichi to celebrate her birthday next weekend at a vacation home that the club had rented. Shinichi and Asami were also football teammates when they were in junior high.

Asami later reveals a confession from four years ago. Sonoko remembers a rumour about a guy who tried to court Asami. Asami denies the rumour, and Conan asks Asami whether she can invite someone else since Shinichi couldn't make it. Sonoko is keen to join the party because of her interest in college men and even offers to invite a great detective to come along. 

The other students from the Literature Department in the party are:-
* Manabu Sawai - Senior, President of the Mystery Club
* Tomoko Hayasaka – Senior, Vice-president of the Mystery Club
* Shigehisa Noguchi - Sophomore
* Chika Miyazaki - Sophomore 
* Yoshinobu Morimoto - Junior

At midnight, Professor Takakura sends a fax to wish Asami happy birthday. Unfortunately, Asami has already fallen asleep on the sofa. They plan to leave the cake tomorrow and go to karaoke without her. At the karaoke party, Sonoko sees Ran looking gloomy and seeming deep in thought. She guesses that Ran must be feeling inferior after seeing how capable Asami is. Sonoko consoles her saying that Asami has already rejected Shinichi. Ran then tells Sonoko that she never knew Shinichi loved lemon pie until Asami mentioned it when they were baking. Sonoko tries to cheer Ran up and tells her that she has her own strength.

Kogoro returns from the bathroom and realises that some people are missing. He asks Manabu whether he has any cigarettes, and Manabu replies that he got them at the store next door. Tomoko and Yoshinobu come back to the room while Shigehisa is still outside to get some fresh air. When they plan to leave, Manabu sees a massive fire at the rental house through the window, and Asami is still inside.

The group rushes back to the rental house, and the fire is getting bigger. Ran wears a helmet and runs into the house with Conan to rescue Asami. They see Asami on the floor. 

Later, Ran wakes up, and Sonoko tells her that she is in the hospital near the rental house. Asami is sleeping next to her and experienced a mild carbon monoxide poisoning. Ran doesn't recall that she saved Asami but remembers that Manabu led them to the safety exit. She passed out after they went outside. According to the fireman, the fire started at 2.50 am. One of the neighbours noticed the smoke. The fire began in the living room when the cake candles fell to the floor and spread to the curtains. Tomoko is convinced that she blew out the candles before they left. Meanwhile, Sonoko tells Ran that Asami kept mumbling about being sorry to Shinichi when they carried her to the hospital.

Conan wonders why Asami was on the floor and not on the sofa when they found her during the fire. The furniture  appears at different positions than before the fire. Conan can't think of anyone at the karaoke who had the time to commit the crime. He begins to agree with Kogoro's deduction that Asami was the one who caused the fire. A fireman hands over the remaining fax letter to Conan to pass it to Asami. Conan suddenly remembers someone said something unusual during the fire. Then, some firemen are saying that the candles were lit and the cake was put on a chair near the chest of drawers.

Conan goes to the store next door and asks the owner about the customers who came in at three in the morning. He even asks an unusual question, and the owner agrees with Conan's guess. Conan now knows who set the place on fire, and the person is still holding the evidence.

### Case 051 - Missing Older Brother Case
#### File 176 - The New Student... / The Transfer Student
Ayumi shares the news of a new student transfer to Genta and Mitsuhiko. They wonder what the new student will be like, and Ayumi asks Conan about his opinion. Conan thinks maybe the new student is a nerdy type. The teacher comes into the classroom and introduces her as Ai Haibara. Everyone gives her a warm welcome, and Genta offers to be her deskmate. Ai ignores Genta and straight away walks to the desk besides Conan. 

After school, Ayumi approaches Ai and offers to walk home together. Genta tells Ayumi to give up, but both Ayumi and Mitsuhiko ask where she lives and offer to walk her home. Ai tells them her house address, making her living next door to Shinichi. Conan, however, does not recall seeing a "Haibara" name-plaque near his house. Ayumi and Mitsuhiko try to recruit Ai to be part of the Detective Boys. Ai asks them whether Conan is one of the members, and Genta replies that Conan is his right-hand man. They tell her that people submit their requests and put them into Genta's shoe locker. Genta opens the locker and wants to show that it will be flooded with requests for their service, but it appears empty. Conan suggests heading to the park to play football. As Genta wears his right shoe, he notices a request note hidden inside and eagerly reads the contents. The letter requests them to meet at a specific classroom, and Ayumi asks Ai to come along.

At the classroom, they meet a boy who claims that his older brother has gone missing. The boy tells them that he saw his older brother in the evening a week ago. The brother told him that he was going to a friend's house but never came back after that. The police are looking for him, and the boy thought the Detective Boys probably could help. Conan suggests that it's better to go to the boy's house to learn more about it.

When they arrive at the boy's house, the boy's mother calls him Toshiya and asks whether the group are his friends. Conan asks Toshiya to bring them to his brother's room, and the group searches around to look for clues. Genta thinks that the brother probably ran away, but Conan disagrees because he finds the brother's wallet in the drawer. Ayumi then discovers a series of bizarre paintings under the brother's bed. Ai immediately identifies one of the artworks as a recreation of a Picasso painting. They discover several recreations of various famous artworks. Conan doesn't think the brother was kidnapped for ransom money, as Toshiya himself would be an easier target. 

Conan notices that the quality of the artwork is still amateur and not good enough for artistic forgeries. The group's attention is drawn to another portrait, and Toshiya tells them that the image is Natsume Sōseki.  Toshiya explains that his brother is a big fan of the author's work, and the portrait has been displayed at a town exhibition. Since the brother just copied it from a pre-existing image, the painting was panned. However, there was a woman who was interested in his work. Conan asks him about the lady's appearance, and Toshiya describes that the woman wore a wide-brimmed hat and dressed in all black. Conan's eyes light up with shock as he interrogates Toshiya about the woman, believing her to be a member of the Black Organisation. Conan commands Toshiya to bring him to various locations that his brother might have gone to. Meanwhile, Ai observes the entire scene with a blank expression.

Sometime later, the Detective Boys feel exhausted after going to several places suggested by Toshiya. Ayumi proposes to take a break at a nearby convenience store to have some drinks. Inside the store, Conan notices a suspicious man dressed in dark clothing paying for a singular pack of cigarettes with a ¥1000 banknote. Conan thinks the man is strange because there is a vending machine outside, and the man has no reason to pay for the cigarettes at the cashier. Conan climbs onto the store desk and demands to see the banknote. He checks the banknote under the light, and he can't find a watermark on it. He casually notifies the store cashier to call the police about the counterfeit money and rushes out of the store.

#### File 177 - The Lady in Black / Woman in Black 
Conan tails the suspect from behind and tells the group about the discovery of counterfeit banknotes. Toshiya asks how the finding relates to his brother's disappearance. Conan points out that Natsume Sōseki's image is on the ¥1000 banknote, and Toshiya's brother has the skill to forge  an image of Natsume Sōseki. His skill probably attracts someone's attention, forcing him to create fake banknotes. Conan is thinking about whether the Black Organisation is involved with the underground counterfeit ring, and he accidentally mentions his shrinkage. His remark confuses the others, and he says that he is just messing up with them. He doesn't want the kids to get involved in such a dangerous operation, but at the same time, he doesn't want to lose track of the suspect. He approaches the man in black clothing and hands him a ¥1000 banknote, saying he had dropped it on the street. The man spitefully grabs the note and walks away in silence. After the group is disbanded and goes his separate ways, Conan puts on his tracking glasses and tracks a transmitter he placed on the banknote to a newsagency near the train station.

At the newsagency, the store clerk tells him that the man in a black cap was there earlier. He bought a can of coffee with a ¥1000 banknote and used the pay phone after that. He placed the changes on top of the phone and talked for around 10 minutes until the coins used up. Conan asks whether the man got his change of coins for the phone, and the store clerk explains that she was low on ¥100 coins, so she gave him ¥500 with his ¥890 worth of change. When Conan is thinking about whether the man had gone, a janitor approaches him. He assumes Conan is the man's friend and hands him a ¥50 coin that the man had forgotten to extract from the ticket machine. The janitor recalls that he purchased a Tohto line ticket using a ¥500 coin and had received at least three coins as change (including the ¥50). Conan digests the given information and determines that the man must have bought a ¥320 Tohto line ticket and heads to Daitoma station.

Conan arrives at the Daitoma station but fails to find any leads about the man with the black cap. He decides to enquire at a nearby real estate agency about any recent rentals at remote places. The agent denies any knowledge about such a property and instructs Conan to leave with his friends. Conan turns around and sees the Detective Boys, including Ai, are outside of the agency. His friends tell him that Ai had figured out his intention and suggested they follow him. Toshiya then asks the real estate agent if a novelist lives nearby. He explains that his house had recently received a frantic phone call from his brother, and his grandmother only heard something about "being with people like Sōseki". The agent then recalls a man who looks like Sōseki owning a bookstore nearby. 

The Detective Boys go to the bookstore but are quickly evicted from the establishment. Conan surmises that the owner is innocent since the store has no printing machinery. Hearing those remarks, the agent recalls that there's a newspaper company near the police station that had recently purchased several printers. When asked about the company's employees, the agent describes that the president is a woman who always wears a wide-brimmed hat. Ayumi and Mitsuhiko discuss the possibility of the newspaper company's connection to Sōseki, leading Conan to recite a famous Sōseki quote about hiding in plain sight. Conan deduces that Toshiya's brother is in the newspaper company. The kids report their findings to the police officers at a nearby police booth, but the police don't believe them. Conan tells them to stay put at the police booth. He runs to somewhere else, calls Inspector Megure as Shinichi, and informs him about the counterfeit ring. As Conan hangs up the phone, he is reminded of a previous encounter with a female member of the Black Organisation that ended in a horrific tragedy.

Genta disobeys Conan's instructions to remain at the police box and ventures into the newspaper company's headquarters in search of the counterfeit ring. The Detective Boys search around, but they can't find any evidence for criminal activity. Ai, however, finds various bottles of ink, indicating that attempts at counterfeiting were conducted.

Meanwhile, the woman in black hits the man in black for his recklessness in using the counterfeit money. Nevertheless, he delivers the requested ink, a special ink required for Toshiya's brother to produce a perfect ¥10,000 counterfeit. A member of the operation notices the five children in the room next door as their images captured by security cameras. Toshiya's brother recognises his younger brother, and the man closes his mouth to prevent him from making any sounds. The woman pulls out a firearm, intending to silence their little intruders. Back at the police box, Conan learns of his friends' whereabouts and rushes to save them.

At the same time, Gin drives his car down a busy city street while calling Vodka over the phone. The two discuss an individual who managed to escape their grasp and who could leak the syndicate's secrets to the public. Gin gives an order to capture the individual, dead or alive.

### Case 052 - Murdered Professor Case 
#### File 179 - The Girl Made of Lies / The False Girl
Ai reminds Conan about her address, 2-22 Beika, which is Agasa's house. Once Conan realises it, he dials the professor's number using his Earring Cellphone but gets no response. Ai replies with a smirk, saying that Agasa is busy and is in another world. Conan gives her a sharp glare. When they arrive at Agasa's house, Conan is relieved to see Agasa is safe. Ai makes herself at home, placing her bag on the table and taking out a magazine. Agasa explains that he is obsessed with the Internet, causing his phone line to always be busy. Agasa is surprised that Conan forgets his address, and he says that he never sends anything to Agasa by mail. 

Conan asks Agasa for details about Ai's background. Agasa claims that it's true that Ai Haibara is associated with the Black Organisation, and she had taken the same drug as Conan. The name "Ai Haibara" is derived from the names of two fictional female detectives, V.I. Warshawski (I = Ai) and Cordelia Gray (Gray = Hai). Conan shouts in anger and doesn't understand why Agasa let a member of the Black Organisation live with him. Ai explains that the professor saved her when he discovered her unconscious in front of Shinichi's house. Conan asks why she appeared in front of his house. Ai explains to him that the syndicate was investigating Shinichi twice because among those who took the APTX4869 poison, he is the only person whose death remained unconfirmed. Ai was with the investigation team when they visited the house, but the house was covered with dust and looked abandoned. On her second visit after a month, the house remained dusty, and she started to think that Shinichi had died. But then, she noticed that the childhood clothing was missing from the drawer. Based on the experiment on lab animals, in which the mice reverted to a younger state, Ai came to a conclusion that Shinichi Kudo had become a little boy. Conan is worried that the syndicate is aware of his survival, but Ai says that she listed his death as confirmed. She wanted him to be alive since she wanted to observe the prolonged effects of her poison.

Ai reveals that she betrayed the Black Organisation after finding out that they tested her drug on humans without her consent but adds that the main reason is her sister's murder. She kept asking for the reason for her sister's death, but they wouldn't tell her why. In protest, she refused to continue her research on the drug. In response, the Black Organisation confined her in a room at the lab while waiting for the leaders to decide her fate. Believing that she will be killed eventually, she attempted to commit suicide by taking the APTX4879 pill, only to transform into a small girl. Her shrunken hands allowed her to slip through the cuffs and escape through a garbage chute. She had nowhere to go, and Shinichi Kudo was her only hope. She thought Shinichi would be sympathetic to her since they share the same predicament.

Conan feels he has no reason to be sympathetic to a person who created a poison to kill people and even accuses her of being an accessory to countless murders. Ai implies that she was forced into the work, and the substance wasn't supposed to be a poison. Agasa tries to calm Conan down and says that she already left the syndicate and believes that maybe she can come up with an antidote since she was the creator of the drug. Ai replies that all her data is at the syndicate's lab, and she can't remember all. Conan asks for the lab's location, and she shows them a newspaper from three days ago about an accident that has destroyed a laboratory of a pharmaceutical company. She assumes that the Black Organisation is afraid she will expose them, leading the syndicate to destroy all the research and probably other facilities as well. They are now hunting her down, but they don't know about her transformation. Conan understands that if they find Ai, they will eventually track his whereabouts. Thus, he allows her to stay with Agasa.

The professor asks about Ai's parents, but the girl states they are deceased, having died in a laboratory accident when she was very young. The Black Organisation sent her to study abroad in the United States while her sister remained in Japan, working for the syndicate under surveillance in exchange for Ai's safety. Suddenly, Ai recalls that a few years ago, her sister sent several floppy disks containing photographs of her trip. She looked at the contents and later sent the disks back. Later, she noticed one of her disks which contains research data, was missing. Conan deduces that Ai may have accidentally included the lost data in the set of returned floppy disks. Agasa suggests researching the sister's belongings, but Ai says that the syndicate had killed her sister and cleared out her apartment. However, Ai remembers being told that the received floppy disks were originally from her sister's professor, Masami Hirota, who was a member of her trip.

Agasa manages to call Professor Masami Hirota through the university directory and asks about the floppy disks, giving Ai's sister's name, Akemi Miyano. Professor Hirota confirms that he is in possession of the floppy disk in question, noting the inclusion of a "strange disk" mixed amongst them. Agasa sets an appointment in three hours.

Conan notifies Ran of staying over at Agasa's house and sets off to Professor Hirota's residence in the Shizuoka Prefecture. During the journey in the car, Conan tells Agasa to remain alert around Ai, finding her ignorance about the syndicate's motives suspicious. He also expresses familiarity with the name "Masami Hirota" but remains unsure about where he had previously heard it.

Once at the Hirota residence, they are greeted by the professor's wife, Toshiko Hirota, who guides them to his office. She knocks on his door but oddly finds it locked. Conan climbs up to check the room through the high window. To his shock, he sees Hirota crushed under a bookshelf with a puddle of blood around his head. With Agasa's help, Conan breaks through the door and into the room, confirming Masami Hirota's death.

#### File 180 - Checkmate 
Inspector Sango Yokomizo arrives at Professor Hirota's residence and declares the death an accident, resulting from the professor climbing the bookshelf, losing his balance, and the bookshelf falling and toppling on him. The statuette fell first and hit Professor Hirota in the back of his head. The door was locked, and the windows were shut from inside. The only key to the room is under the notebook on the floor. Conan disagrees, pointing that the phone on the floor is covered by an open book. The handset is still in place and doesn't look like it fell from the table. The disarray in the room was created to look like an accident to hide a murder. Sango questions Mrs Hirota whether Professor Hirota had any guests earlier, and she responds there were four, including Agasa. Agasa explains his reason for the visit while Ai operates the office computer and finds that the entire floppy disk collection is missing. Conan and Ai suspect the Black Organisation committed the murder but decide to withhold their judgement until they meet with other suspects.

Toshiko only met the first guest before she went to a town meeting. The professor told his wife that the guest was a former student named Kazuhiro Hosoya. Conan finds the light on the phone is blinking, indicating the existence of voice messages. They check the messages on the phone and find the other two guests, Akira Shirakura and Michio Morioka. Akira suddenly appears and says that he left a lot of messages but never reached the professor, and so, he comes over. Sango also finds a voicemail left by an "insurance agent" wishing to visit his home to advertise a new plan. Conan looks shocked after listening to the tone and voice, and Ai recognises that it is Vodka's voice. Agasa turns to Conan, asking for confirmation of the Black Organisation's involvement, but Conan replies that the possibility has decreased. If their goals were to retrieve the missing floppy disk, they would not leave the voicemail at the crime scene. Ai agrees, surmising that Vodka set an insurance appointment that was likely to allow him and his operatives to better infiltrate the professor's home to retrieve the floppy disk. Ai believes that the syndicate will be worrying about the tape, and Conan smiles, believing the Black Organisation still lurks around.

The three suspects arrive at the scene and give their stories. Kazuhiro Hosoya visited to ask if Hirota could write a letter of recommendation for his daughter, though he left after noticing the professor appeared tipsy. Michio Morioka stopped by to play a chess match but left after finding the office door locked. Akira Shirakura, who arrived at the residence after the body was discovered, came to claim a floppy disk containing a photograph of him dressed as a drag next to the professor. He wanted the photos included in a magazine issue about the faces of models. 

Conan analyses the crime scene and is still thinking of the warped tape in the answering machine. Ai quietly observes Conan and recalls a conversation she had with Akemi, her sister. Akemi had told her about Conan Edogawa, describing him as a peculiar and intelligent young boy. Ai asked about Akemi's welfare after hearing that she was in trouble. The sister gave her assurance that she can handle it but worried about Ai, advising her to stop working on that drug and live a better life. Ai's mind dwells on a newspaper page reporting the one billion yen robbery and murder case, featuring a distraught Conan standing besides the corpse.

Ai approaches Conan and says that it's impossible to lock the door from outside and get the key under the notebook inside the room. She reminds him of the dangers of lingering and asks to leave. Ai declares "checkmate" and places a chess knight piece atop the phone. Conan, inspired by this action, solves the locked-room mystery and stops Ai from leaving. He wants to demonstrate what really happened to prove to her that there is no unsolvable mystery.
