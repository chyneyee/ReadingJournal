# This Is How You Die - David Jester

## Summary
### Part 1
Herman, a 16-year-old teenager, is experiencing torture and bullying from his schoolmates. He fantasises about killing the bullies and admires The Butcher, a notorious serial killer who was reported in the newspaper. Herman describes his father as an apathetic person who doesn't care if he comes home with bruises. His father appears as a kind and polite man from outside, although he witnessed his father abuse his mother once. A year later, his mother left them, and he blamed his mother for leaving without saying goodbye. When Herman arrives home, he finds the door is unlocked and discovers his father is dead in the living room.

After Herman's father's death, he finds an unknown key in his room. The key unlocks the box which is buried in the garden. Herman finds out that his father was the prolific killer, The Butcher, after discovering various body parts that his father collected from his victims. The box also contains files and floppy disks which record the details of all twelve killings over the last five years. Herman is shocked with the findings but also filled with reverence for his father's legend. He decides to continue his father's legacy and become the Butcher.

<br>

### Part 2
The second part introduces the detective, Lester, who has been actively pursuing the Masquerade in the past eight years. The Masquerade has killed a dozen people in the past years, including his bullies, his uncle, and the main bully's family. The killings are brutal and varied, although some of the victims are criminals. After killing eight people in one night, Herman disappears, and some assume that he's dead after he sets fire to his house. However, Lester suspects Herman is the serial killer, The Masquerade.

<br>

### Part 3
The third part focuses on how Herman targets his prey and what type of person that he's looking for to fulfil his satisfaction.

## Personal Thoughts
* This is a depressing story to read. Nobody has career prospects or futures. Men lead to gangsterism and unemployment while women lead to prostitution.
* I wish the writing style was more straightforward. Many unnecessary details. I think the writer tries to portray an angry teenager, but the mind is more than a teenager's mind, and rarely does a teenager have morality and a philosophical mind.
* The story is filled with rage, anger, sexual content, self-pity, and the fantasy of being macho and a hero. It also consists of too many discussions about youth issues and morality.
* Somehow, I feel the thoughts expressed in the story are beyond Herman's age and more like the writer's mind. Herman is a serial killer, and although serial killers usually have unusual thinking, after all, he's still a teenager. The story is portraying a mature or an adult psychopath.
* The narrator keeps mentioning that he has no problem with the issue that he raised, but at the same time, he severely criticises the issue and hates people who don't share the same hatred as him. I don't understand why the narrator needs to keep defending what he believes or hates and keep telling himself that he has no issues with it.
* The book feels like a place for the writer to vent his rage against the world.
* I understand the writer's attempt to portray the mindset of a serial killer, but I really don't like the derogatory writing style.
