# The Tommyknockers - Stephen King

## Summary
### Book I - The Ship in the Earth
In 1988, in a small town of Haven, Maine, Roberta "Bobbi" Anderson, a western writer, stumbles upon a plate-shaped metal object while walking in the woods. She leaves the object as it is, but the urge to dig more is getting stronger. Bobbi has no idea who to tell about her discovery but realises several strange incidents have occurred since she stumbled upon the spaceship, such as irregular menstruation, dead birds near the location of the object, and her beagle, Peter, acting weird.

Bobbi's friend, Jimmy Gardner, or "Gard", is an alcoholic poet and is prone to binges that result in violent outbursts followed by lengthy blackouts. Gard intends to commit suicide but chooses to go to Haven to check on Bobbi after hearing the "call" that she's in trouble. Gard is shocked to see Bobbi looks paler and thinner than the last time he saw her, and she collapses on him. Gard is also stunned when he sees Bobbi has invented several unusual gadgets. 

Bobbi brings Gard to the place where she discovered the spaceship. He feels the vibration and experiences nose bleeding when he touches the ship, but he's immune to other effects because of the steel plate in his head, which he has had since he was a teenager due to a skiing accident. Gard suggests Bobbi take action on what she discovered, but fearing that either the government will take over the land or she will probably be admitted to a mental institution, Bobbi won't report the finding of the spaceship to the government but chooses to continue digging the ship.

### Book II - Tales of Haven
Rebecca Bouchard Paulson was married to Joe Paulson, one of Haven's two postmen. Joe was cheating on his wife, which Bobbi knew through her new mind-reading ability. In the last few days, Rebecca became aware of the affair. She believed that Jesus communicated with her when she saw Him in the picture talking to her. Rebecca, also known as 'Bekka', suddenly becomes an expert in gadget invention and aware of other townspeople's secrets. Meanwhile, Joe has been winning a lot of money from his recent bets, but he's also often having headaches recently. His adulteress, Nancy Voss, is also capable of inventing something unusual on a toy car. Unfortunately, Rebecca's new gadget kills both her and her husband.

Hilly Brown is a ten-year-old clumsy boy but has been tested in school as someone who possesses high intelligence. On his tenth birthday, he is given a magician kit. He does a few successful tricks, but he wants to earn honest praise. Around the time when Jesus talked to Rebecca, Hilly had a lot of new invention ideas and invented a new gadget that can make things disappear and reappear. He successfully shows the trick to his small audience, but the audience doesn't show much appreciation. He hopes his last trick, which is to make his younger brother, David, disappear, will be a stunning show. Unfortunately, not only does it not capture the audience's interest, but David also fails to reappear. Hilly faints after that, and Haven's residents have to search everywhere for David.

Ruth McCausland is a constable in Haven. She senses the transformation of the residents, and they are watching her. In their mind, they're calling her to leave the town while they still allow her to leave, but Ruth insists on staying. Ruth has a strong defence to block others from reading her mind, and she senses that the aliens are taking over the town. During David's disappearance, she finds a message on the blackboard telling Ruth that David is in Altair 4. The residents are telepathically urging Ruth not to ask the state troopers for help. The residents don't want the outsiders to disrupt their transformation process. Later, Ruth decides and tries to leave, but she can't, as there is a barrier preventing her from leaving the town. Ruth detonates explosive devices in the town-hall clock tower, killing herself but sending a signal visible for miles. 

Ev Hillman, Hilly's grandfather, is a war veteran who has two small steel plates in his head. He leaves Haven to bring Hilly to the hospital. Ev, accompanied by Trooper Butch Dugan, returns to Haven to look for David. Dugan is later released by the residents with his memories altered and commits suicide after that.

### Book III - The Tommyknockers

Gard goes into the shed and finds out that Ev, Anne (Bobbi's sister), and Peter are wired to shower cabinets filled with glowing green liquid. Their life energy is painfully drained to power the growing alien technology. Bobbi and Gard finally finish digging and discover the hatch. Bobbi and Gard are the first to go inside the spaceship. Gard discovers many dead Tommyknockers inside the ship and believes that they were violently fighting each other over the ship's controls.

Bobbi has fully changed under the alien influence and views Gard as a threat to the ship. During the confrontation, she forces him to swallow a lethal dose of Valium at gunpoint to eliminate him.  Gard blocks her from reading his mind, pulls his own hidden gun, and shoots Bobbi. During their struggle, Bobbi's modified radio device explodes and sets her ablaze. Before Bobbi dies, she telepathically screams and alerts the residents. The other residents rush to Bobbi's farm in an attempt to prevent Gard from doing anything to the ship. Gard, who has broken his ankle, is taking salt water to force himself to vomit out the pills. Gard goes to the shed. Ev helps Gard escape into the woods in exchange for saving David Brown from Altair 4. Gard sets fire to the shed, mercy killing Ev, Anne, and Peter.

Gard avoids the pursuit of the residents and goes to the buried spacecraft. With his remaining strength, he telepathically launches the ship into space and triggers a massive fire that sweeps through the woods and engulfs Bobbi's property. The departure of the ship also causes the deaths of nearly all the transformed residents of Haven who were dependent on the alien radiation.

## Personal Thoughts
- The pace is very slow at the beginning of the story. There was a lot of rambling for the build-up, which made it difficult to see where the story was heading. I wonder whether King's drug influence when writing this book affected the quality of the story.
- It's very difficult to get intrigued to continue reading the book until the end. It's a struggle to follow the storyline.
- Once you are able to follow the story, there are some interesting parts, but you have to bear with the ramblings. Somehow, I wonder if the ramblings are depicting Gard, an alcoholic who is struggling with his life while Bobbi's sanity is fading due to the effect of the spaceship.
- The second part is getting better as the pace is faster and the story switches to new characters, telling the impacts or unusual changes happening to other residents.
- The overall story is interesting, but I find the story difficult to follow.
- The first part is bad, the second part is good, and the third part is better.
- Good story, bad writing.
