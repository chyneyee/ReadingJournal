### Case 053 - Mystery Writer Case
#### File 182 - The Mystery Writer Vanishes  / The Writer that Disappeared
Kogoro, Ran, and Conan are discussing who is the culprit of an episode of Detective Samonji while having dinner. Kogoro is surprised that the culprit's identity is different from the previous time. Ran replies that the series is always a remake and the culprit is different every time. He recalls Ai telling him that he needs to remain hidden behind Kogoro's business, as it may eventually lead him to a case involving the Black Organisation. Kogoro questions why the TV series is still ongoing despite the Detective Samonji novel having ended quite some time ago. Ran replies that the original author, Nintaro Shinmei, has begun writing new volumes again. Their conversation is interrupted by the ringing doorbell. Ran wonders whether it is a client, but Kogoro tells Ran to ask the client to return another time. The client gives her business card to Kogoro and introduces herself as Kaori Shinmei, daughter of Nintaro Shinmei. She tells them that both of her parents have been missing from their home for the past two months. They disappeared the week before the new series started. They left a note saying that they would be out for a while. Their friends and family have no idea where they are. The strange thing is Nintaro's installments are faxed to his editor every Saturday at midnight sharp, and there is no sender's number on it. The police ask her not to worry since there's no message from a kidnapper and the book is published on schedule. Kogoro suggests heading to the place where the faxes are sent.

At the publishing company, the editor is waiting for Nintaro's new chapter, which will be sent soon. He also confirms that Nintaro's writing and signature were in the draft. Kogoro asks the editor whether there was anything unusual with Nintaro when they had meetings. The editor replies that he didn't notice anything strange before they started the series, although he feels odd that Nintaro decided to restart it. He tells them that he had tried to get Nintaro to restart the series before, but Nintaro was always against the idea to revive the character. Suddenly, Nintaro asks for a space in the magazine for a new Samonji series two months ago. Besides that, he noticed the odd dedication on the first draft – "To all detectives across the nation, if one wishes to surpass me, one must first find the truth hidden in the mystery." As a result, the publishing company was swarmed with letters and calls from the fans to solve the puzzle. He recalls that they even received a lunatic call, threatening to burn the place if they did not continue the story. The editor says that Nintaro inserts himself into the story in the newest drafts. Nintaro plays the role as a struggling author in France, and Samonji calls him to get some advice for a case. Conan asks for the drafts and notices that in one instance, twelve is written in its kanji form (十二), while in another instance, one is written in the western-style number (1). The editor wanted to make them consistent and also had promised Nintaro that they would not change a single character. 

When the clock strikes midnight, the publisher receives the newest drafts via the fax machine. Conan grabs the draft and notices that Nintaro's signature is the same from chapters six through eight, while it has variations from chapter one until six. Conan notes that the discrepancy probably indicates that Nintaro may already be dead. As Kogoro requests to call for police, Conan notes that the key to Nintaro's fate may lie hidden within the pages of the drafts.

#### File 183 - Half of the Top / Climax of 1/2
Inspector Megure and Wataru begin to examine the contents of each draft which involved Nintaro's appearance. They figure out that each instance involves the character's presence in France. Initially, Conan fails to decode any messages hidden behind the mixture of words in the character's dialogues. However, he cracks the code after hearing the working title of the piece. Suddenly, the office receives a phone call from an individual claiming that he has solved Nintaro's challenge. The caller is revealed to be Heiji, and he explains to Megure that the dialogues have hidden messages. The title "Climax of 1/2" refers to the character of each top of the line, which is half of a whole character. The first characters include 力, 目, 十, 1, C, 一, く, 他, ノ, and 1. After combining the characters, it becomes:

* 目 + 力 = 助

* 1 + 十 = け

* 一 + C = て

* く (omit 他 as it means "else")

* 1 + ノ = レ

The result of putting together forms a sentence, "助けてくレ", which means "please help me". Heiji suspects that the captor is either a crazy fan or someone who wants to collect the royalties. Megure asks Wataru to check all suspicious fan letters, leaving Heiji hanging on the phone. Conan takes the phone, and Heiji is excited to hear his voice. He invites Conan to come to Osaka to enjoy the local cuisine, leading Conan to threaten to hang up. Back to the code, Conan tells Heiji that his method does not work on the other lines of dialogue. Heiji admits that there must be some other missing piece but hangs up the call after deciding to leave everything to Conan to solve the remaining puzzle. 

Conan struggles to piece together the culprit's motive. He then overhears two editors discussing a spelling mistake, having written "I'umour" instead of "Humour". Their conversation leads Conan to make a breakthrough but wonders if there is little time left to act.

### Case 054 - Stabbed Wallet Case
#### File 185 - Gourmet City / The Living City
Director General of the Osaka Prefectural Police Heizo Hattori is discussing the details of two horrific murders with Chief Criminal Investigator Ginshiro Toyama. Both victims have no connections but share the same modus operandi (MO): both victims were stabbed with a knife through a wallet in the chest pocket and right into the heart. Director Hattori believes it's an act of a serial killer instead of a copycat because the MO remained undisclosed to the media.

Meanwhile, Heiji is excited to give Conan, Ran, and Kogoro a guided tour of various places in Osaka town. Conan asks the reason for his invitation to the town before commenting on the fragility of life. Heiji then recalls his dream about Conan being stabbed when Conan tried to catch the criminal but later says that it was a joke. The group is then joined by Yusuke Sakata, a detective from the Investigation Unit, who was arranged by Heizo to facilitate the group's tour of the city. Yusuke drives the group for lunch with the newest police car in the fleet. Heiji is proud of using the police car to avoid traffic, but Ran is embarrassed when the passerby treats them as criminals. 

Heiji then takes the trio to one of his favourite udon shops, where the owner wittily asks whether Ran is his new girlfriend. As the two deny such a relation, Ran suddenly feels a vicious glare upon her but notices no one. She continues to feel watched even when they are in a different restaurant after that. The group later reaches an okonomiyaki shop. Heiji briefly leaves the group to call his mother, and a teenage girl immediately takes his vacant seat. The girl suddenly directs the duo (Ran and Conan) with the question, "You're Kudo, right?" The girl then adds that Heiji has told her everything, leading Conan into a panic mode. Then, the girl directs her anger at Ran instead of Conan, misunderstanding that Kudo is a girl who tries to seduce Heiji. She asserts that she and Heiji share a long-standing bond, and their friendship is connected by a "steel chain". Heiji then returns and expresses his confusion, calling the girl "Kazuha". He proceeds to clear up the confusion, telling Kazuha that Kudo is a guy. Kazuha is embarrassed, and Heiji continues to add that Ran is Kudo's girl. Ran denies the relationship, leading Kazuha to have doubts about Heiji's words, and Heiji says that Ran is shy to admit it. Ran asks what Kazuha meant by "steel chain", and Heiji explains that the two played with his father's handcuffs when they were kids and accidentally cuffed themselves together. Ran makes a guess that they're dating, but Kazuha denies it and claims that she acts like a sister figure to him. Heiji clarifies that they're childhood friends and their fathers are colleagues and best friends. Then, Heiji asks Kazuha what she is doing there, and she replies that she has been monitoring a girl from Tokyo to ensure the girl doesn't mess up with him. Ran internally understands that Kazuha was the one who kept watching her all this time.

After the meal, the group boards the police car with Kazuha tagging along. The group then notices a crowd of people around their car while looking upwards and pointing up. All of a sudden, a man crashes down onto the car's bonnet, a knife through a wallet and into his heart.

#### File 187 -  Inside the Wallet / In The Wallet...

### Case 055 - Stadium Threatening Case 
#### File 189 - The Targeted Ball
On January 1st, the Detective Boys attend the Emperor's Cup Finals match between the Tokyo Spirits and Big Osaka. Hideo Akagi scores a perfect header into Osaka's goal, sending the crowd into a frenzy. Genta and Mitsuhiko are in awe of Hideo's skill, believing him fit to join Samurai Blue in the FIFA World Cup had he not been injured. Conan feels disbelief with his friends' ignorance and illustrates the significance of the World Cup. Ai teases Conan about being a kid when it comes to football while she reads a magazine at her seat. Conan feels strange with Ai's cool appearance, and she replies that her purpose in attending the match is to keep him company. The syndicate knows how she looked like a kid, and thus she cannot risk being televised by a stadium camera.  Conan then takes her sunglasses and places his hat on her head, confidently assuring her that she is now safe with the disguise. Conan pulls her to the side rail, asking her to temporarily forget the organisation and enjoy the match. Ai quietly joins her companions but instead focuses on Conan, internally reflecting on how he has unknowingly implicated himself in the Black Organisation's half-century-long top-secret project.

Meanwhile, a man named Kaneko swiftly directs the game broadcast from a Nichiuri TV truck outside the stadium. He then receives a phone call from an unknown caller who instructs him to focus on the Detective Boys sitting in the front row. Specifically, the caller asks the director to focus his attention on Ai. 

Back at the Detective Boys, Conan turns to Ai and remarks on her calmness. He tells her that when he first found himself in a first-grade classroom, he felt embarrassed with the kids, but Ai was able to adapt well. The girl smirks, saying Conan is the reason for her calmness, and it's because of the presence of another person sharing the same burden. Conan is taken aback. He then asks her age, to which she jokingly replies "eighty-four". But before she can reveal the truth, a sudden gust of wind blows her hat away and onto the pitch besides a soccer ball that oddly deflates. Curious, Conan jumps over the rail and inspects the ball. He notices two holes in the ball and sees an object lodged in the grass besides it. He uses a switchblade to dig out the object and discovers a 7.62 mm bullet.

Sometime later, Kaneko informs Inspector Megure that someone shot a gun in the stadium. He watched the ball on the monitor by following the instructions from the man on the phone. Then the ball suddenly bounced up and deflated. He believes that someone shot the ball. Megure remains sceptical but is soon convinced after Conan appears, showing him the bullet he discovered in the ground. Conan suspects the gunman uses a Tokarev with a silencer. Hearing the news, Megure orders the immediate cancellation of the match, but Kaneko refuses, saying that the caller had said he will begin randomly shooting spectators if they notice any attempt at evacuation. Megure asks what the man wants, and Kaneko replies that the culprit wants ¥50,000,000 in a bag before halftime. Megure gets a confirmation from Kaneko that the caller was demanding the money from his company, Nichiuri Broadcasting Station. Megure deduces that the person either holds a grudge against the station or is a terrorist.

Megure sends his men to monitor the crowd as he plans to apprehend every individual who is on their phone when the next call comes. Kaneko then notices Ai and realises she is a girl, noting that the culprit referred to her as a boy. Hearing this, Conan tells Megure to cease his orders, explaining that since the shooter must have fired at the ball from close proximity to the Detective Boys, there is no way they would have mistaken Ai for a boy. Therefore, Kaneko's caller must have been a different person who observed Ai from the opposite side of the stadium. With the information that there are at least two culprits, Megure changes his orders, and it's a race of time to find two people among 56,000 fans in the stadium.

<br>

#### File 190 - 56,000 Hostages / The 56 Thousand Hostages
Megure updates his men with news of a second culprit and asks them to be alert as he determines to catch the criminals when the next call comes. While the Detective Boys consider how the caller could identify Ai's  physical attributes from across the stadium, Kaneko receives the call. Megure notifies his men to pay attention to the crowd for anyone using the phone and asks Kaneko to take his time to talk to the culprit. After confirming Kaneko has prepared the money, the caller demands him to bring it to Gate 18 during the break when the first half ends. Without any further words, the culprit hangs up. Megure's men confirm eight individuals who were on their phones during the duration of the call. Megure orders his men to continue monitoring the eight suspects. Before he can leave, he notices his phone is missing and in the hands of Conan. Conan promptly returns the device, and Megure uses the phone to order several officers to Gate 18. When Ai asks what Conan did with the device, he tells her that he had attached his microphone to it. Conan then asks Ai where their friends are, to which she replies that they have re-entered the stadium.

Inside the stadium, the three Detective Boys communicate with each other via their detective badges to find the culprit. Ayumi receives an incoming call from Conan, and he reprimands her for putting themselves in danger. Yet, the kids are determined to catch the criminal, much to Conan's annoyance.

At Gate 18, several officers observe a lone man approach the bag of money. Assistant Inspector Miwako Sato springs into action, but the officers fail to find a gun on the culprit. They then receive a message over the culprit's phone. The man's accomplice urges them to let his partner go, threatening to random shoot the spectators in the stadium. 

Since Kaneko violates the agreement by contacting the police, the culprits increase their asking price to ¥1,000,000,000, giving Nichiuri TV until the end of the game to ready the additional sum. Megure then turns to his phone and asks if any of the eight suspects are currently on their phones, to which none of his men reply in the affirmative. The culprit provokes Megure, telling him to withdraw his men in the stadium, as he knows who they are. While the culprit lists each officer's physical description and location, Conan takes the opportunity to contact the Detective Boys and asks if they have visuals on an individual nearby who is simultaneously using binoculars on the crowd and is also on their phone. Ayumi locates one such individual, prompting Genta and Mitsuhiko to ignore Conan's orders to remain still and tackle the suspect to the ground. However, the group finds that the man is instead an undercover officer, Wataru Takagi. 

With their leads gone, the Detective Boys discuss their predicament, with Ai noting that ¥1,000,000,000 is too large of a sum to be ready in forty-five minutes. Conan agrees, deducing that the culprits had the intention to murder someone at the game from the beginning.
