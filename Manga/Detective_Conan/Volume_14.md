# Detective Conan Volume 14 - The Magical Suicide

## Summary

### Case 037 - Magician's Suicide Case 
#### File 131 - Found the Picture! / That Old Photo!!
Nanae Tsukumo's husband, Motoyasu Tsukumo, who had died the previous week, was believed to have committed suicide. She found her husband's lifeless body on the ground when she came back home one day last week. The police said that the husband took a poison called "Aconite", a type of toxin that numbs the nerves. When she discovered the body, she was with her husband's three apprentices. Both she and her husband are magicians, and Motoyasu was internationally famous. Ran remembers the name because the family went to Motoyasu's magic show a long time ago and looks for the photo. When Ran finds the picture, she discovers Conan has a striking resemblance to Shinichi.

The three apprentices are the following:- 
* Kazumi Sanada, the star of the magician's troupe who is likely to be the successor.
* Mako Miyoshi, a top-rated female magician with first-rate technique but headstrong.
* Yuji Momochi, a promising newcomer.

On the day of Motoyasu's death, the three apprentices were with Nanae at Beika Hotel, performing magic at a dinner show. There was no trace of anything on the victim's body or being tied up. The house was locked, and there were no signs of forced entry. Nanae doesn't believe that it's a suicide because he already bought a present for their daughter's birthday tomorrow. Nanae also found the cards of the ace and jack of spades stuck together on the desk in the basement. Conan suggests taking a look at Nanae's house, which Kogoro initially refuses until Ran convinces him about Conan's knowledge of unusual facts. When they leave the house, the photo album drops to the floor, and it shows the photo of Ran with Shinichi at the magic show when they were kids.

<br>

#### File 132 - The Telephone's Number / Numbers on the Phone
When they arrive at Nanae's mansion, they encounter Yuji and Mako. Mako doesn't believe it's a suicide, but Yuji thinks the playing cards probably were a set-up. Kazumi takes the card from him and rips off the playing cards. Kogoro panics, but Kazumi reveals the real ones are in Kogoro's coat's pocket. Kazumi asks Kogoro to leave to avoid his reputation being tarnished, leading Kogoro to believe that Kazumi is unaffected by the death. Mako disagrees when she finds different patterns of the cards on the floor, revealing Kazumi is a perfectionist and rarely makes such a mistake. Nanae comments that Kazumi takes the death hard because he treats her husband like his father. Motoyasu's daughter, Ayano, suddenly appears and recognises Conan. Conan remembers her in a different class from his school. She asks Conan to go shopping with her for her birthday party tomorrow, but Conan wants to stay. Ran tells Ayano that Conan is busy, and Nanae tells Ayano that she can't go with her as well. Ayano is upset and calls everyone a liar until Mako offers to bring her to go shop for her dress. 

They go to the room in the basement where Motoyasu's body was found. They see a row of framed photos of the husband's apprentices. Conan finds there's one photo that is not dusty compared to the rest. Nanae tells them that the photo is of Kinoshita, who died young while practising a trick fourteen years ago and was her husband's favourite apprentice. Kogoro believes Motoyasu's death is a suicide because the victim didn't leave the room to ask for help after being poisoned. Conan disagrees because the outline of the body on the floor shows that the victim was lying in a composed manner, which shouldn't happen to a person who got poisoned. After Nanae reveals that the husband's speciality trick was manipulating inanimate objects with invisible strings, Kogoro believes that the victim's thumbs probably had been tied together with the rings and poisoned before the apprentices left. The strings were cut off in the confusion after they discovered the body. Nanae later reveals that the phone was buried in the pile of cards. Conan checks the phone under the pile of cards and sees the symbol of the redial button. The symbol looks the same as the cards of the ace and jack of spades stuck together. When they press the redial button, the number is no longer in service. Conan jots down the number and tries to decode the string of numbers that appears on the phone display.

<br>

#### File 133 - The Case is Just Beginning? / The Case Has Only Just Begun!
Conan's pencil is out of lead, and Nanae finds all the pens used in tricks are missing from the drawer. Since all the pens are missing, Conan believes the victim would try to leave some clues when he was shut in the room the whole time. Kogoro thinks that the victim could have called for help if he had used the phone, but Conan points out the possibility that the phone was unplugged. This leads Kogoro to believe the suspect unplugged the phone before shutting the victim in the room, and the victim left a message using the redial button. The two cards are a hint pointing to the redial button. The victim piled the cards to hide the phone to prevent anyone from using it and to mitigate the possibility that the murderer might destroy the numbers that he inputted using the redial button.

Kogoro decodes the string of numbers with Conan's help, and the decoded message is 'DC Mako'. They don't understand what the word "DC" stands for until Ran mentions that it stands for "Da Capo" on musical scores, which means "go back to the beginning and repeat". Both Kogoro and Conan are shocked with the revelation, and Kogoro asks Nanae to contact Mako as soon as possible. Kogoro reveals that Mako intends to kill Ayano, as the message indicates that "Mako will kill once again". Kogoro suggests getting the other two apprentices to help search for Mako and Ayano. Nanae doesn't understand why Mako killed her husband, and Conan thinks it's related to Yoshiro Kinoshita. This leads Kazumi to reveal that he saw Mako hugging the picture to her chest in the basement on one late night. Kazumi feels guilty of not noticing Mako cutting the strings off the victim's rings and plugging the phone cord back in front of him when they discovered Motoyasu's body in the basement. Mako suddenly appears, holding Ayano, who is sleeping, and hands her over to Nanae. Mako tells them that she planned to make Ayano sleep forever like Mako's brother, Yoshiro.

Mako reveals that Yoshiro Kinoshita was her brother who was fourteen years older than her. He was a genius magician and already very successful when he was twenty. She lost both of her parents when she was young, and her brother was the only family she had. She tells them that Motoyasu murdered her brother. She tells them that she saw Motoyasu fiddling with the handcuffs fourteen years ago when she was at the back stage. Later, when her brother practised the trick, he drowned to death underwater after failing to unlock the handcuffs. On the day of Motoyasu's death, Mako threatened to kill his daughter. Motoyasu killed Yoshiro for his reputation and asked for forgiveness from Mako. He begged Mako to spare Ayano's life. Mako initially planned to kill Ayano but couldn't do so because it's not Ayano's fault. Mako laughs and is full of pride because she easily tricked the greatest magician, Motoyasu. Motoyasu went to the basement with her because she told him that she wanted to show him the new tricks. He let her loop the strings around his rings and even swallowed the poisoned capsule. Kazumi disagrees with Mako and reveals that Motoyasu was standing besides him when Kazumi saw Mako was hugging her brother's photo in the basement. Motoyasu didn't want Kazumi to tell anyone about it. Kazumi says that Mako has successfully tricked the police and the audience. Mako says that she doesn't have the intention to go on stage when her trick is revealed and decides to turn herself in. She feels bad that Ayano has to suffer the same fate as her.

Kogoro, Ran, and Conan return home after accompanying Mako to the police station. Ran forgets to buy anything to prepare for the late dinner and brings Conan along to the convenience store. Instead of going to the convenience store, Ran brings Conan back to Shinichi's home. Ran believes Conan is Shinichi, but Conan denies it and argues that he's just a kid. Ran thinks Agasa made some potions that turned him into a kid. Conan asks for proof.

### Case 038 - [Suspicious Uncle Case](https://www.detectiveconanworld.com/wiki/Volume_14#Suspicious_Uncle_Case)
#### File 134 - Question in Dead Leaves / Interrogation Amidst Falling Leaves
Ran throws away Conan's glasses and talks about his resemblance to Shinichi. She asks Conan to admit that he's Shinichi, and suddenly, Shinichi's mother, Yukiko, appears and asks why they're in front of the house at such a late hour. She tells Ran that she just came back from Los Angeles while Conan hides behind Ran. Ran feels this is the right timing to ask for confirmation from Yukiko, and Yukiko recognises the little boy is Conan. Yukiko claims Conan is her grandfather's older brother's daughter's cousin's uncle's grandson. Ran feels confused because Conan is Agasa's relative, and Yukiko claims that Agasa and she are distantly related. Ran feels Shinichi and Conan are too alike, but Yukiko feels that Conan is much better behaved and asks Conan if his mother's name is Fumiyo for confirmation. Ran says that it's hard to believe that a first grader has such an outstanding deduction skill. Yukiko comments that maybe Shinichi taught Conan a lot because Conan and Shinichi used to hang out together. Ran still has doubts, while Conan hides behind Yukiko and is afraid of Ran. Yukiko says that Shinichi definitely wouldn't act like this, leading Ran to apologise to Conan and promise to cook a delicious hamburger for him. Yukiko wants Conan to stay with her a few days since she has a few days off and hasn't seen him for quite some time.

The next day, Yukiko rides her motorcycle with Conan inside her clothes to Gunma Prefecture. She introduces Conan as her younger son who was born in Los Angeles to her childhood friend, Hiromi Yabuuchi. Hiromi asks Yukiko's help to investigate her uncle, Yoshifusa, who moved to Brazil for thirty years but came back three days ago. His friend, Carlos, came back with him. Hiromi feels there's something strange about his uncle. Her parents passed away and only left Yukiko and her, who had interacted with Yoshifusa before. Yukiko wonders why Hiromi is suspicious of her uncle, and she mentions that it's related to the inheritance. Hiromi's husband, Hidekazu, interjects the conversation and says that the uncle might be an imposter who might grab his father-in-law's estate. He explains further that the father-in-law has left a significant estate, and the instructions were not to leave any money to anyone absent at the reading of the will. When Yukiko asks when the reading of the will is, Hiromi's younger brother, Yoshiyuki, replies that the reading of the will is tomorrow at 10 pm and urges her to get the truth about the impostor. His wife, Keiko, believes that their share will get smaller if the impostor gets part of the inheritance. Yoshichika's second wife, Machiko, interjects and says that maybe they don't get anything since her late husband cares about his younger brother than anyone else.

Then, they try to look for the photo of Yoshifusa around the house. When Conan finds an old photo, Hiromi and Yukiko suddenly remember that they cheered for him at the town's baseball tournament and he injured his leg that day. He had to get some stitches at the hospital, leading Conan to point out the existence of a scar on his leg. Later, Hiromi serves some tea to Yoshifusa, and she pretends she accidentally spills the tea to check the scar on Yoshifusa's leg. Hiromi checks Yoshifusa's scar on his left leg, and Yoshifusa shows her the scar on his right leg. Yoshifusa mentions that his older brother was right when he told Yoshifusa about his bad children. Yoshifusa reveals that Carlos is his bodyguard because he received a letter of a death threat if he comes back for the inheritance. 

A mysterious man appears in front of the house.

<br>

#### File 135 - In Mother's Chest!? / In Mom's Chest!?
Yoshifusa says that he received the letter a month ago in Brazil. He has no interest in the estate but is curious about the letter. Conan suddenly asks Yoshifusa to teach him how to write "Happy New Year" in Kanji, and the rest are surprised to see that Yoshifusa is left-handed. They still have doubts about Yoshifusa's identity, but Conan confirms that he is real because the old photo indicates he's left-handed, and an old New Year's greeting card has the same handwriting as Yoshifusa's handwriting.

In the backyard of the house, there is a well with the fence around it. Hiromi tells Yukiko and Conan that they found her mother dead in the well fifteen years ago. Yoshiyuki says that the funeral was tough because his mother's older brother accused one of the family members of pushing his sister into the well. Hiromi explains that her mother probably tried to climb up the camellia tree besides the well to get the flower and slipped her foot. She thinks that the brother never accepted the explanation, leading Yoshiyuki to wonder whether the brother will get a share of the inheritance. Hidekazu doesn't believe that he has the legal right, but Machiko thinks that it's possible since her late husband was always concerned about him. After giving her opinion, Machiko says that she's going to a friend's wedding reception and leave the rest to Hiromi. Yoshiyuki criticises his stepmother, who acts like part of the family, but she was only married to the family five years ago for the estate. Conan sees a mysterious figure peeking through at the back gate, but Yoshiyuki and Hidekazu can't find anyone outside of the house. Machiko gives a slight grin behind the window. 

During the hotpot dinner, Machiko calls and tells Hiromi that she had stacked more firewood in the morning for the bath. During the phone call, Hiromi hears another man tell Machiko to drink. When Hiromi prepares the firewood for the hot bath, Conan wonders whether Carlos takes a bath together with Yoshifusa. Hiromi says that his uncle has been taking hot baths by himself and leaves Carlos in the house. Conan finds it strange that Yoshifusa baths alone without his bodyguard. Conan later figures out that Carlos watches everyone in the house while Yoshifusa bathes. 

Around 9 pm, Machiko calls and says that she won't come back until after eleven. She asks Hiromi to keep the bath hot until then. Yukiko goes next to take her bath and finishes around 10 pm. Yukiko comments that the water is cooling down when Hiromi asks her about the water temperature. Hiromi thinks it's better that she put more wood in the furnace to keep the water hot. After a while, the group in the house hears Hiromi's scream. Hiromi claims that an unknown person wearing sunglasses slipped out the rear gate. Conan discovers a bucket being pulled up high at the well. Yoshiyuki and Hidekazu pull up the rope on the well, and everyone is shocked to see Machiko's body appear. Conan finds Machiko has stab wounds in the chest and a camelia flower in her pocket. Everyone thinks Hiromi's mother's older brother is the killer.

<br>

#### File 136 - Conan's Grin... / Conan's Smile
Hiromi tells Detective Misao Yamamura that Machiko was at a hotel across the street from the train station. When Machiko came home, it should have been after 10 pm, and all of them were in the same room together except Yukiko, who was taking a bath. Misao accuses Yukiko of being the murderer, but Yukiko claims that she only met Machiko for the first time that day. Misao later recognises Yukiko as the actress from a TV series that inspired him to become a police detective. He feels Yukiko is suspicious because she used to act as a police officer. Hiromi tells Misao that there was a strange man lurking around. Yoshiyuki claims that man is his mother's older brother. When Conan shows Misao that the camellia flower was found in the victim's pocket, Misao is scared and reveals that it is his first time on the crime scene because others fall sick. Misao plans to find the taxi that took the victim home from the hotel, but Hiromi says that the victim drove herself to the hotel. When they go to the yard, the car is not there, and a police officer informs Misao that they found a car in the woods behind the house. In the woods, Misao speculates that Machiko probably left the car because it was out of fuel or had an engine problem. Yukiko and Conan find the car's engine is fine and the key in the ignition. The petrol tank is not empty, and there are no flat tyres. They find an empty cassette case and a phone in the glove compartment. The family members have to sleep on the floor in the same room with Misao. When Conan returns to the room, he sees the storage room's door is open and checks inside with Yukiko following behind him. The mysterious man is smoking behind one of the wooden poles at the top area without Conan's knowledge.

The next day, a police officer informs Misao that they found the murder weapon in the well. The knife is wrapped with a women's rain poncho. Meanwhile, a lawyer wants to come into the house to read the will, but Misao postpones the reading until the police complete the investigation. Yoshiyuki is not happy with the delay, and Yoshifusa criticises him for being greedy. They end up in a fight, but Yoshifusa tackles Yoshiyuki with his Brazilian jujitsu. Conan wonders why Yoshifusa still hires Carlos as his bodyguard since Yoshifusa is so skilful at self-defence. 

The will reading takes place at night in the presence of the police. Conan hears a strange sound coming from the cabinet when the lawyer plays a cassette recorded by Yoshichika himself. There is no sound when the tape is playing and the lawyer accidentally presses the record button. Yoshifusa tries to turn it off before the tape gets erased but accidentally spills the tea. During the chaos, Conan finds out that there is a weapon pointing in the direction of Yoshifusa, and Conan shouts to him to get down. Yoshifusa successfully avoids the arrow, and Yoshiyuki finds a crossbow with a clock inside the cabinet. While everyone is thinking who would set up the devices, Conan is grinning.

#### File 137 - Yukiko's Grin... / Vivian's Smile
While everyone is guessing that the late mother's older brother is the suspect, Conan shows them the holes on the tatami mat, indicating that the suspect has practised several times to hit the right spot. The arrow's direction is targeted to Yoshifusa because the seat was reserved for the most senior person. They get back to the will reading after the lawyer figures out that he plays the wrong side of the tape. Before they can hear the will, a police officer informs Misao that they discovered a suspicious cassette tape near the furnace that heats the bath. The police also tell Misao that the forensics only found the victim's blood, but the prints on the knife were of an ordinary grip. The hotel confirms the victim was there before her death, but nobody knows when she left.

#### File 138 - One More Person... / The Mystery Man

<details>

Conan points out that if the killer were waiting for Machiko, he would attack Machiko in the woods where she parked the car since it's more hidden. Besides, the man wouldn't have tied the body to the rope in the well for it to be easier to find. Once the body is found, the police will come in, and it's not easy for a stranger to leave the property freely. Besides, Machiko has nothing to do with the incident 15 years ago, and it shouldn't be the mother's older brother. As for the man in sunglasses who is lurking around, Conan assumes that he's probably a detective hired to investigate whether Yoshifusa is really their uncle and remains in the area even after the arrival of the police. 

Machiko made her first phone call from the party after 8 pm. She left the party after that in a hurry. Then, she parked the car in the woods just after 9 pm. She made her second call while playing the recording of the hubbub of the party in the background. She wanted to create a false alibi to murder someone but ended up being killed by her intended target. The rain parka found on the well was worn by her to avoid blood splattering onto her clothes. She placed the camellia flower in her pocket because she planned to leave the crime scene to make it look like the murder was related to the incident 15 years ago. The tape was dropped on the furnace when she went to kill her victim, who was bathing at the time, or during the time the killer carried her body to the well. In any case, she intended to burn the tape in the furnace after completing her crime. Machiko's intended victim was someone who liked to bath every day, and the person is Yoshifusa. The prints on the knife were a forward-facing grip, and there's a technique in Brazilian jujitsu where you twist around the hand of a knife-wielding assailant, making the assailant stab him or herself. Yoshifusa received two threatening letters and mentioned someone still targeting him after the arrow incident. Yoshifusa tied Machiko's body in the well to give a clear warning to the other person who threatened him, or probably it was the first thing that came to his mind. 

When Conan finishes his deduction and asks Yoshifusa to confess, a stranger's voice says that the deductions are incomplete. The mysterious man who is in sunglasses and wearing a scarf comes into the room and asks the lawyer to play the tape of the will. According to the tape, the estate is divided equally among six people – Machiko, Hiromi, Hidekazu, Yoshiyuki, Keiko, and Carlos. The elderly gentleman is not Hiromi's uncle but Carlos's bodyguard. Carlos is the son born to the real Yoshifusa and a Brazilian woman. The mysterious man later reveals that he is Yusaku, Conan's father. 

Yusaku explains the entire story after reading a pile of letters hidden behind a secret door in the storage room. Yoshifusa and the old man were friends when they worked on a plantation in Brazil. Ten years ago, a tornado injured the man's right leg, and Yoshifusa lost his left hand. Since that day, the old man has written the uncle's letters for him. He keeps the secret that Carlos was Yoshifusa's son to avoid being the target of the threats. He impersonated Yoshifusa and left Carlos with a group of people, which is safer when he goes to bath. He only intended to catch the people threatening him but ended up killing one of them. Although it's self-defence, he would probably have to go with the police. He couldn't leave Carlos alone while another threatening person is still out there. So, he hid the body and planned to see whether he could talk to the detective. Unfortunately, the detective is unreliable and inexperienced, and he has to remain silent until the reading of the will. Yusaku asks the old man whether he misses anything and greets him as Mr Hickson Tanaka, the second-generation Japanese Brazilian. Yusaku even offers to introduce a good investigator for him to help out with the case.

Two hours later, Inspector Megure arrived. Hickson and Keiko were taken to the Gunma Prefecture Police. Carlos looks sad, and he didn't understand much Japanese. Yusaku tells him that Hickson will be coming back. In order to protect Carlos, Hickson didn't tell him about the inheritance or that the house was his father's family home.

The next day, Yusaku tells Conan that Agasa gave him a receiver that picks sound through Conan's bow tie, which helps him with the deductions. Yusaku knew the imposter before the letters because Hickson's injury was on his right leg. Yoshifusa was a left-handed batter, and he stretches his left leg out to the base. Then, Yukiko appears and bickers at Yusaku. She is still angry with him after what happened in Los Angeles but accepts Yusaku's invitation to go home. When they drop Conan at Ran's home, Yusaku warns him to be careful because of Ran's unusual smile the other day, and Yusaku advises Conan not to underestimate a woman. When Conan arrives home, Conan is scared when Ran tells him that Agasa has told her everything. She scolds Conan for not informing her when he went for an overnight trip.

#### File 139 - An Unexpected Crew / A Strange Gathering
Sonoko, Ran, and Conan go to ski during the winter and bump into their former elementary school's teacher, Akiko Yonehara. At the cafe, she introduces her colleagues – Kohei Shimoda, Ryuichi Sakai, and Minori Nakamura. Sonoko, Ran, and Conan join the teachers returning to the rental cabin, and the teachers assume that their colleague is waiting for them there. When they are on their way to the cabin, they ask for directions from a local resident. The local resident warns them they better not go to the cabin because a blizzard is expected to hit the area by evening. After the group of teachers find out that none of them rented the cabin, they decide to go ahead and give a surprise to another colleague who probably was the one who organised the trip. When the group is enjoying the facilities after arriving at the cabin, suddenly there's a knock on the door. Atsushi Mori introduces himself as a newspaper reporter that they knew three years ago and tells them that he received a tip that something will happen that night. He walks in and proceeds to go to one of the rooms. 

Sonoko helps Akiko to put the sheets on the beds. Suddenly, Sonoko hears a loud dropping sound, and she goes to the next room to check on Akiko. She screams after finding Akiko is lying on the floor and a mysterious figure is hiding behind the door without her knowing it. She sees the word "MI" on Akiko's hand, and the mysterious figure puts a cloth on her mouth. Sonoko passes out, and he attempts to strangle her with a rope from behind.

<br>

#### File 140 - The Last Guest
Ran kicks the room's door and tries to wake Sonoko up. Sonoko is sleeping while Akiko regains her consciousness. Akiko tells them that someone gagged her from behind when she was putting on the bedsheet and she eventually lost her consciousness. She struggled before that and ended up getting her head banged against the wall several times. Her hand is written with "MI", and Sonoko's hand has the words "NA" in lipstick. Atsushi speculates that the suspect hints that he will kill everyone and mentions that they hid the truth three years ago. Conan tracks the footprints outside the window which lead to the front door of the lodge. The group of teachers still assumes that it's a prank from their colleague, but Akiko doesn't think so. Conan realises that Minori's pant legs are wet. Conan asks where she is when he didn't see her in the kitchen just now. She replies that she was outside the kitchen door to check the gas tank.

During dinnertime, the phone rings and the caretaker of the cabin calls to make sure the heater is still working since he hasn't turned it on for a very long time. Conan feels something is not right and decides to do more investigation. He uses the earring-shaped mobile phone to call Dr Agasa to find out what happened to the Haido Elementary School three years ago. Suddenly, the doorbell rings. When Kohei opens the door, he sees Sugiyama and brings him inside. Sugiyama immediately falls at the door.
