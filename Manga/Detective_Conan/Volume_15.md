# Detective Conan Volume 15 - The Frozen Teacher

## Summary

### Case 039 - Ski Lodge Case
#### Gadget Introduced - [Earring Cellphone](https://www.detectiveconanworld.com/wiki/Earring_Cellphone).

<br>

#### File 141 - Gone!? / Missing?! 
Everyone assumes Sugiyama froze to death outside, but Conan deduces that he was murdered. There are rope marks on his neck, and his stiff body indicates that he probably died more than nine hours ago. His hand is written with the words "KO". Kohei suddenly screams, runs away and says that he has nothing to do with it.  Ran wishes Shinichi were there, and Akiko remembers Shinichi as a smart kid. Atsushi says that he plans to do a feature story about Shinichi but hears a rumour about his disappearance after getting involved in the wrong case. Ran wants to tell him that Shinichi is alive, but Conan stops her. Atsushi tells them to keep an eye on Kohei because his instinct tells him that Kohei probably is the next target. 

Agasa tells Conan that three years ago, a girl student from Haido Elementary School was found hanging in the volleyball locker room. The cause of suicide was probably the stress of the entrance exam, and many people blamed the homeroom teacher. The student's name was Minako Mochizuki. Coincidentally, during the call, Ran sees Conan and wonders what he's doing. She takes the phone and hears Agasa calling Shinichi. Ran asks whether Shinichi is with him, and Agasa says that he just went into the bathroom. Suddenly, a scream comes from upstairs. Conan runs upstairs, asking Ran to wait there, and Atsushi appears nearby. Kohei is found lying upside down from the bed with the rope marks on his neck. The window in the room is closed. Conan tries to find the rope used to strangle Kohei but to no avail. He believes one of the three has the rope.

<br>

#### File 142 - The Disappearing Rope / The Missing Murder Weapon
The staircase is the only way down to the first floor. Conan was on the first floor and didn't see anyone flee. He went through the rooms and everyone's belongings but couldn't find the rope. Akiko suggests Atsushi do a body search on Ryuichi, and Ran searches the women. No one has the rope.

At the outside of the lodge, Conan finds a long piece of kite string and a broken rubber band on the end. Conan somehow figures out the tricks of the attack and knows who the attacker is. When he's thinking about how to reveal the killer, Ran finds him, and he drops the earring-shaped cellphone. He remembers that he can transmit his voice from the bow tie to the cell phone. He has no choice and makes a call to Ran when she's picking up the earring-shaped cellphone. Conan uses Shinichi's voice and explains everything through the phone. He asks Ran to tell the truth based on his explanation. Ran wears the earring and repeats what Shinichi says to her.

<br>

#### File 143 - Truth From Tears / The Truth, Through Tears

<details>
  <summary>Spoiler</summary>
  
  With Shinichi talking to Ran through the earring cellphone, Ran explains how the kite string with a rubber band at its end was used to tie Sugiyama in front of the door. The snow, wind, and cold will deteriorate the rubber band, and when it snaps, the body will fall into the doorbell and make it ring. 

  Ran hesitates but has to reveal that the killer is Akiko. She explains that Akiko braided her hair to act as a rope and strangled Kohei with it. Ran tells them that Akiko is wearing a wig, and Conan found a single strand of human hair from the wig on Kohei's finger. Then, Sonoko says that she saw Akiko was lying on the floor when she was attacked. Ran explains that the one who was lying on the floor was actually Sugiyama. Akiko dressed Sugiyama to look like her and hid the body under the bed. She pretended to lie on the floor before Ran came in. Akiko even called Kohei on his cell phone, telling him that she knew who the killer was and that she went to his room to kill him. 

Akiko admits that she's wearing a wig to hide her haircut, which is too short, but the wig proves nothing. Ran insists Akiko is the killer because there is something else on the wig. Kohei's neck was deeply scratched up where he had clawed at the rope. Ran believes some of the blood appears on the wig. Akiko in the end admits that she kills to avenge the death of Minako. She reveals that exactly three years ago, Minako went to her and told her that there were two teachers who were influencing the outcome of the entrance exams. Minako named Sugiyama as one of the teachers, but she kept another teacher secret because she admired him a lot. The next day, Minako hanged herself, and it was treated as suicide. Akiko believes that Sugiyama and another teacher killed her to shut her up. Akiko organised the trip to gather the teachers she knew Minako was a fan of. She used her lipstick to spell out Minako's name on all of the victims' hands to bait the other guilty teacher. Kohei's panic reaction revealed his involvement. Akiko couldn't forgive them for betraying a student who looked up to them and even killing her.

Ran leaves the room and sobs heavily. She sees Conan standing outside the hall, ready to "tell" her that Shinichi is gone; before he can do so, she falls to her knees and hugs him tearfully. After two hours, the blizzard ended and the police arrived. Conan feels he has successfully solved the case but fails in keeping Ran's tears from spilling out.

### Case 040 - Two-Mix Kidnapping Case
#### File 144 - Their Voices Are Alike!? / A Similar Voice?!
Ran and Sonoko come to the Budokan to watch the live concert by the Two Mix duo. The duo consists of Minami Takayama and Shiina Nagano. Conan and the Detective Boys are also at the Budokan playing games. The kids bump into Minami, and before Ayumi recognises her, she quickly takes them somewhere else to hide her identity. Minami treats the kids to a meal in a nearby restaurant. Mitsuhiko asks Minami whether she needs to do a sound check before the show. She says that she couldn't reach her partner, Shiina, because she forgot her cell phone at the office and a man is taking his time on the pay phone in the restaurant. Minami later reveals that things are getting strange recently, such as daily prank calls, and someone ransacked her locker at the office. The prank call leaves the same message every day, asking them not to sing. The harassment started after last Sunday's live radio broadcast of their new song. 

Conan lends Minami the earring cell phone, but before she makes a call, she receives a call from Shiina via the restaurant's phone. On the phone, Shiina tells her that he's in a car in front of the restaurant. Shiina asks Minami to go to him because he has something to show to her.  Someone is threatening Shiina inside the car with a gun. Shiina hears the click of the gun, and he shouts to Minami on the phone not to come over. Minami can't hear his warning and still goes to the car. When she opens the door, a man points a gun at her. The man who was talking on the payphone in the restaurant is standing behind Minami, demanding her to go inside the car. Conan and the Detective Boys call the police. The abductor makes a call and requests for Minami's bag that she left behind at the restaurant. Megure offers to send his man to deliver the bag to the abductor, but the abductor asks for Conan and the Detective Boys to do it.

<br>

#### File 145 - What Do They Want / What They Are After...
Megure has no choice but to send all the four kids to deliver the bag to the abductor. He asks other detectives to investigate the past cases that he handled because the abductor knew his name and used a device to disguise his voice. The abductor calls one of the payphones at the Kudanzaka train station and instructs Conan where to go next. Conan tricks the abductors by asking the caller whether it's fine after he accidentally drops the bag in the ditch earlier. Conan hears one of the abductors asking the other whether the tape is safe. Conan immediately knows that the abductors are after the recording tape. The abductors are waiting for their chance when the kids are not visible to the police. They call Conan on another train station's payphone and instruct him to hand the bag to their intended person in the bathroom without the police seeing it. Conan asks the others about the Two Mix duo's interview, which discussed the lyrics of their new song. He figures out that the abduction is likely connected to a murder case at Shoenji Temple during last New Year's Eve.

The kids board the train just before the doors close except for Ayumi. Two detectives are able to get into the train, and they see the kids throw the bag outside the window. Megure and the rest rush to the track to wait for the abductor to appear to collect the bag. Later, it reveals that Ayumi has swapped places with Conan. Ayumi went into the train with Genta and Mitsuhiko, and the one who missed out was Conan, who dressed up like Ayumi. The abductor asked them to pass the tape to Ayumi and ditch the police. Conan offered to swap the role with Ayumi. The abductor asks Conan, who he thought is Ayumi, to deliver the recording tape at a warehouse. When one of the abductors opens the door, Conan uses his stun gun and shoots a tranquiliser dart. Conan is confident that the police will be coming soon to save the hostages because of his tracking glasses and the badge that can transmit the signal to the Detective Boys. Unfortunately, the abductor caught the Detective Boys when they were loitering around the warehouse. The Detective Boys didn't give the tracking glasses to the police because they wanted to save their favourite idol and get the credit.

#### File 146 - Duet?! / A Duet!?
<details>
  <summary>Spoiler</summary>

The abductor fires his gun on the explosives with the intention to burn all the hostages. Conan reads part of the lyrics from the new song and reveals that the abduction is related to the murder on New Year's Eve last year. The police concluded that the murder was justifiable self-defence, but the truth is that the killing was intentional. The duo assumes the sound of the gunshots was fireworks and included it in the new song's lyrics. The duo also mentioned in the interview that they got the inspiration around the time, day, and location when the gunshots occurred. The abductors heard the demo played on the radio and planned to destroy the tape. 

The abductor reveals himself as Satoshi Miyahara. The victim was one of his robbery gang members eight years ago. He fled overseas but recently came back and extorted money from them. After the revelation, the abductor leaves the warehouse and shuts the door, confining the hostages with the fire. The fire is spreading faster, and the thick smoke causes Conan to almost faint until he sees the earring cellphone on Minami. When the abductors go inside the car, the group calls him and says that they got out easily by calling the neighbourhood police. Satoshi goes back to the warehouse to check whether the group is still there. The group hides behind the door and hits him with the pipe rods. Before they leave, Satoshi wakes up and fires his gun, injuring Shiina's arm. Satoshi plans to kill everyone but fails to do so after Conan kicks an oil can at him.

The police arrive 30 minutes later. The suspects are handcuffed, and the rest rush to the Budokan. The crowd is getting rowdy. While the duo is preparing for the show, Conan sings from the backstage. Unfortunately, despite having the same voice as Minami, he is tone-deaf and causes more anger from the fans who have been waiting outside for more than half an hour. Minami appears on the stage with Conan while holding his hand, and the Detective Boys join them as well.

### Case 041 - Loan Company President Murder Case
#### File 147 - Licking His Finger!? / Finger Licking
Kogoro and Conan are playing mahjong at the Beika mahjong parlour. After Kogoro wins a round of mahjong games with the help of Conan, one of the players decides to go home, causing them to lose one player. Another player suggests calling the owner of the loan shark office across the street. Mitsuhiro Hida, the president, is busy counting money and tells the caller that he will join them after thirty minutes. Ran comes to the mahjong parlour and wants to take Conan home after discovering that Kogoro brought him there. The players invite Ran to play for half an hour, and she keeps winning the game. One of the players realises that Mr Hida is late and decides to call him again. The office's light is still on, but no one answers the phone. The player who called Mr Hida earlier mentions that he heard something strange during their conversation on the phone. Kogoro suggests going to the shark loan office to check on Mr Hida. 

At the front of the loan office, an employee of Hida Financial, Hisayoshi Minamizawa, suddenly appears, wondering why a group of people is in front of the office. After Hisayoshi gets back the cell phone that he left in the office, he brings the group to the president's office. Since there isn't a spare key, Kogoro and others try to break the door. They discover Mr Hida lying on the floor, and Kogoro confirms that Mr Hida is already dead.

The police arrive, and Megure reveals that the cause of the death is suffocation due to potassium cyanide. The estimated time of death is between 7 and 8 p.m. The first caller from the mahjong parlour tells Megure that the victim is still alive at least until 7.30 p.m. The door was locked, and the only key to the room was on the victim's desk. It is a case of murder in a locked room. Conan suggests checking the tape from the security camera located at the entrance. Megure watches the tape and couldn't find any intruders after the employees left and before Kogoro's group came into the office. Takako Fujii and Hiroshi Iino, the other two employees, arrive at the office. When they learn about Mr Hida's death, they are shocked. Hisayoshi mentions the possibility of suicide, but Kogoro strongly disagrees because Mr Hida was planning to play mahjong. 

Mr Hida has a habit of licking his right thumb when he counts money. When Hiroshi admits that he was the one who handed the money to Mr Hida, Kogoro accuses him of being the killer. The forensics officer reveals that the poison on the notes shows up as thumbprints, indicating that the victim already had poison on his thumb when he touched the money. There is also no trace of poison on the cup or in the coffee. The forensics reveals that other places that tested positive with the poison are the inside door knob and the lock above it, and what shows up in those places is also the victim's thumbprint.

<br>

#### File 148 - She Used Magic!? / Some Kind of Magic?!
Kogoro deduces that the victim touched something outside of the room that had poison on it, then came into his room and locked the door. The victim licked his thumb to count the notes and died as a result. 

The three employees are not allowed to touch anything in the office. When the forensic team wants to turn off a computer after accidentally switching it on, Hiroshi panics and takes over to do it. Takako reveals that Hiroshi probably worried that the details of the false accounts that he created for fund embezzlement would appear. Hisayoshi says that the boss already suspected Hiroshi, and Hisayoshi accuses him of killing the boss to hide his crime. Takako reveals that Hisayoshi is a suspect too because the boss wanted Hiroshi to shoulder the loss after a client with a huge loan fled. 

The forensic team concludes that there are no other traces of poison in the office. Megure suggests the three employees show what items they have with them. There are no traces of poison on their belongings. Conan tries to recall every employee's gestures. Conan suddenly remembers something, and he whispers his question to the forensic officer. After getting the intended answer from the forensic officer, Conan gets the confirmation that he wanted. Conan rushes to the bathroom and finds a bottle of bleach with fresh drips near the cap. He figures out who the killer is.

<br>

#### File 149 - A Devil's Calling / The Devil's Summons
Kogoro scolds Conan and asks Ran to take him home. Conan takes the chance and shoots Kogoro with the stun gun. Conan uses his voice-changing bowtie and acts as Kogoro. He asks Ran to return home and get a bottle of iodine from the medicine chest.

<details>
  <summary>Spoiler</summary>

Conan says that the suspect put the poison on the burner's switch. The burner switches are designed for right-handed people, but the suspect applied poison at the left side of the switch. The victim's right thumb touched the left side when he turned the burner off. After the victim turned off the burner, he returned to his room and closed the door, explaining why the poison is only on the inside door knob and the lock above it. 

Conan reveals Takako is the killer. She prepared the whistling kettle on the burner and adjusted the heat so that the water would boil when the boss was in the midst of counting the money. She planned to wipe off the poison the next day, but the body was discovered sooner than expected. She was afraid that the poison would be discovered and snuck into the coffee room to wipe it off when the police weren't around. While Takako reminds them that there is no trace of poison on her handkerchief, Ran returns with the bottle of iodine. Conan asks Megure to put a drop of iodine onto Takako's damp handkerchief, and the spot disappears. Conan reveals that the dampness of the handkerchief is not from sweat or water but from sodium thiosulfate. Sodium thiosulfate is a bleaching agent that is used to get rid of disinfectant stains and also neutralises the poison of potassium cyanide. 

Takako admits that she is the killer. She reveals that her boyfriend borrowed money from the victim but hanged himself after he couldn't return it. She always had a heart problem, and she has no family after her parents died young. Her boyfriend supported her medical expenses, and after she recovered, she found her boyfriend hanged himself in his apartment. There was a loan note from Hida Financial at his feet. She joins the company to take revenge for her boyfriend. She asks how she was found out as the killer, and Conan reveals that she always keeps her left hand in her pocket except at the time when she turns off the burner. Besides, she worried that the poison would go to her since she's a heavy smoker. For that reason, she used her left hand to turn off the burner. She kept her left hand in the pocket all the time to avoid touching something just in case she accidentally touched the poison from the switch.

Three days later, Kogoro goes to the game centre to play mahjong with Conan. Nobody wants to play with him because they don't want to be reminded of the murder. While they're enjoying the game, Ran comes to them. They assume that she is going to scold them, but instead, she takes over their mahjong game and wins it.

</details>

### Case 042 - Bond of Fire Case
#### File 150 - The Blood-Stained Bandage / The Bloody Bandage
The chairman of the Nagato Financial Group, Dosan Nagato, has requested Kogoro to come to his mansion and Ran and Conan to tag along as well. Mr Nagato wants Kogoro to help him to look for his long-lost love, but he couldn't remember what she looked like. He only has some news about her, and his secretary, Miyuki Hyoga, gives them the details of the person. Her name is Kotomi Akagi. She is already married and had a child, but the family disappeared five years after that. Kogoro asks who recommended him to Mr Nagato. Mr Nagato mentions that his friend did it, and his friend, Heizo Hattori, the head of the Osaka Police, appears. Heizo tells them that his son was the one who kept pestering him to get Kogoro. His son, Heiji Hattori, suddenly appears and greets Kogoro, Ran, and Conan. He accidentally calls Conan's real name because he thought it was no longer a secret. Suddenly, the chairman's two daughters come in. The eldest daughter, Nobuko Nagato, voices her unpleasantness when seeing Miyuki while the second daughter, Yasue Nagato, advises her elder sister not to talk in such a bad manner. The chairman introduces Kogoro to the daughters, and Nobuko asks why there is a need for a detective. Miyuki explains the reason, and Nobuko slaps her. Nobuko accuses Miyuki of trying to talk her way to her father so she can replace her mother. Then, a fountain pen drops from Miyuki, and Nobuko criticises her for keeping such a disgusting memento from Miyuki's father or whoever. Before the daughters leave, Nobuko warns Yasue to be careful of Miyuki stealing Mitsuaki.

Mr Nagato decides to make an announcement that night and tells Miyuki that he hopes that the decision is not based on sympathy for his son. Miyuki replies that the fiery bonds forged years ago had brought them together. While Heizo is going to give the updates about Kotomi, a man with a bandaged face suddenly comes into the room. The guests are shocked, and Mr Nagato introduces him as his son, Hideomi. Hideomi burns his face badly in an accident when he was in high school. He is accompanied by Yatsue's husband, Mitsuaki, and comments that the accident led to a happy ending. Hideomi is unhappy with the comment and leaves. Mitsuaki wishes 'Happy Birthday' to Mr Nagato and jokingly threatens him to step down to make Mitsuaki the chairman. Heizo decides to leave and let his son be Kogoro's assistant. 

During the birthday celebration at night, Mr Nagato announces the engagement between Miyuki and Hideomi. Mr Nagato asks where Hideomi is, and Mitsuaki offers to go and look for him. Later, he calls into the room and says that he couldn't find Hideomi. Suddenly, Mitsuaki screams on the phone. Kogoro snatches the phone from the butler and asks what is going on. Mitsuaki replies that he's in the room right below. Someone turned off the lights and came to him with a knife. Before Mitsuaki finishes his sentence, Kogoro hears another scream. Everyone goes to the balcony and looks down. They see Hideomi with a knife on his mouth, and he looks at them. Everyone runs to the room below, but the room is locked. When they open the door with the spare key, there's no one in the room, but Kogoro finds a rope tied to the balcony railing. Conan discovers Mitsuaki's body is pierced on top of the fence.
