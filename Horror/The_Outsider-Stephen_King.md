## Summary
### The Arrest (July 14th)

On July 10th, Jonathan Ritz walks a dog in the evening and finds a boy's body in a brutal state. The victim is an eleven-year-old boy named Frank Peterson.  Ralph Anderson, a detective in Flint City, is accompanied by two police officers to arrest the Little League coach in the middle of a baseball game at Estelle Barga Recreational Park. 

Before the story proceeds to the arrest, the narration goes back to another statement from a different witness, Mrs Arlene Stanhope, who claims that he saw Terry Maitland, the Little League coach, approach Frankie before he died. The story continues switching between a number of witnesses' statements and the arrest of Terry Maitland after several eyewitnesses and solid evidence point to him as the killer.

Ralph still has some hesitation because of the fast progress, but Bill Samuels, the district attorney, is convinced that Terry is the killer that they are looking for. Terry's fingerprints match those that were found in the stolen van, which has bloody clothes.

Terry tells the police that he was in Cap City with a few teachers for a conference on the day of Frankie's murder. He left at ten on Tuesday (10th July) morning and got back late Wednesday (11th July) night. His favourite author gave a speech at three on Tuesday at the Sheraton hotel. At the same time, one of the witnesses mentioned that she saw Terry put Frankie's bicycle into the stolen van and rode away with the boy.


### Sorry (July 14th - July 15th)
Howie's investigator, Alec, who was a former police officer, finds a strong alibi to prove Terry was at Harlan Corben's speech while the police still believe in witnesses' statements and fingerprint results. The police are looking for any evidence to shake the alibi.

### The Arraignment (July 16th)
The semen sample on the victim matches the cheek swabs that they took from Terry. On the arraignment day, the crowd gets chaotic when the police escort Terry to the court. Shots are fired; there are a few injuries. Oliver shoots Terry, and the police shoot Oliver. Both died on the scene. 

### Footsteps and Cantaloupe (July 18th - July 20th)
Ralph is under administration leave. Frankie's father attempts to commit suicide but is saved by his neighbour. However, he experiences brain damage.

### Yellow (July 21st - July 22nd)
An unknown man appears in front of Grace. He has straw eyes the first time she saw him, but when the second time he talks to her, he has her father's eyes. He asks her to pass a message to Ralph, asking Ralph to stop investigating if he doesn't want any bad things to happen to him.

### Holly (July 22nd - July 24th)
Alec hires Holly for some investigation work in Dayton. Holly finds out that another similar case happened in Dayton which involved an orderly who worked at the Heisman Memory Unit. Terry's father, Peter, who has Alzheimer's, is living at the Heisman Memory Unit. 

### Visits (July 25th)
An unknown figure appears to Jack and Jeannie, asking for them to warn or stop Ralph from continuing his investigation.

### Macy's Tells Gimbels (July 25th)
Holly finds some connections between the two murder cases, but Ralph didn't buy it because he finds it too impossible to believe. The unknown figure orders Jack to stop Ralph's investigation, and Jack follows Ralph.

### No End to the Universe (July 26th)
Holly believes the killer eats sadness and also the victims' pain as the victims are dying.

### Bienvenidos A Tejas (July 26th)
The outsider's next target is Claude after Claude got a cut by the outsider's fingernails when the outsider was impersonating Terry. The outsider hibernates in or near places of the dead, preferably those associated with the bloodline of the person he's changing into or out of. Ralph, Holly, Howie, and Alec keep their next destination in secret after picking Yune up at Claude's house. The outsider can read his target's mind, and so, they don't want Claude to know their destination.

### The Marysville Hole (July 27th)
Jack waits at the Marysville Hole, waiting for Ralph and the rest to appear. He plans to shoot all of them so that the outsider will take back the poison he gave to Jack. The outsider promised Jack that he will take away the skin cancer if Jack stops Ralph from investigating the case.

### Ending
* Howie and Alec die after being shot by Jack. Jack is already bitten by a poisonous snake, but when Ralph confronts him, Ralph shoots him.
* Holly destroys the outsider with her handmade weapon.

## Personal Thoughts
* The writing style is so different from his old style. I need some time to adapt it because I read more of King's old works than the newer ones. It's more straightforward and less blabbering. Still draggy but less beating around the bush.
* I hope Ralph Anderson is not a recurring character. I really don't like him. He's overly self-confident and unwilling to accept other people's opinions that contradict what he believes. He's quite full of himself.
* The story mentions Bill Hodges several times, which I assume is referring to the Bill Hodges trilogy, but I haven't read any of the books yet.
