#### File 201 - Another Victim / And Again...
As the Detective Boys discuss Conan's sudden disappearance, Ai notices the knocked-over chair, the scattered books, and the uncovered clock lid. She tries to track Conan's whereabouts and arranges the books on top of the chair. She steps on it and changes the hands of the clock. She is abruptly stopped by Takahito, who assumes her actions are in mischief. The Detective Boys explain that they're looking for Conan, and Katsuo suggests that maybe Conan is in the bathroom downstairs. After failing to find him in the bathroom, the kids surmise that Conan is hiding to play a prank on them and head off for dinner.

The Detective Boys celebrate over a large feast that lies before them, bringing life to the castle that had been missing since the fire accident. Agasa asks about the father's and son's decision to stay there after the fire. Takahito claims that he dreamed of moving back to the castle after college to work on his painting. Mitsuru says that he stays for the sake of his mother-in-law, but he becomes fond of the castle as time goes by. Their conversation is interrupted by the matriarch, stating that Mitsuru is only interested in the hidden treasure in the castle. She rebukes Mitsuru and asks the servant to bring her daughter to see her and also take her dinner to her room. Takahito expresses pity for his grandmother, who is still thinking that his mother is alive, while his father says that maybe her senility is getting worse because she has been stuck in the castle since her leg injury ten years ago. Mitsuru turns to Agasa and asks whether he solves the puzzle. He reminds Agasa about his father-in-law's will, which mentioned the hidden treasure, but Agasa replies that he doesn't know where to start. Ai replies that the starting place is the chess piece in the garden and hints that Conan will be the best candidate to solve the puzzle. Ayumi asks for a small plastic bag to save a piece of bread for Conan. Mitsuru asks about the boy's whereabouts, hoping he did not approach the burnt tower. He explains that two years ago, a new hired servant disappeared one night. Prior to that, the servant had claimed there was something of interest left in the tower. He was found four days later in the forest in a complete state of emaciation and starved to death. Since then, the workers sealed the entrance to the tower. There is also a rumour among the staff about the worker being killed by the curse of the victims of the fire. Horrified by Mitsuru's story, the Detective Boys fill Ayumi's bag with more bread and head out to find Conan.

The group looks for Conan everywhere in the garden, castle, and the locked entrance of the burnt tower, but he is nowhere to be found. The only place left is the forest, but the night is getting darker, and it's started to rain. They plan to return to the castle and call the police the next day. Ayumi is distressed by the decision and begins to cry. Ai, who still looks composed and calm, tells Ayumi that Conan is stronger than what she thinks, and he will find his own way back. Confused as to how Ai understands Conan so well, Ayumi asks her if she likes him. Ai asks Ayumi what if she does like Conan, and Ayumi replies that she won't like it. Ai assures her that she is not interested in Conan, filling Ayumi with joy. 

Agasa approaches Ai, and the two discuss Conan's situation. She doesn't believe Conan disappears just like that and is pessimistic with the situation. She suggests he call the police immediately and begin a thorough search of the premises. Without their knowledge, a mysterious figure is hiding behind a pillar, listening to their conversation. Agasa acts on her words and wanders down a corridor alone to find a telephone. He finds one at a corner in the hallway but is distracted when he finds Conan's beanie on the floor. He also finds a mysterious revolving door built into the wall and steps into the room to investigate. However, he is suddenly bludgeoned and dragged away by the unknown figure hiding behind the door. The professor's body disappears into the wall moments before Ai arrives.

#### File 202 - Countdown
Ai uses the telephone to call Inspector Megure. However, before they can communicate, Mitsuru and the Detective Boys interrupt, forcing Ai to quietly leave with them to bed.

Later that night, Ai leaves the bed after confirming her friends are asleep. She notices that Agasa's bed is empty, and she decides to sneak out. She hides behind a wall as she observes Masuyo wandering the hallway in her wheelchair under the delusion that her deceased daughter is coming to visit her. Once Takahito brings his grandmother back to her room, she sets out on her investigation, but not before discovering that the Detective Boys have been following her through the corridors. The kids are worried about Conan and Agasa, expressing their desire to help her look for them. Ai reluctantly agrees and warns them about risking their lives. Without their knowledge, a mysterious figure is tailing them.

The Detective Boys re-enter the room on the second floor from where they observed the chess pieces, and Ai activates the hidden door using the broken clock on the wall. The kids descend the long dark staircase with their Wristwatch Flashlights. Ai finds fresh blood on the ground and believes that it's likely belonging to Conan. Mitsuhiko then finds words etched into one of the steps, reading, "Someone is pretending to be me to get the castle's treasure. " Ai deduces that the inscription has been there long ago, and the writer was trapped there for quite some time. Genta finds Agasa's broken glasses further down the stairs, causing the group to panic. Ai notices a section of the wall slowly creaking open and immediately holds it closed. The wall then violently revolves, pushing Ai, Ayumi, and Mitsuhiko out into the hallway and leaving Genta alone in the hidden passageway along with an unknown figure.

The remaining trio return to the room with the clock but find the door shut. Ai concludes that they must have been followed and that Genta has likely been caught. Mitsuhiko suggests waking up someone to get help, but Ai notes that they cannot trust anyone in the castle, and there's a possibility that everyone in the castle is involved in it. She suggests finding another entrance into that passage before it's too late.

The trio enter a room filled with sculptures and paintings and find a pile of newspapers from four years ago detailing the fire that burned the nearby tower down. Ai reads that the fire took fifteen lives and that the flames were so intense that the investigators could only identify each of the remains based on dental records. Still, one individual remains missing. Ai deduces that someone used that accident to switch identity with someone else. Meanwhile, Mitsuhiko finds Conan's glasses on the hallway floor. He looks up to see two large doors swaying down the corridor. Ayumi notices Mitsuhiko's disappearance and alerts Ai. They suspect Mitsuhiko must have gone through the door that leads to the burnt tower that is supposedly locked. The iron doors appear open, but Ai stops Ayumi from entering inside, telling her it may be a trap. She instructs Ayumi to hide in the grass and count to 300 while she searches the tower; if Ai does not return by the time she is done counting, she is to run onto the main road and try to find help. Ayumi reluctantly accepts and does what she is told. However, as the seconds pass on and her count approaches 299, Ayum tries to peep the room behind the opened door.

#### File 203 - The Ivory Tower / Ivory Tower 
Ayumi chooses to enter the burnt tower instead of finding help. She tries not to cry and keeps calling Ai's name without knowing that a mysterious figure is tailing her. Ayumi enters a decrepit bathroom, locking the door behind her. She then hears tugging on the locked door and assumes it is Ai, but after witnessing the door bolt rattling and the door violently shaking, she freezes in fear. The unknown figure eventually breaks through the door to an empty bathroom, but Ayumi is nowhere in sight. The mysterious figure rotates a lamp on the wall and opens another hidden passage. On the opposite side of the door, Ai is seen hiding with Ayumi. When the coast is clear, Ai scolds Ayumi for not getting help before they descend a ladder. Ai asks about their Detective Badges, but Ayumi says they had run out of battery and gave it to Agasa for the fix.

Meanwhile, the mysterious figure notices a slight difference in the secret door they had just opened and quickly descends after the girls.

As Ai and Ayumi further explore the dungeon, the latter finds a human-shaped figure in the distance and runs to check it out. To her horror, she finds an old human skeleton slumped against the wall. Ai examines the skeleton and determines that although the individual had died many months ago, the body was moved to its present location quite recently. Ai suspects the skeleton was the one who did the engraving on the staircase. The skeleton was probably moved after Conan found it. Masuyo Mamiya then suddenly appears behind the girls and asks what they are doing, wondering where their other friend went. Ayumi tries to explain their situation to Masuyo but is stopped by Ai, who quickly drags her away. Ayumi says that they can't leave her behind, but Ai hastily explains that Masuyo was the one who attacked her. Based on the remains of the discovered corpse, it likely belonged to a female with limited mobility: the former matriarch. She deduces that the impostor had likely captured and detained the real matriarch in the secret halls of the castle. The impostor then underwent plastic surgery to look like the matriarch and pretended to be senile to act dumb. Believing the matriarch's daughter would not be fooled, she set fire to the tower to silence both her and the Mamiya family's most loyal staff.

The two exit the corridor through a trapdoor leading to the grand hall. However, Masuyo's impostor suddenly grabs a hold of Ai's leg and attempts to pull her back into the hidden passageway. She reveals herself to be a former servant of the Mamiya family and that she swapped places with the matriarch after she was dismissed for obsessing over finding the family's hidden treasure. The impostor then raises her rod and prepares to strike the girls but is disarmed by Conan, who kicks a bucket into her wrist. Conan, Mitsuhiko, and Genta stand atop a mezzanine railing demanding the woman stop her evil deeds. Ai and Ayumi are in joy seeing the boys, while the impostor looks in shock. Conan explains that Mitsuhiko had used the tracking device in his glasses to track his friends' locations via their Detective Badges. The woman turns around to see Agasa at the doorway. The professor calls the impostor Mutsumi Nishikawa and finds out about her true identity after consulting his friends who work as plastic surgeons for her request to change her face to that of an old woman. Conan explains that the loophole was when the impostor mentioned the change of passport size years ago. If the matriarch had been locked up in this castle for the past ten years, she wouldn't know that. He deduces that the impostor must be a frequent traveller to maintain her post-surgery face. Mutsumi laughs, confident she can escape the castle before the police arrive. Conan agrees with her assessment, but he claims to have solved the castle's mystery, deeply enticing the woman.

Conan explains that the chessboard is organised with black on top and white on the bottom. The rows are usually differentiated by the numbers 1–8, and the columns are differentiated by the alphabet letters A–H. Using the direction of the knight as a reference and the positions of the white pieces, one can spell the phrase "EGG HEAD" by listing the name of the column in order of the ascending rows. Since "Egg Head" is an English slang term for a member of the brainiac, the puzzle refers to the portrait of the patriarch, who was a proud historian. Finally, by rotating the portrait anticlockwise following the shape of the black pieces, a secret passage will reveal itself.

Mutsumi follows Conan's directions and indeed finds another hidden passage. She ignores Conan's explanation about how she murdered the servant who misinterpreted the black pieces to spell the letter "G," having gone to explore the burnt tower. Mutsumi frantically ascends the stairs towards a door concealing a source of incredible light. She swings the door open, hoping the treasure is in the room behind it. However, to her confusion, the door opens to the roof of the central tower. Conan points to the inscription written on the door: "I'll give the first person to reach this place the castle's incredible view." Mutsumi can't believe that she changed her face to an old woman for nothing. She slumps against the side of the tower, looking lost and sad like a real old woman.

An hour later, the police arrive and arrest the culprit. Mitsuru is disappointed about the truth behind the castle's treasure, but Takahito seems relieved that Mutsumi has been arrested. Takahito had his doubts about the fire and decided to stay at the castle to look for clues. According to the patriarch's notebook recovered from the hidden room, the secret passageways were originally installed in the castle from the earliest moments of the castle's construction. The structure originally belonged to a noble family in Europe and was eventually purchased by the patriarch, disassembled, shipped to Japan, and then reassembled with the vast resources controlled by the Mamiya family. The passageways were thus installed to give the ruling king and queen a route to escape in case the castle was ever besieged.

As the Detective Boys make their way back into the car, Katsuo runs after the group and tells them that Takahito wants to treat them for breakfast. Conan declines the offer, gallantly stating that he has both the camping food and Ayumi's bag of bread. Ai, however, accepts the gardener's offer to have a good breakfast with Genta, agreeing with it as well.

### Case 059 - Shinichi's First Murder Case 
#### File 204 - The First Entry / His First Bow
At the Haneda Airport, the Detective Boys excitedly observe the Boeing 747 that they will soon ride to Okinawa. Kogoro is annoyed about having to babysit the kids, but Ran reminds him that they are able to have the free trip because Professor Agasa won it on the Internet. Speaking of Agasa, Kogoro asks his whereabouts, and Conan replies that he's not coming due to a cold. Ayumi then asks whether Ai will join them, and Conan says that Ai is going to take a pass because she's going to take care of Agasa. Ran, who has yet to meet Ai, asks about her. Ayumi and Mitsuhiko describe her as a transfer student who has recently joined the Detective Boys. Ran looks back at Conan with an amused look, complaining to him that he never tells her about his life at school.

The plane soon takes off, and Ayumi is amazed with the view outside the window. She wants to share the view with Conan. She calls for him but finds that he has already fallen asleep. Ran tells Ayumi that Conan has already fallen asleep because he was reading the whole night. Ran smiles and removes his glasses, reminiscing about a time one year ago when she took a plane to Los Angeles with Shinichi, who also fell asleep shortly after take-off. Kogoro expresses disapproval about that trip, and Ran explains that she went to visit Shinichi's parents with him after they gave her the ticket. Kogoro later questions whether Shinichi has done anything improper to her. Ran brushes it off and decides to take a nap. 

Ran later dreams of her flight to Los Angeles a year ago and is awakened by a commotion in the plane. She sees a middle-aged passenger named Tsuneo Ukai ask his neighbour, Kazuhiro Otaka, to stop smoking in the non-smoking section. However, Kazuhiro not only ignores the advice, but he even rudely puffs some smoke towards Tsuneo. Tsugumi Amano, his girlfriend, scolds him for his uncivil behaviour. He maintains his arrogant demeanour, boasting about potentially becoming a rich man after selling his valuable negative photos and living a better life in America. Chizuru Tachikawa, his colleague, asserts that he will only receive such fortunes if the photos depicting an American politician caught in a scandalous act in Japan are truly genuine. Kazuhiro remains confident, stating that he already faxed a couple of the photos and confirmed the authenticity. Chizuru reminds him that they are on a vacation, and the deal is only a side trip. Kazuhiro changes topic and talks about surfing with another colleague, Noboru Saginuma, who has already fallen asleep behind his seat. Kazuhiro decides to catch a nap as well, and in front of him, Edward Crowe, a freelance journalist, appears to have heard the group's entire conversation.

Sometime later, Tsugumi begins to experience motion sickness and asks a stewardess for medication. Her coughing is getting worse, waking Chizuru up, and Chizuru offers to switch seats with her. They then realise Kazuhiro is not at his seat and Chizuru guesses that he might be in the bathroom. 

After quite some time, a passenger aggressively knocks on the door of an unresponsive lavatory occupant. A stewardess joins the passenger to check on the occupant, opening the door out of concern. To her horror, she finds Kazuhiro dead on the toilet seat. The stewardess' screams alert the entire plane, including Inspector Megure, who happens to be on board. Ran is also awakened by the commotion and finds the seat next to her is vacant. Megure approaches the crime scene and asks if the body has been left untouched. The stewardesses tell him about a strange boy who is currently inspecting the victim's body. The boy declares the victim's death a murder caused by asphyxiation, a puncture to the cervical cord. The murder weapon is a pointed subject, something like an icepick. Additionally, he believes that the victim may have been already in an unconscious state before being stabbed. Megure brushes past the stewardess and grabs the boy, demanding he identify himself. The boy turns around and gives his name, Shinichi Kudo, marking the beginning of his accomplished career as a private detective.

#### File 205 - A Locked Room in the Sky / Midair Sealed Chamber
Megure recognises the boy as Yusaku Kudo's son and expresses how happy he is to see him. The inspector later escorts Shinichi out of the lavatory, believing that a high school boy shouldn't meddle with a crime scene. Megure asks the stewardess for a camera to photograph the crime scene. However, the stewardess replies that she had captured over sixty photographs of the crime scene prior to Megure's inspection as instructed by Shinichi. Based on the livor mortis, Shinichi had narrowed the time of death to about 1 - 2 hours prior. He outlines three points of interest. First, there's no blood on the victim's clothes despite there being spots of blood on the wall near the body. Second, there's a strange scratch below the fatal wound. And third, the victim's left pocket was soaking wet. He believes someone purposely soaked the victim's hand. Megure dismisses Shinichi's analysis of the case regardless of his findings and asks the stewardess to get his associate's assistance in carrying out the police investigation.

Ran approaches Shinichi and scolds him for acting as a private detective, causing them to bicker against each other. A novice Wataru Takagi soon joins Megure, and the two begin collecting evidence, and the stewardess realises that their statements are the same as Shinichi's deduction before that. Wataru wonders who the stewardess is referring to, and Megure briefly explains who the boy is. Wataru is impressed with Shinichi's deduction skill, but Megure feels that Shinichi is still an amateur. Megure asks Wataru to look for anyone wandering near the area around the estimated time of the crime.  Shinichi replies that there are four suspects whom he observed who went to the bathroom within the past two hours, excluding the stewardess and passenger who discovered the body, as they lacked the time to commit the murder. Ran is surprised that Shinichi has been awake all night despite appearing asleep soon after the plane took off. The boy laughs it off, though he internally reflects on how he failed to maintain his sleep after noticing Ran sleeping besides him. With Megure following behind, Shinichi points out Chizuru Tachikawa, Tsugumi Amano, Tsuneo Ukai, and Edward Crowe as the four suspects.

The four suspects are taken to the crime scene, where Tsugumi breaks down crying upon the sight of her deceased boyfriend. Tsuneo suggests that someone probably killed Kazuhiro because of the negatives. Chizuru asks whether the negatives were found on Kazuhiro's body, and Wataru replies there's no such thing found from the victim. Megure deduces that the killer must have been someone who knew about the negatives. Noboru thinks the killer is a professional after asking Megure about the killing method. Shinichi says that the killer could have been anyone with a sedative, a weapon and some basic medical knowledge. 

Megure begins by asking each suspect about their actions preceding the discovery of Kazuhiro's body. Tsugumi states she went to the bathroom, but when she went to her seat, she was sure that Kazuhiro was still at his seat. The claim was supported by the stewardess who gave her medicine. Tsuneo says he entered the bathroom shortly after he witnessed the stewardess' interaction with Tsugumi. Chizuru says she visited the bathroom about twenty minutes after Tsugumi received her medicine. Kazuhiro hasn't returned for a while, and she went to check on him. She knocked on Kazuhiro's door and heard a knocking sound in response, indicating he was still alive at that stage. Lastly, Edward communicates with Shinichi in English, stating that he does not remember when he entered the bathroom. Shinichi walks away from the interaction with a cough and tells Ran that the man is using a strong cologne. Noboru suggests checking each suspect for the weapon and claims that he's innocent since he was asleep the whole time. Tsuneo accuses him of lying because he recalls that Noboru had also left his seat. Ran asks if Shinichi is sure about his list of suspects. Shinichi answers that he is but recalls being briefly distracted by his sleeping neighbour. Megure instructs that all five of them have a body check in the lavatory and their baggage to be searched. 

Sometime later, Wataru returns from the body checks and informs Megure that they couldn't find any plausible weapons from any of the five potential suspects. Megure asks Shinichi whether he's sure those people are the suspects. Shinichi finds the case is getting interesting and believes that the murderer is carrying the weapon and planning to leave the plane with it.

#### File 206 - The Last Trump Card
Shinichi requests another search of the suspects' belongings, believing the culprit has camouflaged their weapon with something else to smuggle it on board. Chizuru's belongings include a passport, wallet, handkerchief, makeup kit, tissue, boarding pass, sunglasses, camera, notepad, and a ballpoint pen. Tsugumi items are passport, wallet, handkerchief, beanie, boarding pass, camera, make-up, sewing kit, and motion sickness pills. Tsuneo's belongings are a passport, boarding pass, wallet, handkerchief, electric razor, towel, shaving cream, and a mask. Edward's personal items are a passport, wallet, handkerchief, boarding pass, towel, newspaper, two paperback books, glasses case, sewing kit, and a chequebook from the American National Bank. Shinichi also notices that the smell of Edward's cologne does not extend down to his wrists. Lastly, Noboru's possessions are a passport, wallet, handkerchief, boarding pass, camera, negative strips, wristwatch, cigarettes, lighter, and motion sickness medication. Shinichi takes an interest in Noboru's negatives and holds them to the light. He finds images of American Senator Dickson with an unknown woman. Chizuru points out those are the same as Kazuhiro's negatives, which are to be paid with a fortune by a newspaper in Los Angeles. Noboru retorts that these are his own, having taken the same photographs as Kazuhiro despite the quality not being as good as Kazuhiro's; he tries to sell them as well.

When the suspects undergo additional body checks, Shinichi wanders the aisles in deep thought. He analyses the suspects' seating and their actions prior to Kazuhiro's death. He then notices a fragment of Kazuhiro's negative strips next to Noboru's seat. Ran arrives and tells Shinichi that there is no weapon found on the suspects. 

As tensions rise between the suspects and the police, Shinichi suggests the police search Kazuhiro's personal belongings. Tsugumi points to Kazuhiro's bag but recalls a past incident where he found his bag cut to shreds after leaving it in the studio. He had thus purchased a new bag for the trip. Inside, the police find a passport, camera, film strips, sunglasses, three packs of cigarettes, a wallet, a towel, two magazines, a boarding pass, and a wristwatch. With no plausible weapons found, Shinichi begins to lose clues. Wataru then recognises Kazuhiro as a recipient of Nichiuri news' best photography award, recalling his award-winning photograph of a mother passing her child to a firefighter while engulfed in flames.

The suspects grow restless. Noboru ridicules Shinichi for his inexperience and naivety. As Shinichi re-examines the various alibis, Tsuneo approaches Noboru, asking him to confess the murder since he was the one who lied earlier. He repeats his statement, saying that Noboru was not in his seat when Tsugumi was asking for medicine. Tsugumi and Chizuru suddenly recall that Noboru was sleeping in his seat, while Tsuneo says that it doesn't make sense and the stewardess saw it too. Megure turns to the stewardess who supplied Tsugumi with medicine, hoping to put an end to the conflicting narratives. Unfortunately, the stewardess doesn't remember it. Shinichi approaches the stewardess and quietly asks a question which is immediately answered in an apologetic manner.

With this new information, Shinichi believes he has identified the culprit, which is supported by the other testimonies and the findings of the evidence. The only remaining is the missing weapon. While he is trying to connect the dots for the final piece of the mystery, he is interrupted by Ran. Ran tells him that Megure wants to see him. Shinichi uses the opportunity to quietly ask Ran a deeply embarrassing question, to which she sheepishly affirms. Ran's answer leads Shinichi to finally figure it out and confirm that he found the killer's trump card.

#### File 207 - A Secret Hidden in the Heart / Hidden Within The Depth Of The Heart...
Megure puts Noboru as the leading suspect but is interrupted by Shinichi. Shinichi claims that Noboru is not a suspect, but he is used by the culprit in his method for murdering Kazuhiro.

Wataru's suspicion falls on Chizuru, who claims to have knocked on Kazuhiro's door without any witnesses. Shinichi validates Chizuru's statement, though it adds that the person she heard knocking in response was not Kazuhiro but rather Edward. He accuses the man of searching Kazuhiro's body for the negatives, having stuck his hand into the man's left pocket. After noticing that he had left the scent of his cologne in Kazuhiro's pocket, Edward washed his hands and used a damp handkerchief on the pocket, thus leaving it in a soaking wet state. Megure remains sceptical and tells Shinichi that he should speak in English if he wants to communicate with Edward. Shinichi feels there is no need to do so because Edward is actually fluent in Japanese. Edward admits that he did search the body for the negatives. He explains that he was hired by Senator Dickson to secretly write a cheque to Kazuhiro in exchange for the negatives. Noboru accuses him of being the killer, but Shinichi claims that Kazuhiro was already dead when Edward entered the lavatory.

Edward's story matches almost identically to Shinichi's prediction, adding that he could not find the negatives on Kazuhiro. Shinichi adds that the dried bloodstain on the wall left no trace on Kazuhiro's clothes, indicating that the body's position had been altered after the culprit had left Kazuhiro's corpse. Edward would not report about Kazuhiro's death, as he had tampered with the crime scene. Additionally, the only person who would consider checking Kazuhiro's pockets for the negatives would be Edward, who sat in front of Kazuhiro. The other three suspects were sitting next to the victim and saw him pull the negatives from his chest pocket.

Megure demands Shinichi name the culprit, and Shinichi rules out Edward and Chizuru, who knocked on the door while the victim was in the lavatory. He continues with his deduction and confirms that Noboru was sleeping in his seat, adding that the murderer put a hat and eye mask on him. He explains that after murdering Kazuhiro in the lavatory, the murderer sat next to Noboru. The culprit disguised Noboru to look like Kazuhiro, then called the stewardess to ask for medicine to create an illusion that Kazuhiro was still alive at that time.

At this stage, Noboru and Chizuru seem able to guess who the killer is, and Shinichi declares Tsugumi as the killer. Tsugumi returned to her original seat one row ahead and took the seat next to Chizuru, whom she would wake up with a loud cough. Tsuneo asks how about the empty seat he saw, and Shinichi replies that he probably saw the seat behind Noboru. Megure questions that the trick won't be able to be executed unless the seat next to Noboru is vacant. He also points out that anyone would wake up if someone suddenly put a hat and a mask on them. Shinichi replies that someone can buy an extra ticket to vacate the seat, and Noboru would go into deep sleep if Chizuru gave him motion sickness medicine that caused drowsiness. Wataru asks about the possibility that the stewardess notices that Chizuru wasn't sitting next to her. Shinichi replies that she can use a blanket to cover the luggage as camouflage. He further adds that the culprit cut up the negatives using the scissors in her sewing kit and flushed them in the toilet. She hopes to make it appear as if someone else committed the crime and to avoid being suspected as the culprit. 

Tsugumi probably told Kazuhiro that she needs to talk to him at the toilet after Chizuru falls asleep. Shortly after, she followed him to the toilet. When Kazuhiro opened the door, she pressed the drugged handkerchief into his face, then stabbed him in the cervical cord to kill him. She took a risk by leaving the door unlocked when she killed the victim so she would be able to notice if someone was waiting. The group asks for the evidence and the murder weapon since all of them went through the body check twice. Shinichi reveals that the weapon is the metal underwire on the right side of Tsugumi's bra. By straightening the wire, an individual would be able to puncture the neck with enough force to reach the spinal cord. The scratch mark on the victim's neck below the puncture mark was caused by the other end of the wire scraping against the skin. Lastly, the reason she returned the wire to her bra was to avoid placing focus on her missing bra wire during the body checks.

Tsugumi reveals the reason for her crime, explaining that she met Kazuhiro after he won the Nichiuri News' prize for photography. His winning photograph had brilliantly depicted the range of emotions surrounding the fire that claimed the lives of dozens of people, including her brother. However, in his room, she had discovered several photos of the building both during and before the fire, implying he had committed arson in hopes of creating the perfect scene for his shot. When she confronted him about his heinous crime, he gave her a smug look and was proud of his act. She thought that she had perfectly kept the weapon. Shinichi says that he saw her hesitating to reach the luggage rack with the right hand and then switching to the left hand. He thinks the pointed wire must have pricked her, causing her to hesitate. Megure and Wataru express their admiration for Shinichi's deductive skills. Shinichi asks Megure the reason for the trip, and Megure replies that they are headed to Los Angeles to extradite a Japanese criminal. Seeing Shinichi able to identify the murder weapon of the case, Ran finally understands why he was asking her such an embarrassing question about the specifics of women's undergarments.

### Case 060 - Treadmill Murder Case 
#### File 208 - The Sakuradamon Incident / The Sakuradamon Incident!?
The Detective Boys arrive at the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department Headquarters with excitement despite the fact that the purpose of the trip is for the interrogation about the incident at the Mamiya castle. Detective Wataru Takagi soon arrives and introduces himself to Professor Agasa before leading the group inside.

The Detective Boys are surprised with the interior's cleanliness, as they were expecting that the detective's office would be filled with cigarette smoke and full of unfriendly faces, similar to how police stations are depicted in popular culture. Agasa asks about Inspector Megure's whereabouts, saying he had personally invited them. Wataru says he is busy tracking down a team of robbers who stole ¥200,000,000 from a bank in Haido City. The inspector's wife was at the bank during the time of the robbery. She sustained a minor injury from the ordeal, leading Megure, with determination, to apprehend the robbers. As Wataru prepares the paperwork for the questioning, the Detective Boys notice the button on his sleeve is coming off, the collar is dirty, and the shirt is all wrinkled. Ayumi asks Wataru whether he has a girlfriend. Agasa offers to introduce him to his first cousin twice removed, but Wataru refuses, stating that he already has a crush for someone.

A female officer then walks into the room, asking Wataru about being accompanied by the kids there. Wataru is caught off guard, struggling to introduce the woman to the kids, especially when the kids tease him and assume that the woman is his girlfriend. The woman then introduces herself as Assistant Inspector Miwako Sato, whom Ayumi recognises from the operation at the Emperor's Cup.

Initially, the kids are not happy that Miwako seems unappreciative of their help at the Emperor's Cup; however, the Detective Boys are immediately inspired by her words when she says that "justice is not something you openly say it but rather something you keep in your heart." Wataru asks why Miwako is in the office, believing she was assigned to the robbery case with Megure. She says the branch manager of the robbed bank had called her the night before, claiming to have remembered key information about the culprits. The manager intends to arrive at 2 pm with his wife, who was a hostage during the robbery. Wataru recalls receiving a similar phone call from the wife herself, and she wanted to meet at the police station at 5 pm. Miwako explains that the branch manager had combined their two appointments to operate as a pair during the day for safety reasons. Miwako then shifts the conversation topic, asking Wataru if their mutual friend from the traffic bureau, Yumi, had told him anything about her drunken outburst last night. Wataru denies having any information about it. At the same time, he internally reflects on how Yumi had approached him earlier that day, telling him that Miwako had divulged that she had a crash for someone in his department.

The branch manager, Keizo Masuo, then arrives at the office. Wataru asks about his wife, and he tells him that his wife is on her way. He decides to borrow the office phone and call his house. Conan and Ai both find that Keizo had strangely looked at his watch three times over the course of a brief introduction. The call goes through, and Keizo's wife, Kayo Masuo, picks up while on her exercise bike. Keizo passes the phone to Wataru and asks about their meeting time at 2 pm, though the woman responds with confusion. Keizo quickly retakes the phone and reminds her about their conversation the night before. Wataru's hand accidentally presses the speaker button just as Kayo notices danger behind her. The woman's sudden scream is blared over the telephone speakers before going completely quiet, unresponsive to Wataru's words. Miwako orders Wataru to get the investigation unit to wait at the house while she drives her car. 

In the car, Miwako asks whether Keizo noticed anything unusual about his wife in the last few days. He says that the wife mentioned a strange man lurking around outside their house last night. Miwako thinks that there's a chance that the robbers wanted to silence Kayo because they worried that she had seen their faces. Keizo begins to panic, but Wataru tries to calm him down, suggesting maybe it was a burglar or something less serious. Conan suddenly voices his opinion, saying that she would tell the police when talking on the phone if she saw a burglar. Wataru is in surprise, wondering when Conan is in the car. Miwako says that she has no time to drop Conan off, as Kayo's safety is the highest priority.

Once at the house, Miwako and Wataru check the second floor while Keizo inspects the living room. Conan notices a hole has been cut in a window down the hall. Keizo's screams alert the officers as they discover Kayo's corpse on the living room floor with a knife in her back. While Keizo begins to lose control over his emotions, Conan notices a few unusual things about Keizo, leading him to question whether this is a premeditated murder.

<br>

#### File 209 - The Inspector's Deduction
The detectives deduce that the crime must have occurred when the victim screamed and she was attacked from behind when she was on the phone. Conan believes that Kayo had been using the exercise bike at the same time while talking on the phone. He finds the victim is soaking wet with sweat. When a human dies, the body is no longer producing sweat, but the sweat remains for a long time. Moreover, he can't find any jogging shoes which further support his theory. Impressed with the boy's observations, Miwako asks for his name, to which Conan responds with his full name and declares that he is a detective. 

Several officers leave the house to look for witnesses while Miwako orders a thorough search of the house. Suddenly, a confident voice in the doorway expresses his disagreement, saying the crime was targeting the wife from the beginning. Ninzaburo Shiratori, who was recently promoted to the title of inspector, makes his entrance and immediately takes control of the investigation. Based on the flirting interaction between Miwako and Ninzaburo, Wataru begins feeling disappointed as he considers the possibility that Miwako was referring to Ninzaburo when talking about her romance interest. Ninzaburo then begins to deduce that the culprit is not a burglar because the burglar would run away if the victim screamed. He believes the culprits were the bank robbers who made Kayo as a hostage, as they didn't want the victim to say anything to the police. He mentions Kayo having violently resisted the robbers to such an extent that Megure's wife volunteered to take her place. Keizo then remembers that Kayo had mentioned something about foreigners last night. Ninzaburo proceeds to jump to conclusions and orders Wataru to alert the robbery investigators that at least one of the culprits is a foreigner. However, Conan feels something is not right and points out that the space behind the exercise bike is only about one metre. There isn't a door behind the exercise bike, indicating the impossibility of someone attacking the victim from behind. Ninzaburo dismisses the boy's observation but nonetheless sparks further trepidations in Wataru, who imagines the inspector together with Miwako and a child. 

Ninzaburo then asks Keizo about the couple's daily routines. Keizo states that he leaves for work in the morning, while Kayo had been out drinking with her friends last night and slept until noon. She had always ridden her exercise bike from noon to 2 pm every day. Ninzaburo walks over to the wall behind the exercise bike and pulls the curtains, revealing a sparsely filled bookshelf, which he believes the culprit had hidden in. Wataru says that the culprit would not take the time to reinstall the shelves back into the closet if he killed her in a panic. An officer then bursts into the room holding a large book containing photo albums. Several individuals captured in the photos have been circled with a pen. Keizo is shocked and hurriedly explains that it was merely just a little joke from Kayo. Seeing the man's reaction, Conan is confident that Keizo is the murderer, but he lacks the evidence required to override the man's perfect alibi.

Keizo then randomly licks the tip of his finger after placing his handkerchief into his pocket. Conan then finds a small detail in the exercise bike's joint connecting the body to the pedal. He climbs on the bookshelf behind the bike. While he initially struggles to find what he had expected, he finds a bloodstain in the bookshelf seemingly cut off. As Conan removes from the bookshelf by a police officer, he smiles, having solved the trick used by Keizo to kill his wife.

<br>

#### File 210 - An Unforeseeable Enemy / An Unexpected Rival
Ninzaburo plans to wrap up the investigation and report it to the headquarters. He asks Wataru to drive Conan back to Kogoro's house but then sees Conan folding an origami sword. Conan uses his prop to demonstrate that a knife-wielding assailant would either strike their victim from above or below, with the blade aligned in the direction of the attacker. Therefore, if the culprit lunged at Kayo from behind, the knife would not be horizontally lodged in her back.

Ninzaburo explains the possibility of the culprit having thrown Kayo to the floor and stabbed her while keeping her down with his knees. Conan retorts that if such a scenario were to be true, the police would have discovered traces of Kayo's sweat all around the body. Miwako and Wataru discuss other unusual events, mentioning how the culprit had bothered to close all of the doors leading to the living room and had locked the window they entered on the way out. Conan then points out a long, thin wire tangled around the pedal pivot of the exercise bike. He even finds the tip of the wire tied into a small loop. Keizo anxiously explains that his friend's kids had played with the exercise bike during a gathering six months ago, believing the wire to be the result of their mischief. Wataru suggests the wire had been originally tethered to the bookshelf behind the exercise bike. Once the pedals had rotated enough times, the bookshelf would eventually topple onto the rider. If the knife had been attached to the top of the bookshelf, it would automatically stab the victim in the back.

Ninzaburo finds the theory a bit far-fetched, but Wataru in the end concludes that Keizo is the culprit. Both Wataru and Miwako believe that Keito could have pushed the toppled bookshelf back into place shortly after discovering his wife's body. Keizo objects, stating that he had no way of knowing that only two officers would arrive at the house in response to his wife's call. Additionally, if the knife had been attached to the bookshelf, the knife would be pulled out of Kayo's back upon pushing the bookshelf back into its original place. Conan then climbs to the bookshelf and demonstrates that one can stick the knife between the top shelf and a row of books. Furthermore, one can place additional books on top of the bookshelf to better camouflage the knife with the surroundings, thus preventing Kayo from noticing that she is in a dangerous position. Wataru reasons that even if several officers had discovered Kayo's body before Keizo could alter the crime scene, the scattered books would appear as a result of a physical struggle between Kayo and her killer.

Once the bookshelf fell, the wire would naturally detach from the thumbtack and wrap itself into the rotating pedal. Ninzaburo offers to perform a simulation to further prove their theories, but Keizo instead admits his crime in his own volition. He states he killed her to collect her life insurance money, making it appear as though it was the work of the bank robbers. Miwako then accuses the man of being the mastermind behind the robbery on his branch. The robbers were likely his friends who coincidentally took Kayo as their hostage. Kayo had recognised the robbers as Keizo's friends through their masks. Therefore, Keizo's real motive was likely to prevent her from sharing such information with the authorities. Keizo falls to his knees defeated. Miwako explains that the wife had circled the robbers' faces on their photo album to bring to the police.

Two hours later, the two bank robbers are arrested while counting their money in an apartment. Keizo reveals in his interrogation that he originally planned the robbery to support Kayo's lavish spending. Unfortunately, the botching of his operation would eventually lead to her silencing, putting an end to a rather ironic case.

### Case 061 - Wedding Murder Case
#### File 211 - A Perfect Day for Tokyo / A Nice Day I'm Tokyo
Heiji and Kazuha are walking at the busy weekend streets of Shibuya, accompanied by Conan, Ran, and Kogoro. Heiji explains that they are visiting on behalf of his mother, whose former classmate's son's wedding is planned for tomorrow. Although his mother was originally invited, she was unable to attend due to sustaining a toe injury while cooking. Thus, Kazuha has joined him in her stead. Kazuha expresses she wasn't interested in coming, but Heiji's mother begged her to look after him. Heiji teases her, saying that she was so eager to shop in Shibuya. Ran offers to take Kazuha to her favourite store, but Kazuha coldly rejects it, preferring to follow her own plan. 

The two girls accompany each other at an apparel store, where Kazuha continues with her cold treatment. Her bad mood persists even after they return to their parked car. Ran politely confronts her about her demeanour, curious about the cause of her resentment. Kazuha reveals her speculation about Ran secretly coordinating her outfits with Heiji, pointing to both of their matching colour and style of shirts on that day and during her previous visit to Osaka. Ran realises Kazuha is not happy with the coincidence, and Ran immediately swaps her shirt for her new purchase and changes it in broad daylight. Kazuha is astonished by her kindness and instantly changes her opinion about Ran.

The group then heads to Meiji Jingu Shrine, and Heiji is surprised by the crowd. Kogoro remarks that June is a month for all the June brides to pray for their marriages. Kogoro realises that Ran is not wearing the shirt her mother gave her, and Ran replies that the shirt got sweaty. Kazuha feels guilty and apologises to Ran.

Heiji and Conan visit the offering box to make a wish. Heiji notices a young girl besides him also making a wish with a deeply saddened expression. He is then recognised by Akio Shigematsu, his mother's classmate's family butler. Mr Shigematsu points to the young girl, saying he is accompanying his young master's fiancee, Kaede Katagiri, on a tour of Tokyo. Heiji asks about the groom, Kikuhito Morizono's, whereabouts. Kaede replies apologetically because she insisted on visiting the Tokyo Tower, but the fiancé is afraid of heights. Mr Shigematsu asks Kaede about her wish, and the latter voices her desire for children. Heiji remarks that Kaede's expression told a different story. Ran and Kazuha voice their opinion, believing that a girl who is getting married has a lot in her mind and Kade is probably experiencing wedding blues.

Later that day, Mr Shigematsu invites the group to the Morizono family mansion. While on the path to the building, a young servant named Yuji Sakuraba energetically lands on the path before the crowd while holding the family cat, which had ascended a tall tree.  Kaede's future sister-in-law, Yurie Morizono, appears and warns Kaede to follow the family rules even if she is the daughter of a chairman of a big company. Yurie later changes her tone and says that she was just joking. She claims that she usually lives abroad, and she won't be able to get on Kaede's nerves. She claims that Kaede is a capable girl who manages to pin down her playboy brother. Mr Shigematsu corrects her, stating it was Kikuhito who fell in love with Kaede. She replies that it wasn't what she heard from him earlier and says that her father will probably reward him for arranging the marriage.

At the dinner table, the visitors meet Mikio Morizono, the family patriarch. The man is a fan of Kogoro and asks how he feels to be called one of the greatest detectives. Kogoro replies that it's definitely a surprise since most of the cases are solved so quickly, like in a dream. Heiji agrees with it, and Conan kicks Heiji's leg under the table. Heiji looks in pain, and Kazuha asks what he meant. The awkward moment is interrupted by Mikio asking Mr Shigematsu to find a camera to take a commemorative photo. He also thanks his butler for his hard work for his son's marriage and promises to pay him handsomely. Mikio then notices his cat is once again missing and asks Yuji to find her again. He adds that his late wife, who died in a traffic accident four years ago, used to love the cat. The group also finally meets Kikuhito, who refers to Kaede as "honey", making Ran and Kazuha cringe.

After dinner, Kogoro, Ran, and Conan are leaving to return home. As the three near the exit, Heiji sighs in relief, commenting on the lack of incident despite Kogoro's presence. His relief is short-lived as a window on the third floor suddenly shatters. Conan asks Ran to keep an eye on the window and start to run into the mansion. Heiji instructs Kazuha to get Mr Shigematsu while he rushes back into the mansion to investigate. 

Heiji knocks on the door and finds the room is locked. Soon after, Kogoro and Kazuha join them, and Heiji asks where Mr Shigematsu is. Kazuha replies that he's been missing for quite some time. Heiji and Kogoro decide to batter down the door. They find an ominous trail of blood leading to an adjoining room. Following the trail, the group is horrified to find a bloodied Mr Shigematsu slouched against the wall.
