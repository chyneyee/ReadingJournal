# Detective Conan Volume 22 - Murder on the Hokutosei Express

## Summary
### Case 061 - Wedding Murder Case
#### File 212 - The Same / A Matching Set 
Kazuha's scream alerts the entire mansion. The other family members are in shock, and Kaede passes out. Kogoro calls the police, but Heiji and Conan already begin to investigate the crime scene. They find all the windows are locked, while Ran, who had been watching the room's main window from the building's exterior, reports no unusual activity. Heiji then finds Mr Shigematsu's bundle of keys in his coat pocket. They ask Yuji whether Mr Shigematsu is the only one who has all the keys in the house. Yuji replies that everyone has a spare key to their private room, but Mr Shigematsu has all the keys of the house. After learning that Mr Shigematsu contains the unique key to the room, Heiji declares the case an impossible locked-room murder. 

Yurie suggests the possibility of a suicide, but Kogoro debunks the theory, pointing to the missing murder weapon. Kikuhito intends to continue with the wedding plan despite Yurie disagreeing with it. He doesn't think his wedding and honeymoon should be postponed because the victim isn't a member of the Morizono family. The heartless words cause Yuji to strike Kikuhito with his fists, sending him crashing against a glass window. Mikio takes Yuji's side and harshly condemns his son for his insensitivity. He reminds Kikuhito that Mr Shigematsu was like Yuji's father after Yuji lost his parents at a young age and that the butler also served as an invaluable member of the family business for which he acted as Kikuhito's advisor. Mikio also gives a stern warning to Yuji for raising a hand against a member of the family he's serving. Yuji apologises, and Mikio asks Yurie to call Kaede's parents to inform them of the wedding postponement. Suddenly, Kazuha lets out another scream, believing Mr Shigematsu's body had displayed some movements. However, the family cat, Rebecca, appears from behind the body and shows its fondness for Yurie because Yurie is the splitting image of her mother.

With news of the police's arrival, Conan and Kogoro accompany a cane-wielding Mikio downstairs, who tells them he permanently damaged his leg in the accident that claimed his wife's life. The trio is then greeted by a swarm of reporters who battle the police for entry into the mansion. After a brief tussle, Inspector Megure, Wataru, and several supporting officers manage to squeeze through, introducing themselves to Mikio.

Kogoro and Heiji inform Megure about their findings. Wataru reports to Megure that all the servants were having dinner at the time of the murder, and the only people who don't have clear alibis are Mikio, Kikuhito, Yurie, Kaede, and Yuji. Megure commences the investigation by ordering interrogations and body checks of the five suspects and also instructing his men to conduct a thorough search of the property for the missing weapon.

Some time later, Conan and Heiji stand at the mansion entrance and share their findings, both having noticed four suspicious points about the crime scene. First, the culprit removed the weapon from the body, thus reducing the probability of suicide and increasing the chance of being caught. Second, Mr Shigematsu's body was dragged to another room. Third, the light is turned on in the room when the body is inside. And finally, the cat's presence. As they ponder, they see Yuji asking Wataru if he can assist in the search for the murder weapon. Wataru refuses, and Kikuhito grabs Yuji's shirt, saying Yuji causes more trouble for the family every time he tries to do something. He then throws Yuji to the ground and warns him to remember his position as a servant, walking away with a smirk. Kazuha and Ran rush to help Yuji; the former notices that he has something that matches with another person. Ran tries to check her shirt, but Kazuha tells her that Yuji and Kaede are wearing the same pendant. The girl's observation draws Conan's and Heiji's attention, the two remarking that the new finding may be a pivotal key to solving the mystery.

#### File 213 - The Evidence / The Evidence...
The investigation continues as the police struggle to locate the missing murder weapon. Heiji asks to also look for bloodstained clothes because when the killer pulled the knife out of the victim, the blood must've splattered everywhere. Kogoro suggests the possibility of a burglar as the murderer, but Wataru disagrees because the house has a perfect security system. Heiji adds that the doors and windows were locked, narrowing down the suspect to five people. Megure explains that Mikio was waiting in his study for a pre-planned meeting with Mr Shigematsu, Yurie was taking a bath, Kikuhito was reading a magazine in his room, Yuji was searching for the family cat, and Kaede claims to have been sleeping. The inspector finds Kaede is suspicious, noting that Yurie had attempted to invite Kaede to take a bath but received no response when she called. 

A forensic analyst then discovers a faint bloodstain on a glass door latch. Heiji reasons that the culprit had opened the glass door with their bloodied gloves and exited onto the balcony. Conan finds a broken glass shard with a small hole in it. Prior to Kikuhito slamming into the glass, the hole would have been positioned near the edge of the windowpane, making it difficult to spot upon first inspection.

Mikio then enters the room and expresses his desire to hold a funeral service for Mr Shigematsu, having him buried near his late wife's tomb. Heiji is reminded of gossip his mother had shared with him about the wife having originally been in love with Mr Shigematsu. The wife had regularly visited the mansion to meet the butler, but Mikio had misinterpreted her frequent appearances as a sign that she had romantic feelings for himself. Following Mikio's proposal, the two families greatly supported the arrangement and overwhelmed her into agreeing to the marriage. Conan finds the story "interesting", and Heiji is still thinking about Yuji and Kaede wearing the same pendant. He also says Kikuhito adds pressure on Kaede's parents' company for the marriage. Conan seemingly agrees with his analysis but shifts the discussion to the culprit's escape route. Conan suggests Heiji tie a rope around his waist in an experiment to test whether one can escape to a lower level. Heiji effortlessly manoeuvres around the third-floor balcony and lands on the platform below. He then notices an object caught in the nearby tree canopy. As Conan leans over the railing to get a better look, he is caught off guard by Kikuhito and tumbles over the edge, barely managing to hold onto the ledge. Kikuhito is unable to help the boy due to his acrophobia, leaving Yuji to come to the rescue.

Heiji and Conan later climb the suspicious tree and discover a bloodstained shirt tied up by a long, thin string. They also find a pair of bloodied gloves and the killer's bloodstained kitchen knife. The two happily remark they have solved the mystery but feel there are still many questions unanswered. Rain begins to fall, prompting Ran and Kazuha to call for them to retreat inside. Hearing the word "rain", Conan and Heiji suddenly remember the initial state of the rain shutter. They finally figure out how the entire murder was executed and who the murderer is, but they can't do anything due to their lack of evidence. Conan suggests baiting the killer since they can't find the proof. Some time later, everyone is summoned to the crime scene, where Heiji steps forward, declaring that he has solved Mr Shigematsu's murder. He immediately turns to Yuji and names him the killer.

#### File 214 - The Trapped Quarry / Caught Prey

Heiji prepares a long, thin string with a loop created at one end with tape. He exits the shattered glass door and restores the shard to its original position lining the windowpane. He then places the string's loop around the latch and passes the string through the shard's hole. At the balcony, Heiji pulls on the string, which in turn manoeuvres the latch to lock the door. The string is then removed from the room entirely by tugging on it with greater force. He accuses Yuji of having attempted to destroy the evidence of the glass with a hole by punching Kikuhito into the window. Heiji adds that he already tested the method that the killer used to escape the balcony. He states that Mikio cannot perform the outlined trick due to his injured knee, Yurie and Kaede are also incapable due to wearing dresses, and Kikuhito would not be able to exit onto the balcony because of his acrophobia. Thus, the only remaining suspect is the athletic Yuji who can climb a tree to get a cat. Yuji is shocked by Heiji's accusation, exclaiming that he is innocent. Kaede steps forward to provide his alibi, revealing that she had been accompanying him on his search for the cat during the time of Mr Shigematsu's murder. Megure asks why she did not come forward with this information sooner. Heiji deduces that the two are secretly lovers, pointing to their matching pendants. Despite Yuji and Kaede continuously denying their involvement in the crime, Wataru ushers Yuji away for further questioning at the police station. Megure tells Kaede that testimony from a person who has a close relationship with the suspect does not hold up in court. Kikuhito comforts a distraught Kaede, voicing his resolve to wait for her to eventually forget Yuji. Kazuha and Ran watch in disbelief when Heiji coldly labels Yuji a violent killer as he is led out the door. Megure suspends the investigation for the day, planning on resuming the search for the weapon the next day. With the case seemingly closed, Kogoro prepares to take his leave but is stopped by Conan, who suggests they stay the night.

Later that night, an unknown person in a raincoat is retrieving something from the ground beneath a tree. He quietly sneaks into the servants' quarters and approaches a room labelled "Sakuraba". He then finds a duffle bag placed atop the bed and attempts to plant a knife into the man's belongings. Suddenly, the lights are switched on by Heiji, catching Kikuhito Morizono in the act of framing Yuji.

Heiji states that Kikuhito purposely left a bloodstain on the door latch, but no such marks were left anywhere on the balcony. Moreover, someone who made sure to not leave their fingerprints inside would not then remove their gloves once they exited the room. Conan then appears, explaining that Kikuhito had never left the locked room. Kikuhito was likely hiding behind a curtain in the darkened adjoining room. The reason only one of the rain shutters was closed was to avoid being found out by the people outside. The trail of blood leading to the bright room was to direct the initial responders away from his hiding spot. He then joined the rest of the group in reacting to Kazuha's scream, making it appear as though he had just arrived. Lastly, he shut the cat at the crime scene to ensure that Yuji would be without an alibi, as he needs to search for a cat that is hidden in a locked room. Conan reasons that no killer would bundle all of the evidence together unless they were afraid of stepping out onto the balcony. Kikuhito objects, saying he was not trying to plant the knife but rather just found it moments ago in Yuji's bag. Heiji reveals that the bag belongs to him and Yuji's room is next door. The room, the bag, the weapon in the bushes, and Yuji's arrest are all to trap Kikuhito with Megure's help. Conan also alerts him that the police have surrounded the building.

Kikuhito explains that Mr Shigematsu had threatened to expose his company's misdeed to Mikio if he did not call off his wedding with Kaede. Thus, Kikuhito chose to murder Mr Shigematsu and place the blame on Yuji, whom he believed stole his woman. Heiji corrects him by revealing that Kaede had always been interested in Yuji from the beginning. Mr Shigematsu initially thought Kaede was interested in Kikuhito, and that's why he pushed for the marriage. Mr Shigematsu didn't tell Kikuhito the truth after that because he didn't want to hurt his feelings. Having experienced the exact same situation before when he was young, Mr Shigematsu wished to see Yuji and Kaede have the happy ending that he never has.

The next day, Kogoro and Conan see Heiji off at Tokyo Station. They recall Yuji and Kaede both feeling relieved and sad at the same time after hearing the news. Heiji then asks about Kazuha's whereabouts before hearing her pleading with Ran not to pressure her into their plan. He turns around and sees Ran push Kazuha towards him. Kazuha, dressed in a similar pinstripe shirt as Heji, is embarrassed by the situation. Kazuha attempts to run to the bathroom to change into something else but is stopped by Heiji, who says he does not mind, remarking that they look like siblings. Kogoro feels it's more like a comedy duo.

### Case 062 - North Star Murder Case 
#### File 215 - The Northbound Train / The Northern Star
At the luxury overnight express train, Hokutosei, with a scenic view outside, Ran reads a letter from Natsue Hatamoto ([Case 007 - Hatamoto Family Case](https://github.com/chyneyee/ReadingJournal/blob/main/Manga/Detective_Conan/Volume_03.md#hatamoto-family-case-case-007)) who had sponsored their trip to Hokkaido. Kogoro reflects on his experiences on the ship where they last met Natsue, realising that it was the first time that he suddenly dozed off in the middle of a deduction. The trio then plans to head to the dining car. As Kogoro exits the cabin, Kogoro collides with an ill-tempered masked man who quickly walks away after recognising the detective's identity. The masked man, Yasuji Asama, is seen returning to his cabin in a sweat and wonders why Kogoro is on the train.

As the train speeds through the night, Kogoro voices his contentment with the luxury experience. However, their conversation is interrupted by an argument behind their table. The jewellery store owner, Keitaro Izumo, scolds his manager, Toshinori Kaetsu, for his inability to secure a luxury room on the train. Kogoro vaguely recognises Keitaro from media coverage about him chasing away the thief who broke into the jewellery store the week before. Furuito City mayor Akishige Ishizuchi joins their conversation, bringing up the rumours about the robbery was staged for publicity. Kogoro doesn't understand the remark. Toshinori further explains that there is a rumour circling around that the robbery was set up by his boss to gain favours for the mayoral election. Akishige then cites several mistakes made by the professional thief, and one of the employees in the store heard the thief say that the robbery wasn't the way it was supposed to go before the thief fled the scene.

Kogoro is then recognised by another passenger named Toru Aoba. Then, a mysterious woman named Fumiyo Akechi is standing at the entrance remarking about a "coincidence" before refusing the dining service and returning to her cabin. After the meal service, Conan cannot shake the feeling of having experienced a similar case in the past. The trio also see Keitaro's wife, Azusa Izumo, fearfully scanning the train corridor before retreating into her cabin.

At around 4:00 in the morning, the train enters the Seikan Tunnel on its route to Sapporo. Conan is still awake and in deep thought about where he experienced a similar case. Fragments of literary stanzas begin to flash in his mind just as they correspond to events happening in real time. In the lounge carriage, a masked man greatly resembling Yasuji appears behind Keitaro with a gun. He shoots the man dead and escapes down the corridor, passing Kogoro's cabin. Kogoro and Conan witness Yasuji firing several rounds into his cabin before turning to fire at them. Believing Yasuji had shot the train window in an attempt to escape, Kogoro pursues him. However, by the time the detective pulls open his door, nothing remains in his cabin besides a shattered window. Conan, however, stands at the entrance in shock after realising that the events illustrated in his father's unfinished novel are playing out before his eyes.

<br>

#### File 215 - Give Yourself Away / Search The Train!?
The train is halted, and the police arrive at the lobby car. He hears the witness testimonies, including Kogoro's claim about the culprit escaping through the shattered window.

Meanwhile, Conan tries to dial his father and ask for further details about his unreleased novel. Fumiyo Akechi then approaches the phone booth and places her hand on the glass. Before Conan can get a connection, Ran arrives and returns him to the lobby car.

The detective voices his scepticism about Kogoro's narrative, pointing out that he did not witness the culprit physically jump out of the moving train. He also finds it unusual that a murderer would leave his loaded gun behind during his escape. Conan agrees with the detective's deduction; however, the strangest thing for him is the incident happened exactly like in his father's novel, although the father said he lost it in an accident. The police begin searching various cabins in the train since there's a possibility that the culprit has escaped to a different train car.

When the police knock on one of the cabins, Keitaro's wife, Azusa, shouts at them for interrupting her at such a late night. The police officer introduces himself as Nishimura from the Hokkaido Police and briefly tells her the reason for the interruption. Azusa is shocked when hearing that her husband was shot. The police find a shotgun in Azusa's cabin for a clay pigeon shooting. On the second floor, they find fishing gear in the Toshinori's cabin. Mr Nishimura moves to the cabin besides Azusa's. He explodes on the occupant, Toru, after Toru failed to respond in a timely manner, as he had been listening to music with his headphones. He finds broken parts of a shinai (sword) on the bed, which Toru says that he was fixing for his kendo match tomorrow. Mr Nishimura then pushes past an intoxicated Akishige and finds a bag of golf clubs in his cabin. 

A pair of passengers then alerted Mr Nishimura about Fumiyo who was periodically observing Keitaro in the lobby car shortly before he was shot. The woman in question then appears and explains that she was only glancing Keitaro because of the smell of his cigar. The police then inspect Fumiyo's cabin and find that she only carries her handbag. The woman explains that she is travelling to Hokkaido for equestrianism, which seems to spark a realisation in Conan. He asks Fumiyo about the various attire needed to ride horses and whether she cleans up after herself. She responds that she does, replacing the equipment once it grows old. Suddenly, an officer then rushes down the corridor proclaiming to have discovered the culprit's corpse in the tunnel.

They identify Yasuji's body outside of a tunnel, seemingly having perished upon striking the tracks. Mr Nishimura notices that the man is wearing a wig and removes the piece. They are surprised to see that the victim is the infamous thief who attempted to rob Keitaro's jewellery store not long ago. Mr Nishimura mentions that Yasuji used to work in a group of three but temporarily suspended his activity after one of his partners became a drug addict. With the killer found, Mr Nishimura allows the train to continue its journey to Sapporo.

With the train now moving, Fumiyo attempts to call someone from her cabin but fails to receive a response. She hears a knock on her door and finds Conan on the other side. He accuses her of lying about her practising equestrian, citing that he was talking about cleaning out horse dung and not a piece of equipment.

#### File 216 - To be Continued...
Fumiyo reveals herself to be Yukiko and says she boarded the train after seeing the news about the botched robbery. After noticing a striking similarity to Yusaku's lost story, the couple called the jewellery store and found out that Keitaro was planning to take the luxury train in six days time. They tried to talk to the owner to cancel his trip, but he wouldn't listen. So, they bought two train tickets to stop the murder. Yukiko expresses her regret about leaving for the bathroom at the time of the murder, asking Conan if he thinks she is responsible for the man's death. Conan tries not to upset his mother by changing the topic and asks where the father is. Yukiko replies that he went on ahead to Sapporo on another train. Since they didn't know which train the jewellery owner would be riding, both of them decided to take different trains. Conan learns that the murder scheme works better at night and asks about the culprit's trick but is dumbfounded upon hearing that Yusaku himself had forgotten about its details. Yukiko explains that the unfinished manuscripts were stolen when Yusaku's editor was caught in a bank robbery, and Yukiko recalls that the robbery was orchestrated by Yasuji Asama. Ran then finds Conan in Fumiyo's cabin and tells him that Kogoro is about to start his deduction show.

In the lobby car, Kogoro proceeds to outline his explanation for Yasuji's motivations. The detective reasons that Keitaro had made a secret agreement with Yasuji where the thief would break into the store, resulting in Keitaro collecting the vast amounts of insurance money. They then planned for Yasuji to return the jewellery to Keitaro in exchange for a portion of the insurance money. However, when Yasuji broke into the shop, Keitaro set off the store's alarm in an attempt to capture the criminal. In doing so, Keitaro would raise his popularity for the upcoming election. Keitaro would later receive a call from Yasuji about conducting another exchange aboard the express train, which would explain why Keitaro suddenly altered his travel plans from plane to train.

An officer wonders how Keitaro found himself in contact with a notorious criminal. Toru mentions that there was news in the paper reporting Keitaro's involvement in drug dealing. Conan agrees with Kogoro's assessment that the case had been a standard sequence of events. But with such a strong resemblance to his father's work, he feels as though something is missing. Conan asks one of the officers about the robbery gang, and the officer says that the gang has three members, but one of them, who was a woman, had died from a drug overdose. Conan believes the third member who could've read his father's novel is still on the train. He comes out with a possibility that someone killed Yasuji and threw his body out of the train before he shot Keitaro. His theories are quickly disproven when an officer informs Mr Nishimura that based on police analysis, Yasuji fell from the train at 4:10, lining up perfectly with the witness testimonies. Another officer then states they have not found any shards of glass near the site of the body and that there was a piece of crumbled-up duct tape in Yasuji's belt loop.

Hearing the news, Conan has a realisation and rushes to Yasuji's cabin to search for an object that works better at night. Upon finding it, he internally remarks that he has solved his father's trick. Yukiko soon arrives and expresses her joy in finally getting to see her husband's bruised ego. However, Conan begins to remember more lines from the lost manuscript describing a bespectacled wannabe detective who celebrated his victory too soon. Conan asks his mother about the character in the story, to which she distinctly remembers that the snotty wannabe detective was murdered by the culprit. Believing the story to be actively describing himself, Conan begins to have doubts about both his deduction and personal safety.

#### File 217 - The Terminal Station / The Last Station
Yukiko assures her son of his safety, stating that the character from the novel met their end because he impulsively confronted the culprit without evidence. Conan reveals that he too lacks evidence. He says that with the investigation focusing on Yasuji as the one and only culprit, it is difficult to justify an additional search of the passenger's belongings. Yukiko then suggests they lure the culprit out.

Returning to her Fumiyo disguise, Yukiko tells Mr Nishimura and the gathered passengers that she saw something "long." She claims to have forgotten what it was, but she will remember in due time, promising to inform the police when she does.

The train arrives at Sapporo Station, where Conan and Yukiko are set to execute their plan. With the culprit successfully taunted, Yukiko intends to walk around the platform until she is approached. If all goes as planned, Conan will use the stun gun and aim the tranquiliser dart on the culprit and turn them in to the authorities. Yukiko plants a kiss on her son's cheek and heads off. Conan escapes Ran's supervision by pretending he forgot something on the train and runs off to follow Yukiko's movements. Noticing his mother is attempting to leave the station, he runs over to her only to discover that he has been following a different woman wearing the same outfit. Realising he has lost his mother in the crowd, Conan desperately runs around and spots her on the edge of the opposite platform. He notices the culprit standing behind her and frantically tries to warn her. His attempt is cut short by a stopping train, leaving Yukiko defenceless against the rapidly approaching danger.

Yusaku appears and stops Toshinori Kaetsu from pushing his wife into an oncoming train in the nick of time. Toshinori exclaims that the culprit is Yasuji, who jumped out of the train shortly after shooting Keitaro. Yusaku deduces that the man who shot Keitaro was Toshinori wearing a disguise to look like Yasuji. 

Toshinori had beaten Yasuji to death and broke the window sometime earlier in the train's journey. Using the cover of darkness, he passed a fishing line through Yasuji's belt loop and hung his body on the exterior of the train. The fishing line was then passed from Yasuji's cabin and attached to the Toshinori's cabin door. After shooting Keitaro, Toshinori fired bullets into Yasuji's cabin to make it look as though he just destroyed the window. He then shot several rounds towards his pursuers and used the time they were distracted to slip into the staircase beyond the cabin entrance. After doing so, he cut the fishing line attached to Yasuji's body, causing it to fall onto the tracks at the same time Conan and Kogoro discovered the broken window. Toshinori had to do it at night while the train was in the tunnel so that no one would notice the corpse hanging from the train. The darkness made it easier for him to hide and harder for anyone to spot the string. 

Toshinori reminds Yusaku that Yasuji's cabin door shut shortly before he escaped out the window, something that would not be possible for him if he had supposedly escaped down a different corridor. Yusaku explains that Toshinori linked an additional fishing line from the line attached to Yasuji's body to the sliding door with plastic tape. Once the original fishing line was cut, Yasuji's body weight would pull on the entire contraption, slamming the sliding door shut and ripping the taped lines off of their surfaces and out of the train. The plastic tape left on Yasuji's belt loop is proof the fishing lines had detached from both the body and the train's components. Toshinori points out that the outlined trick could have been used by anyone. Conan then finds Yasuji's disguise he used when he shot Keitaro stuffed in Toshinori's luggage.

Yusaku deduces that Toshinori wanted to avenge his partner who died due to drug overdose. Specifically, he wanted revenge against Keitaro, who had sold her the drugs, and Yasuji, who had encouraged her addiction. Toshinori initially took a job at the jewellery store to plan a robbery, but after his partner's death, he decided to remain as an employee at the store. After noticing that Yusaku's unfinished novel matched his current predicament to an uncanny degree, Toshinori used it to set up a double homicide. He began by telling Yasuji about a plan to fake a theft at Keitaro's store, a plan he did not inform Keitaro about. When the oblivious Keitaro spotted Yasuji in the act of theft, he earnestly rang the alarm bell, causing the thief to say, "It's not like I was told. " Toshinori would later manipulate both Yasuji and Keitaro into boarding the express train via tips and blackmail to execute his murderous plan.

Yusaku then reveals himself as the author of the unfinished novel, greatly shocking Toshinori. The author suggests the man turn himself in, as that is how he intended for the novel to end, although he does add that the culprit was to be killed in a traffic accident on his way to the police station.

However, Yusaku reveals that Toshinori didn't know the real plan, and his real trick would dispose of the killer's disguise. Yukiko praises her husband's romantic act, who rushed to rescue her just a moment before being attacked. ### Case 063 - Sonoko Attempted Murder Case 
#### File 218 - Go On, Sonoko / Go, Serena
Sonoko snaps an embarrassing photograph of Ran in her bathing suit on one of the beaches in Izu. Sonoko teases Ran about showing the photograph to Shinichi. Ran notices that her friend has been mean to her throughout the trip and is worried that she has done something that caused the friend to feel upset about her. Sonoko says that her plan this time was to avoid lodging at her expensive vacation home and to book a stay at a cheap inn to decrease the pressure guys may feel around her. However, her hopes were dashed after all the men attracted to Ran. Suddenly, a handsome young man named Tadahiko Michiwaki approaches the two girls. Sonoko assumes that he is after Ran but is greatly surprised after he invites her to have a meal with him. Ran encourages Sonoko to go with Tadahiko, while she and Conan will stay at the beach. 

Sonoko is unable to do it alone but asks Ran and Conan to accompany her. The group converses over food at a beachside food court. Tadahiko says he is a student at Beika University and that he came to Izu to emotionally recover from being dumped by his ex-girlfriend. The man's flirtations are briefly interrupted by the rude service of the foodcourt waiter, Makoto Kyogoku. Ran seems to recognise the waiter, leading Sonoko to explain that he is the son of the owner of the inn that they are staying at. When asked how she knows, Sonoko replies that Makoto was glancing at them in the lobby, prompting her to ask one of the workers about his identity. Tadahiko realises that the girls are staying at the inn near his hotel and invites them to dinner. He tells Sonoko to bring her camera, mentioning a death that had occurred near the venue one year ago. A brown-haired girl was discovered on the beach with her stomach being stabbed repeatedly, and the culprit still has not been apprehended. There are rumours that the murdered woman liked the restaurant, leaving a gruesome poltergeist to stain any photographs taken within the establishment. As the girls bicker over whether to accept Tadahiko's offer, Makoto rudely slams their food onto the table before telling them to eat quickly. Another man named Shoji Terabayashi is looking at their table while drinking his beer quietly. Suddenly, several customers within the food court begin spreading news of another brown-haired woman having been murdered in the forest by the train tracks.

The group spots Inspector Sango Yokomizo analysing the crime scene, the woman's death appearing identical to the body found last year. Sonoko voices her concern about her safety, pointing to her brown hair. Tadahiko assures her that he will protect her throughout her stay in Izu. Conan notices Shoji seemingly having followed them from the foodcourt. Tadahiko says he will pick them up around 7 o'clock in front of the inn to go for dinner.

Later that day, Sonoko, Ran, and Conan wait for Tadahiko at the inn's entrance. Rain begins to fall. Makoto suddenly appears from the entrance and silently drops an umbrella off before sliding back indoors. Sonoko shrugs off Conan's  theory about Makoto interested in her and runs back inside to retrieve her forgotten wallet. Ran tells Conan that Makoto looks familiar, but she can't remember where she had seen him. Upon entering her room, Sonoko sees a silhouette crouched on the floor. The terrified girl tries to make her escape but is caught and dragged back inside. She is pinned to the floor and watches in horror as the silhouette raises his knife.

<br>

#### File 219 - The Sleeping Beauty / Sleeping Beauty 
Before the assailant can strike Sonoko with their knife, they hear Ran in the hallway calling for her friend. She opens the door to find Sonoko recovering from her attack, free of harm. She says her attacker fled through the window into the rain, prompting Conan to rush to the edge only to find no signs of the culprit. Sonoko describes seeing a man rummaging through their belongings when she opened the door. When asked how she knows her attacker was a man, she explains that she bit down on a hairy arm during the struggle. 

Tadahiko then enters their room, visibly covered in rainwater and with mud-covered pants. He claims that his car broke down and he ran to the inn. He couldn't find them outside, so he asked one of the staff. Conan asks for him to remove his shirt. The man obliges the request, showing no signs of a bite mark nor any arm hair. Makoto enters the room next, sporting a bandage on his right arm. Ran asks about his injury, but Sonoko says he is likely not the culprit, as his arms are not hairy. Makoto thinks the attack is a case of attempted burglary, blaming Sonoko for wearing an alluring crop top that doesn't look good on her. He collects the girls' belongings and moves them to another room before suggesting that it's safer for them to take their dinner in the inn's restaurant.

Tasting the seafood cuisine at the restaurant downstairs, the girls positively comment on its quality. Makoto promises to compensate for their unfortunate experience with food free of charge as long as they don't spread the rumours about the thief. Sonoko then mentions that the underwear thief pulled a knife on her. 

Conan begins to wonder about the culprit's motivations, linking their burglar with the at-large murderer who is targeting brown-haired girls. He asks if Sonoko still has her camera, believing the culprit was after her films. She says the film wasn't there because she already dropped it off to get developed into photos. Sonoko pulls out a stack of photos and shares them with Tadahiko and Conan. Most of them consist of intrusive photographs of other couples committing public displays of affection. With no apparent photographs that would incriminate the murderer, Conan recalls their conversation at the food court. He wonders whether the culprit overheard Sonoko mentioning Shinichi as a detective, fearing what would happen if her photos landed in the hands of a detective. Tadahiko then invites the girls to the supposedly haunted restaurant for lunch tomorrow, to which they excitedly agree. Conan also spots Shoji once again at the inn's restaurant.

The next day, the four of them go to the scenic cliffside restaurant. Sonoko chooses to take a nap in the car, having failed to get proper rest after the incident the night before. Tadahiko leaves the car AC on and locks the door before leaving with Ran and Conan. A shadow appears in front of the car without their knowledge.

Inside the restaurant, the group notices Makoto entering through the front door and taking a seat at a table next to them. Ran asks him whether he's a frequent customer, and he replies that it is his first time visiting. Makoto asks where Ran's other friend is. Conan then notices Tadahiko's car slowly rolling down the hill towards an impending cliff.

Ran and Conan pursue the neutral vehicle and pound on the window in an attempt to wake Sonoko up. Conan sees a lock-picking tool fall from the car and onto the road. With little space left on the road, Ran gains momentum by flipping herself on top of the car and uses her leg to smash the window. She drags Sonoko out of the car right as it rolls through the guard rail and explodes on the rocks below. As Tadahiko and Makoto rush to her aid, Conan states that someone used the lockpick to force the door open to lower the handbrake. He deduces that someone is definitely trying to kill Sonoko.

Inspector Sango Yokomizo arrives at the cliff's edge and assesses the situation. Someone broke into the car using a lockpick with great skill, and the police find a puddle of water that must have leaked out of the AC. 

Meanwhile, Conan internally begins to narrow his list of suspects. He notes that Makoto could not be the culprit, as he had entered the restaurant just before the car had begun to roll. Additionally, Tadahiko is innocent, as he has remained at the table throughout the duration of the meal. Sango then offers Sonoko a ride to the police station, but having just experienced a harrowing incident involving a vehicle, Sonoko expresses her hesitancy. Tadahiko offers to walk her to the police station, which the girl accepts despite Sango's warnings.

The group discusses their predicament during the walk, mentioning that their belongings, including Sonoko's camera and stack of photographs, were lost after the incident. Sonoko assures Ran she still possesses the photo she intends to show Shinichi, having kept it on her for safety. Believing him to be dangerous, Tadahiko grabs Sonoko by the arm and leads her off-road and into the forest.

The group splits in two to evade their pursuer. Ran and Conan sneak around the exhausted man and prepare to launch a counterattack. The man explains that he is a detective from the Chiba Prefectural Police following a suspect. After noticing Shoji wearing sandals without socks and a confirmation from Ran that she doesn't wear socks with sandals, Conan runs after Sonoko.

### Personal Thoughts
I like the appearance of Yusaku and Yukiko. Yusaku is portrayed as a calm and high-intellectual mystery author. His wife and son always treat him as the main rival for solving cases.
